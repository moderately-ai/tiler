# Derive runtime route, completion, and cache conformance obligations

## Goal

Produce owner-derived, fail-loud obligation manifests for runtime route commitment and fallback, adapter completion, and expansion-cache identity, validation, crash/race, and publication properties.

## Authority

Accepted runtime, cache, artifact ABI, and publication contracts plus exact owning source outrank this ticket. Re-audit every Fact at the implementation base.

## Work

- Read complete route, fallback, commitment, completion, cache-key, hit-validation, publication, crash/race, refusal, and consumer paths.
- Preserve the exact seven route failures and five top-level cache properties while deriving the submerged populations.
- Determine whether adapter-associated completion types have a singular owner identity; mark the family unknown rather than defaulting if not.
- Define owner-local subject identities and revision rules without inventing a public boundary.
- Design independent subject perturbations for route commitment, completion, cache identity, validation, publication, and crash/race properties.

## Non-goals

No adapter API implementation, fallback-policy change, cache protocol redesign, artifact schema/domain step, performance qualification, or goal profile.

## Stop conditions

Stop and file a decision packet when completion lacks a singular owner, when route and adapter authorities conflict, or when enumeration requires a consequential public boundary.

## Acceptance

- Runtime route/fallback/completion and cache/publication families are exact or explicitly unknown.
- Every complete census is owner-derived and fails on a subject perturbation.
- Identity, revision, construction, consumption, refusal, and unsupported-population consequences are explicit.
- Unknowns have bounded descendants rather than silent zeroes.
