## User-visible outcome

`sum(x)` — a strict serial reduction whose operand is a declared program input, with no elementwise prologue at all — compiles, instead of refusing at the request boundary under `reduction-prologue`.

## Why this exists

**Fact — the wall is in the schedule IR, not the recognizer.** `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/builder.rs`'s `verify_access_and_semantics` admits a `ScalarProgram::StrictSerialSum` region only when `read.tensor == TensorRole::Intermediate`; the `FusedMultiplyAddSerialSum` and `SquaredSerialSum` arms beside it admit `FIRST_INPUT` because their prologues read the original input. A region folding a declared input directly under a plain `StrictSerialSum` is therefore rejected by the intrinsic verifier as malformed compiler output.

**Fact — the general recognizer reaches it and refuses.** `admit-a-general-program-shape-recognizer-at-the-compiler-request-boundary` generalized the elementwise prologue to the whole `PointwiseF32Expression` vocabulary, and `sum(x)` is the one recognized shape it then had to refuse *at the boundary* rather than admit and fail mid-pipeline. `recognize_reduction` states the rule and names this ticket; `a_reduction_over_a_declared_input_refuses_under_the_prologue_rule` in `crates/tiler-compiler/tests/composed_family_recognition.rs` drives it against an accepted neighbour.

**Inference — synthesizing an identity prologue is not the fix.** Staging a copy of a tensor the fold could read directly adds an observable materialization boundary, and its rounding, that the caller's program never asked for.

## Boundaries

- The schedule-IR arm is the change, and it is a *widening of an admitted access*, not a relaxation: a `StrictSerialSum` region reading `FIRST_INPUT` must still prove its contributor relation, bounds, and ownership exactly as the intermediate-reading one does.
- The split and single-workgroup-tree strategies read the same contributor tensor. Either they widen with it, or the frontier declines them for a prologue-less fold with a stated reason — silently losing an alternative is not acceptable.
- The program assembly must bind one buffer per declared input with no temporary, which `build_fused_core` already does for the single-region shapes.

## Closes when

`sum(x)` compiles through `tiler_compiler::session` to an emitted region under every registered contract; the `reduction-prologue` refusal is removed together with the boundary that raised it; and the schedule verifier still refuses a `StrictSerialSum` region whose contributor access does not match its declared reduction, observed failing.

## Outcome

**Fact — the wall moved where the ticket said it was, and the widening is one typed distinction rather than a relaxed comparison.** `ScalarProgram::StrictSerialSum` carries no prologue, so it says how contributors combine and nothing about where they live: `sum(x)` over a declared input and the same fold over a materialized prologue's result are one scalar program over two tensors. `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/builder.rs` now names that with `ContributorTensor::{Exactly, DeclaredDomain}` — `DeclaredDomain` admits `TensorRole::Input { ordinal: 0 }` or `TensorRole::Intermediate` and nothing else, and every other family keeps an exact role. Each admitted region still proves its contributor relation, its bounds, and its ownership by the same authorities; only which boundary tensor the read may bind moved.

**Fact — the verifier-arm inventory, for `admit-a-materialized-intermediate-read-in-the-scheduled-region-vocabulary`.** Touched: the serial `StrictSerialSum` arm of `verify_access_and_semantics` (its `read.tensor == TensorRole::Intermediate` became `ContributorTensor::DeclaredDomain.admits(read.tensor)`); `SplitFamily::read_tensor`'s type; `multi_pass_family`'s `StrictSerialSum` arm (partial pass `DeclaredDomain`, **final pass still exactly `Intermediate`** — it folds partials its own partial pass staged); `cooperative_family`'s `StrictSerialSum` arm (`DeclaredDomain`, no pass distinction, because a tile stages in workgroup memory rather than a boundary tensor); and the two `read.tensor != family.read_tensor` comparisons in `verify_multi_pass_semantics` and `verify_cooperative_semantics`. **At this ticket's landing, deliberately left untouched**: every *write* rule then still required `write.tensor == TensorRole::Output` on the serial arms, and `verify_pointwise_region` still required its then-stated Input-ordinal and Intermediate-write constraints — neither of which this branch touched; the Intermediate-read successor was expected to land in the pointwise helpers (later named `reads_bind_boundary_tensors_in_order`, not `ordinals_bind_in_order`) and in `pointwise_read_map_is_admissible`, citing this landing for the `ContributorTensor` vocabulary rather than re-deriving one. **Correction — 2026-08-10.** Serial *write* rules now carry `CommittedTensor::CoverAssigned`, which admits `TensorRole::Intermediate | TensorRole::Output` (`admit-a-strict-serial-fold-that-writes-a-materialized-intermediate`, status done). `verify_pointwise_region` admits Intermediate reads under `reads_bind_boundary_tensors_in_order` (Input ordinals non-decreasing by declaration, not forced equal to access index) and admits Intermediate | Output writes; `admit-a-materialized-intermediate-read-in-the-scheduled-region-vocabulary` is done and rewrote that contract.

**Fact — the compiler side is one derivation, not four.** `physical::contributor_tensor` answers the boundary tensor from `NormalizedSerialSum::contributor_input` (the same fact as prologue presence: `contributor_input` is `Some` iff `prologue` is `None`), and `reduction_region`, `partial_reduction_region`, and `single_workgroup_tree_region` all read it; `final_reduction_region` deliberately does not. `subject_contributor_tensor` is its subject-side twin, and `reduction_access_matches` gained the tensor as an explicit argument so each binding arm states which tensor it requires — the fused arm passes `FIRST_INPUT` because its scalar program contains the prologue. **Both parallel strategies widened rather than declining**, so the ticket's second boundary is met by offering, not by a stated decline.

**Fact — the recognizer records absence rather than synthesizing an identity.** `NormalizedSerialSum::prologue` and its subject projection became `Option<PointwiseF32Expression>`, and `recognize_reduction` still runs the contributor walk for a prologue-less fold — that is what discharges the walk's own obligations under the same rules — but records `None` when the fold's operand is a declared input. The alternative the ticket rejected is now unspellable rather than merely unchosen: `pointwise_region` panics if asked for a region a prologue-less fold has no expression for, and `spell_region` resolves the prologue part through `prologue_members()` so an empty member set cannot match it.

**Fact — two authorities below the boundary had premises this shape falsified, and both were corrections rather than relaxations.** `pipeline::verify::verify_equivalence`'s no-proof arm excluded every reduced request on the reasoning that a reduction's only whole-program region is the fused one; the condition is now the *prologue*, because `sum(x)`'s whole-program region merges nothing and `fused_prologue_constants` answers `None` for it. `verify_whole_program_schedule_coverage` required exactly one scheduled region, which was equivalent only while every whole-program cover region was one kernel; a two-dispatch split of a one-occurrence cover is classified `Fused` by `ProgramAlternativeKind::of` (a cover property, as its doc states), so the check is now stated as what it always meant — the dispatches' claims partition the candidate's occurrences, one comparison with no arm that cannot be driven.

**Measurement — `sum(x)` compiles under every registered contract, and its result is the reference evaluator's bit for bit.** `crates/tiler-compiler/tests/composed_family_recognition.rs`'s `a_reduction_over_a_declared_input_compiles_through_the_session` drives all five contracts beside the prologue-carrying neighbour; `pipeline::tests::a_reduction_over_a_declared_input_matches_the_reference_evaluator` compiles `sum(x)` at `[2, 2]`, asserts the single region binds `TensorRole::Input { ordinal: 0 }` and the cover carries no materialization edge, and bit-compares against `ReferenceEvaluator::standard()`. `crates/tiler-macros`'s bare-reduction case moved from the refusal population to the compiling one, leaving `a reduction of a reduction` as the only shape that module's ticket still names.

**Measurement — every new admitting and refusing path was watched failing.** In `tiler-ir`, narrowing `DeclaredDomain` back to `Intermediate` fails all four new admissions (`a_bare_serial_sum_folds_a_declared_input_or_a_materialized_domain`, `only_the_partial_pass_of_a_split_may_fold_a_declared_input`, `a_cooperative_tile_may_fold_a_declared_input`, `an_extrema_partial_pass_respelled_as_a_sum_verifies_as_that_sum`), and widening both `ContributorTensor` arms to admit everything fails all three refusals (the second-input reads and the split's final pass). In `tiler-compiler`, pinning `contributor_tensor` to `Intermediate` fails 3 tests and pinning it to `FIRST_INPUT` fails **194**; re-narrowing `verify_whole_program_schedule_coverage` to one region and re-narrowing the equivalence arm to `try_serial_sum().is_none()` each fail the session test. The `a_bare_fold_over_an_input_still_proves_its_contributor_access` case perturbs the fold's *own* declaration rather than its access, because the bounds proof refines the access and would otherwise report one authority earlier.
