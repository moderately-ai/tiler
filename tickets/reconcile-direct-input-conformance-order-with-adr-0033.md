## User-visible outcome

Four governing records agree on how the selected direct encoded input is validated: checker capability and every selectable alternative's exact enforcement plan are established before `RoutingCommit`; the selected input's unresolved logical conformance begins after that one-way commit and before the selected alternative's first real consumer or any result work. The fused alternative protects its fused contraction access, while the retained materializing alternative protects its `Dequantize`/materialization stage. Hard feasibility is decided per alternative before their costs are compared, and semantic invalidity is terminal rather than a fallback signal.

This is a contract/research reconciliation only. It does not add a public boundary, decide an identity or schema step in advance, implement enforcement, or create a producer that the selected workload does not have.

## Source-first Fact audit — 2026-08-08

- **Verified — ADR 0033's ordering authority.** [ADR 0033](../docs/decisions/0033-semantic-validation-enforcement.md) says that `RoutingCommit` fixes the prepared variant and fallback, `EnforcementCommit` begins unresolved validation, host scans precede result work, and semantic failure never authorizes another mapping. Its source anchors are `Execution has three explicit boundaries` and `Host scans precede result work`.
- **False — artifact-ABI timing.** At dispatched base `812a45e37366dfb543f6a572600c0b3f1eb37de4`, [Artifact ABI](../docs/artifact-abi.md) had a `Logical value conformance` table row beginning `whether a bound value implements the complete resolved type` that assigned the subject to the pre-routing phase, and the paragraph below said a bound scale `belongs before routing`. That contradicted the adopted post-commit route now assigned to this direct input. Preserve the distinct pre-routing obligations — logical-view reconstruction, checker support/preparation, observability/coherence feasibility, physical carrier canonicality, and hard limits — while moving the unresolved logical content check to `EnforcementCommit`.
- **False — artifact-ABI content work.** That same `Logical value conformance` row says conformance includes `the inclusive code domain`. The current [`check_bound_value`](../crates/tiler-ir/src/semantic/conformance.rs) calls `scan_logical_elements` unconditionally after its structural and budget checks, and `scan_component` reads every logical element, including every U8 code and zero point. The `LogicalScalar` arm `UnsignedCode(u8)` does make an out-of-range U8 impossible *after* a trustworthy logical scalar has been constructed, but the present checker does not proof-elide those reads. Record current scanning as Fact. A future implementation may derive proof-elision only after the physical representation owner has established the U8 carrier and element width and the view constructor has established that each read is the declared scalar kind; classify that route as Inference or Proposal, not implemented support. Retain content inspection for positive-normal F32 scales and retain extent, structure, provenance, view, and coherence checks.
- **Imprecise — numerical-semantics scan claim.** [Numerical semantics](../docs/numerical-semantics.md) says `a bound encoded value's conformance is derived from its type` and that `the scan reaches logical elements only`. Both accurately describe the current authority and the boundary of what it observes, but the surrounding reconciliation must not turn the second into a claim that some current obligations do not read content: `scan_component` presently visits every logical element of every component. Preserve that Fact and its exact-subject evidence and deterministic diagnostic rules. Separately label future full-domain U8 proof-elision as an Inference or Proposal contingent on trusted representation and logical-view construction.
- **Imprecise — runtime record's subject and prerequisites.** At the dispatched base, [Runtime execution contract](../docs/research/runtime/runtime-execution-contract.md) correctly said ``Governed strict-affine `Quantize` now produces typed residual obligations``; that general implementation Fact survives. What was stale was the next sentence's assignment of the *first complete runtime vertical* after its `producer, physical-candidate, carrier` prerequisites. The selected program contains no `Quantize` or `Assemble`; replace only that stale first-vertical chain with the delivered direct-binding conformance authority and the selected per-alternative first-consumer plans.
- **False — selected-profile timing and graph.** At the dispatched base, [The first quantized LM profile](../docs/research/numerics/first-quantized-lm-profile.md) said `Before routing commit: every scale positive` and named the obsolete transitive chain through `admit-strict-affine-quantize-physical-candidate`. Replace both with pre-routing support/preparation plus post-commit conformance, and with the direct-input chain. Preserve its source fact that the graph is `input(compound weight) → DequantizeStrictAffine → Contraction` and its retained, separately costed materializing alternative.
- **False — current source does not expose all proposed U8 negatives at one boundary.** `PresentedComponent` says `The *presented* role, type, and shape are what the binding claims` and carries only those three facts; it carries no physical carrier or element width. `check_presented_structure` therefore makes a presented resolved-type mismatch reachable, while `check_scalar` makes a logical scalar-kind mismatch reachable. `LogicalViewFault::UnrepresentableScalar` makes dishonest element-width or carrier interpretation reachable only through the representation owner's and view constructor's work before a `LogicalScalar` is returned. The exact `LogicalScalar` arm `UnsignedCode(u8)` then makes an out-of-range U8 impossible at the checked logical-scalar boundary. Future tests must perturb carrier and width where the physical representation and view are constructed, and perturb presented type and returned scalar kind at the conformance boundary; no invented `PresentedComponent` fields may collapse those owners.

## Required reconciliation

- Reconcile exactly `docs/artifact-abi.md`, `docs/numerical-semantics.md`, `docs/research/runtime/runtime-execution-contract.md`, and `docs/research/numerics/first-quantized-lm-profile.md` with the accepted ADR and current source. Quote new searchable anchors from source bytes and verify each one against its named file.
- Describe two plan records over the same direct `ValueConformanceSubject`, one per retained physical alternative. The fused plan binds the fused contraction stage that first reads decoded weight elements. The materializing plan binds the `Dequantize`/materialization stage that first reads the encoded components and produces the F32 temporary. No route-global witness may authorize whichever stage happens to run.
- Preserve separate feasibility and costing. An alternative without a complete checker, authoritative logical view, observability/coherence route, hard-limit disposition, and exact protected first consumer is infeasible before cost. Costs remain separate per alternative; `Unknown` is never zero and an infeasible alternative is never selected merely because its enforcement cost was omitted.
- Preserve the one-way lifecycle: preparation and support checks occur before `RoutingCommit`; unresolved content enforcement begins after it; failure is terminal with no result allocation, publication, dependent effect, or fallback; success authorizes only the selected alternative's exact first consumer.
- Separate proof-elided obligations from content scans. For selected U8 codes and zero points, fault-inject a mismatched physical carrier, presented resolved type, element width, or scalar kind. Scan positive-normal F32 scale content. Continue checking component count/order/roles/types/shapes/maps, selected per-axis extents, origin/provenance, route/view, version/coherence, validator revision, and evidence-to-consumer binding.
- Remove the obsolete `Quantize` candidate and internal-grouping chain without inventing an activation-quantization, requantization, or other internal producer. Do not reopen the deferred grouping ticket absent its named trigger.
- Do not pre-require an identity or schema step. State the fields the future implementation must derive, then let its final producer-filled grammar determine whether a version changes.

## Reconciliation result — 2026-08-08

The Fact repairs above did not change the ticket's purpose. The four records now use source-byte anchors `Unresolved logical value conformance`, `the implemented scan currently visits every logical element`, `the selected direct input has one enforcement plan`, and `retain two exact enforcement plans`, respectively. Together they place support and preparation before `RoutingCommit`, selected unresolved enforcement at `EnforcementCommit`, the selected exact first consumer after success, and `PublicationCommit` last. They preserve separate fused and materializing feasibility and costs, state current all-element scanning as Fact, and label U8 proof-elision as future Inference contingent on physical carrier/width and logical-view construction.

The artifact contract also records the facts a future producer must derive and explicitly defers both public shape and version consequences until a producer-filled grammar exists. No `Quantize`/`Assemble` producer, device scan, public API, identity step, or schema step was introduced.

The full-section reread also corrected the profile's stale `packed-fused` cost-candidate label: the live alternative is the fused decode over unpacked U8, beside the explicit materializing `Dequantize` alternative and the F32 candidate. The separate-cost semantic claim is unchanged.

## Closes when

All four named records state the same direct-input, post-`RoutingCommit`, per-alternative lifecycle; each exact false or imprecise anchor above has been repaired rather than quoted as live authority; fused and materializing first consumers, hard feasibility, separate costs, proof-elided U8 obligations, reachable negative tests, terminal failure, and unsupported cases are explicit; no public boundary or internal producer is invented; `tkt lint`, `make citations`, `git diff --check`, the exact-base guard, and the applicable documentation gate pass.
