## User-visible outcome

The compiler request boundary recognizes a program declaring several ordered named outputs, producing a normalized subject that names one implementable region partition per output rather than one whole-program strategy reached from a single output.

## Why this exists

**Correction — 2026-08-10.** The Facts and Inference below are the filing-time / pre-landing wall for why this ticket existed. They are **not** live claims about the tree: recognition is per-output (`recognize_program_outputs`), the ordinary boundary admits multi-output programs (parent [`admit-ordered-multi-output-programs-at-the-compiler-request-boundary`](admit-ordered-multi-output-programs-at-the-compiler-request-boundary.md) removed `output-arity`), and the request-subject domain has stepped past this ticket's `v5` step. See **Fact audit — 2026-08-10** at the end.

**~~Fact~~ — historical pre-landing wall: recognition was single-output by construction, and it was the only remaining wall.** `select_supported_strategy` (`crates/tiler-compiler/src/request.rs`) then read `program.outputs().next()`, classified that one occurrence as a serial sum, a contraction, or an elementwise expression, and each recognizer below it required its walk to cover the program *exactly* — `recognize_pointwise` refused under `operation-set` when `recognized.members.len() != program.operation_count()`, and `recognize_reduction` did the same through `check_recognized_operation_cover`. The result was one `NormalizedProgram`: one expression, one output shape, one output key, and one member partition. `crate::physical::spell_region` could spell only a cover region whose members equalled one part of that partition, and `pointwise_region` built its region from the single recognized expression.

**~~Fact~~ — historical at filing: every layer this used to be blamed on had been cleared.** [`implement-general-dag-partitioning`](implement-general-dag-partitioning.md) landed: `crates/tiler-compiler/src/cover.rs` collects the ordered named program outputs a cover must produce and `verify_cover` checks each is produced by exactly one region. [`assemble-a-kernel-program-from-an-arbitrary-cover`](assemble-a-kernel-program-from-an-arbitrary-cover.md) landed: the three fixed plan shapes are gone, replaced by `CoverAssembly::from_plan` over a cover of any region count. [`carry-artifact-program-output-order-into-kernel-program-identity`](carry-artifact-program-output-order-into-kernel-program-identity.md) landed: output order is in kernel-program identity. `tiler-ir` was never the wall — `KernelProgramBuilder::push_output` is bounded by `MAX_PROGRAM_OUTPUTS`, not by one.

**~~Inference~~ — historical at filing: the admissible multi-output set was empty until this landed, so the `output-arity` guard must not move first.** A second declared output either has its producing occurrence inside the first output's recognition walk or it does not. Outside the walk, the walk covers less than the program and `operation-set` refuses. Inside the walk, that value is consumed by another occurrence of the same walk, so its producing region either materializes for a consumer in another region — leaving its one owning write to serve both the materialization edge and the publication — or contains that consumer and must publish two named outputs from one write. A region writes one owning tensor and `ValueRole` is exclusive, so both are refused a layer down.

**Measurement (2026-08-05, at `3adc0689`).** Both branches were observed rather than argued, by relaxing `select_supported_strategy`'s `output_count() != 1` and `verify_artifact_refinements`'s `semantic-output-coverage` arity check together and compiling two fixtures through the ordinary entry point. An independent two-output program (`doubled = x * 2`, `tripled = x * 3`, standard registry) reached `phase: "strategy", rule: "operation-set"`. The reduction-epilogue fixture from `pipeline::conformance` — which publishes `scaled` and reduces it into `reduced` — reached `phase: "program-assembly", rule: "cover-named-output-attribution"`. The perturbation was reverted; nothing of it is retained in the tree.

## Boundaries

- This ticket owns the *recognition* and the normalized subject it produces. It does not relax either `output_count() != 1` guard — [`admit-ordered-multi-output-programs-at-the-compiler-request-boundary`](admit-ordered-multi-output-programs-at-the-compiler-request-boundary.md) owns that, and depends on this.
- The epilogue shape — a program that publishes an intermediate *and* consumes it — needs a copy stage reading `TensorRole::Intermediate` and writing `TensorRole::Output`, which [`admit-elementwise-epilogues-over-a-materialized-intermediate`](admit-elementwise-epilogues-over-a-materialized-intermediate.md) owns. Programs with *independent* outputs do not hit it, and are the reachable target here.
- **`NormalizedProgramSubject` is identity-bearing.** It sits inside `VerifiedRequestSubject`, which every `VerifiedScheduledRegion` and the `ArtifactConstructionPlan` carry. Widening it is an identity-domain step: the version moves at its owning layer, the ledger documents move in the same commit, and every pinned identity is recomputed on the tree the step lands into, with each moved pin enumerated. Do not half-execute it.
- The public boundary of whatever replaces `NormalizedProgram` is Tom's to accept, not a worker's to self-accept.

## Required failure-path evidence

Each observed failing against an accepted neighbour: a program declaring two outputs whose second producing occurrence lies outside the first's walk, once the walk is per-output, must compile rather than refuse under `operation-set`; a program with an occurrence covered by *no* output's walk must still refuse under `operation-set`, so the coverage check is widened rather than removed; two ordered outputs attributed to the same region must refuse rather than publish twice from one write; and two programs differing only in output order must keep distinct identities through the new subject.

## The widening landed, and condition 3 turned out to need the guard it forbids (2026-08-05)

**Fact — recognition is per output.** `select_supported_strategy` keeps its three program-wide checks in their order and then calls `recognize_program_outputs`, which walks *each* declared output in declaration order and returns a `NormalizedProgram` holding one `NormalizedOutput` per output. Each `NormalizedOutput` is the enum the whole-program `NormalizedProgram` used to be, so a partition is still prologue-and-fold for a reduction and one part for the two single-region shapes. `check_output_cover` carries what the per-output recognizers used to check alone: the walks must *partition* the occurrences — every one claimed (`operation-set`) and none claimed twice (`output-partition-overlap`). With one declared output the union is that output's own member set, so the rule is unchanged and the single-output path's behaviour and identities are byte-identical except for the domain step below.

**Fact — the per-region authorities resolve which output owns a region.** `NormalizedProgram::output_for_region` is the lookup, and `crate::physical::spell_region` now returns a `RegionSpelling` carrying the resolved output's declaration position beside its kind. Every region builder (`pointwise_region`, `reduction_region`, `contraction_region`, `fused_region`, `partial_`/`final_reduction_region`, `single_workgroup_tree_region`, `split_reduction_regions`) and `fused_prologue_constants` take that output rather than reading a whole-program accessor; `verify_region_subject_binding` searches the subject's output list and binds against whichever partition the region realizes; `pipeline::region_role` and `planning`'s fused-numerics gate resolve the same way. `VerifiedTargetRequest::sole_output` is the one remaining whole-program derivation, named so its removal is a compile error rather than a silent mis-attribution, and it is what `build_scheduled_regions`, `build_fused_scheduled_region`, and the fixtures use.

**Fact — the identity step was executed, and it moved exactly one pin (this ticket's step only).** `NormalizedProgramSubject` became a length-framed list of `NormalizedOutputSubject`, and the enclosing domain stepped `tiler.compiler.request-subject.v4` → `v5`. Appends-only was considered and does not close: a `v4` subject's first post-identity byte is the arm sub-tag's own length frame and a `v5` subject's is the output count, and nothing rules out a count that frames like a sub-tag length, so the per-tag injectivity argument fails. At this ticket's landing the only pinned identity the request subject reaches was `explain::tests::deterministic_trace_is_sealed_and_rendered_separately`'s trace qualifier, moved `c91fc7c907eed554` → `45467875b9574962` and recorded in that test's own ledger comment in the same commit; every other request-subject assertion in the corpus is relational. **Correction — 2026-08-10.** This ticket's domain step remains `v4` → `v5` for the length-framed multi-output subject. The current tree is at `tiler.compiler.request-subject.v6` (shape-environment identity, later work). The explain pin has moved further and is currently `request=7ba3d77a66f04638` in that same test; the hex pair `c91fc7c907eed554` → `45467875b9574962` is intermediate history for this ticket's step, not the live pin. Reproduce live pin: `rg -n 'request=' crates/tiler-compiler/src/explain.rs`; domain: `rg -n 'request-subject.v' crates/tiler-compiler/src/domains.rs`.

**Measurement — every new refusal was watched failing.** `check_output_cover`'s two arms, disabled together (`if false && …`): `the_output_partition_check_can_say_no_in_both_directions` and `two_outputs_sharing_one_walk_refuse_rather_than_publish_twice` both fail, the first reporting `Ok(())` where `operation-set` was expected. `NormalizedProgram::output_for_region` made unconditional: `a_region_resolves_to_the_output_whose_partition_owns_it` fails, and so do four existing `pipeline::tests` that read `region_role` through it, which is what proves the lookup is load-bearing rather than newly added. Both perturbations were reverted. **Correction — 2026-08-10.** The second named test was renamed to `an_output_key_pair_naming_one_value_still_refuses_by_name` (published-and-consumed neighbour work); the refusal property for two keys naming one value remains under `output-partition-overlap`.

**Inference — the widened `operation-set` arm is unreachable through the ordinary boundary, and that is a property of the strategy set rather than of this change.** Every recognizer's walk is the full transitive closure from its output, and a built program retains only output-reachable operations, so the union always covers. The whole-suite run under the disabled check stayed green apart from the two tests above, which is the evidence: the check had the same unreachability before this ticket, inside `recognize_pointwise` and `check_recognized_operation_cover`. It is widened rather than removed because a strategy whose walk is *not* a full closure — the structural families, or a multi-result occurrence — makes it reachable, and the perturbation above is what proves it can say no meanwhile.

**Consequence — closing condition 3 cannot be discharged under this ticket's own Boundaries section, and the two are in direct contradiction.** Reaching complete-plan selection requires a `VerifiedTargetRequest`, which `verify_request` mints only through `select_supported_strategy`, which refuses `output-arity` first — the guard this ticket's Boundaries section forbids relaxing. Cover enumeration alone *is* reachable without a request and is already discharged by [`implement-general-dag-partitioning`](implement-general-dag-partitioning.md)'s `multi_result_outputs_are_retained_and_a_dropped_one_is_rejected`, so the unproven half is complete-plan selection specifically. A `#[cfg(test)]` bypass was eliminated on maintainability: it would either duplicate `verify_request`'s admission — a second derivation of one authority — or hoist the guard out of the recognizer for a hook [`admit-ordered-multi-output-programs-at-the-compiler-request-boundary`](admit-ordered-multi-output-programs-at-the-compiler-request-boundary.md) deletes in the change that relaxes it. The condition is therefore restated below as that ticket's, where the guard and the evidence land together.

**Fact — one hazard the parent will not hit, checked rather than assumed.** `verify_region_subject_binding` compares `region.index.id` against literal `RegionId`s, so two elementwise outputs both produce regions carrying `RegionId::new(0)`. That is not a collision: `tiler_ir::schedule::model::encode_identity` (`tiler.schedule.v5`) folds the iteration shape, accesses, bounds proofs, ownership proof, scalar program, numerical realization, and schedule, and **not** the region id, and program assembly indexes regions by occurrence rather than by id. The ids are per-output-relative and remain correct.
