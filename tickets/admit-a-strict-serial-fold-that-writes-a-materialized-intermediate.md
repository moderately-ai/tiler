## User-visible outcome

A `ScalarProgram::StrictSerialSum` region under a `ReductionTopology::Serial` may commit its result to `TensorRole::Intermediate` instead of only to `TensorRole::Output`, so `sum(x * x) * scale` has a producer region for the value its epilogue reads.

## Why this exists

**Fact — this is the second of the two walls `materialized_intermediate_epilogue_wall.rs` measured, and the only one still standing.** That file's table records three rows: an elementwise epilogue reading `TensorRole::Intermediate` (admitted by [`admit-a-materialized-intermediate-read-in-the-scheduled-region-vocabulary`](admit-a-materialized-intermediate-read-in-the-scheduled-region-vocabulary.md)), a contraction writing one (already admitted by `verify_contraction`, which accepts `TensorRole::Intermediate | TensorRole::Output` for the owning write), and a serial fold writing one — refused, and pinned by `a_strict_serial_sum_region_cannot_write_a_materialized_intermediate` with a control that differs only in the write's role.

**Fact — the refusing rule, read from the verifier.** `verify_access_and_semantics` in `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/builder.rs` admits the serial `StrictSerialSum` arm only under `write.tensor == TensorRole::Output`. Every other serial arm — `FusedMultiplyAddSerialSum`, `SquaredSerialSum`, `StrictSerialMaximum` — carries the same requirement. `multi_pass_family`'s partial pass is the one fold that writes an intermediate today, and it is a different topology declaring a split rather than a fold whose result another region consumes.

**Inference — the consequence for the epilogue chain.** `contract(a, b) * 2.0` needs only the read, and both of its halves are expressible now. `sum(x * x) * scale` needs this write as well, so `admit-elementwise-epilogues-over-a-materialized-intermediate` can deliver its contraction shape and its published-and-consumed copy stage without this ticket, and not its reduction shape.

## Boundaries

- **A widening of an admitted write, not a relaxation.** A fold committing to an intermediate must prove its ownership, its bounds, and its contributor relation exactly as the output-writing one does; only which boundary tensor receives the committed value moves.
- **Decide the arm set deliberately, and state the decision.** The three prologue-carrying serial families sit beside the bare sum in the same match. Widening only `StrictSerialSum` leaves a reader unable to derive why `SquaredSerialSum` may not stage its result; widening all four is a larger claim than the epilogue chain needs. Either is defensible — the derivation is what must be recorded, in the shape [`admit-a-reduction-over-a-declared-input-tensor`](admit-a-reduction-over-a-declared-input-tensor.md) used for `ContributorTensor`.
- **The multi-pass and cooperative arms already answer this question separately** and must not be folded into the same predicate by accident: their pass distinction is about which tensor holds the *contributors*, not about where the result is committed.
- **Identity.** `push_tensor_role` already writes a distinct self-delimiting tag for `Intermediate`, and the write role already reaches `encode_identity` through both the access list and the ownership proof, so admitting the value is expected to be appends-only under per-tag framing. Determine that at the owning encoding site rather than inheriting this sentence.

## Closes when

A `StrictSerialSum` region under a serial topology whose owning write targets `TensorRole::Intermediate` verifies, with its ownership and bounds proofs discharged exactly as the output-writing control's are; a region that widened the rule too far is observed refusing; and `materialized_intermediate_epilogue_wall.rs`'s serial-sum assertion is inverted in the same change that lifts it, rather than deleted.

## Outcome

**Fact — the rule stated as a family fact something no family decides, and the widening is that misattribution corrected.** `write.tensor == TensorRole::Output` sat inside each fold's arm as though the fold decided it. It does not: a fold's scalar program says how contributors combine, and where the combined value *goes* is a property of the surrounding cover. `sum(x * x)` whose value the caller asked for and the same fold whose value an epilogue scales are one computation committing to two boundary tensors. `CommittedTensor::{Exactly, CoverAssigned}` in `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/builder.rs` now names that — `CoverAssigned` admits `TensorRole::Intermediate` or `TensorRole::Output` and nothing else, `Exactly` keeps a pass whose role in a split fixes its target. Every admitted region still discharges its ownership, bounds, and contributor obligations through the same `verify_proof_records` call in the same order; only which boundary tensor receives the committed value moves.

**Fact — the per-obligation derivation, each traced to its consuming site rather than assumed.** Four obligations were candidates for what `== Output` protected.

- **Result publication — the obligation *moves*, and it was never the region's.** Nothing in `tiler-ir` derives publication from a scheduled region's write role. Publication is a program-layer fact carried by `ValueRole`, and `ValueRole::fills` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/program/model.rs:176`) is where it is enforced, at `KernelProgramBuilder::push_stage`'s `check_stage_accesses` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/program/builder.rs:1141`). `(Temporary, Intermediate)` is `true` and `(Temporary, Output)`, `(Output, Intermediate)` are both `false`, so the pairing stays exclusive: widening the region rule cannot publish a temporary or stage an output. `fills` is unchanged and needed no change.
- **The one-writer rule and race freedom — *carries*, unchanged.** `verify_proof_records` requires `write_proof.tensor == write.tensor` and `region.index.ownership_proof.tensor == write.tensor`, then requires the proof's kind to be `OneGlobalInvocationPerOutput { output_count }` against `owned_output_positions(region)`. None of those reads *which* role; each requires agreement with whatever the write names. An intermediate-writing fold proves total race-free ownership of the intermediate exactly as an output-writing one proves it of the output, which is why the fixtures move the access, its bounds proof, and the ownership proof together.
- **Assembly binding — *carries*, and was already exercised.** `CoverAssembly::from_plan` resolves `Intermediate` to the cover's materialization edge and `Input { ordinal }` against the program's declared interface. It already binds an intermediate-writing region: `verify_contraction`, `verify_pointwise_region`, and `verify_strict_affine_u4_dequantize` all accept `Intermediate | Output` for the owning write, and the split's partial pass writes exactly `Intermediate`. This landing adds no assembly shape.
- **Lowering — *carries*, role-agnostic.** `crates/tiler-ir/src/kernel/lower.rs` propagates `write.tensor` into a `BufferParameter` and classifies nothing on it (`write_tensor` has four occurrences: the `CanonicalPlan` field, its construction at line 263, and two buffer declarations). A serial fold writing `Intermediate` lowers identically to one writing `Output` apart from one tag byte.

One obligation that could genuinely have broken was checked and does not: a serial fold writing `Intermediate` is not confusable with a split's partial pass, because the two differ in `ReductionTopology` — a separately encoded, separately matched field — and the partial pass additionally requires `iteration_shape == partial_shape`, a trailing partition axis the serial arm's `output_shape == iteration_shape` refuses.

**Fact — the arm-set decision, and the ticket's framing of the trade is refuted rather than chosen between.** The Boundaries section offered "widen only `StrictSerialSum`" against "widen all four" and called the second *a larger claim than the epilogue chain needs*. It is not. The read landing's asymmetry was real because a family's *read* tensor is fixed by its scalar program — three of the four carry a prologue that reads the original input. No such distinction exists for the write: no fold's algebra says whether its result is the caller's answer. Widening only the bare sum would therefore assert that a squaring prologue's result is inherently a program output, which is false. Worse, it would be *lossy in a specific way*: `sum(x * x)` compiles as a fused `SquaredSerialSum` region as well as a materialized `prologue -> StrictSerialSum` pair, so refusing the fused family's write would drop the fused alternative for every reduction-with-epilogue program while admitting the materialized one — silently, since nothing measures a strategy the vocabulary cannot spell. That is exactly what [`admit-a-reduction-over-a-declared-input-tensor`](admit-a-reduction-over-a-declared-input-tensor.md)'s second boundary forbids. All four serial arms widen together.

**Fact — the multi-pass and cooperative arms, decided explicitly, and the dispatch brief's premise about them was wrong.** The brief stated that "the split's FINAL pass still writes exactly Intermediate". It does not and never did: the final pass *reads* exactly `Intermediate` and *wrote* exactly `Output`; the predecessor's inventory sentence the brief was compressing is about `read_tensor`. Read from the verifier at base `c5d05896`, the write rules were partial `== Intermediate` (line 1382), final `== Output` (line 1391), cooperative `!= Output` (line 1485). The decision derived from what each pass commits:

- **Multi-pass final pass — widened to `CoverAssigned`.** It commits the reduction's own result, exactly as the serial fold it replaces does. Leaving it fixed would make the split alternative unspellable for every reduction whose result an epilogue consumes — the same silent alternative loss as above, one topology over.
- **Cooperative tile — widened to `CoverAssigned`.** A tile is both halves of a split in one dispatch, so its single write is the fold's committing write. It stages in workgroup memory rather than in a boundary tensor, which is why it needs no pass distinction here, exactly as it needs none for its read.
- **Multi-pass partial pass — deliberately *not* widened, and this is the asymmetry.** A partial is an unfolded fragment of the reduction; it is no cover's declared output, and committing one there would publish a fragment as the program's answer. It keeps `CommittedTensor::Exactly(TensorRole::Intermediate)`. So the rule a reader can now derive is: *every committing pass is cover-assigned; the one staging pass is fixed by its role in the split.* The write target is deliberately **not** a `SplitFamily` field, because it varies by pass and never by family — carrying it there would let a family declare a target it has no authority over.

**Fact — the identity determination, per encoding site, and no pin moved.** Five sites map `TensorRole` totally: `push_tensor_role` in `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/model.rs:1679` (schedule domain, `tiler.schedule.v5`) and in `crates/tiler-ir/src/kernel/model.rs:1551` (kernel domain), `push_tensor_role` in `tiler-compiler`'s `selection.rs:1698` and `frontier.rs:3300`, and `push_tensor_role_name` in `call_registry.rs:395`. Each was read. All four byte encoders write `0x01` plus a four-byte big-endian ordinal for `Input`, a bare `0x02` for `Intermediate`, a bare `0x03` for `Output`; the subject renderer writes the ordinal-suffixed prefix, `intermediate`, and `output`. The tag alone separates the arms before any payload is read and each arm is self-delimiting, so per-tag injectivity holds. The write *position* already encoded `Intermediate` before this branch — `verify_contraction`, `verify_pointwise_region`, and `verify_strict_affine_u4_dequantize` all admit `Intermediate | Output` for the owning write, and the split's partial pass requires it — so no encoder needed an arm and none got one. The verifier's write role reaches `encode_identity` three ways (`push_access` over the write access, its bounds proof, and the ownership proof at `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/model.rs:2467`), which is what `the_committed_tensor_separates_scheduled_region_identity` pins. **No encoder file was edited on this branch** (`git diff --name-only c5d05896 -- crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/model.rs crates/tiler-ir/src/kernel/model.rs crates/tiler-compiler/src/selection.rs crates/tiler-compiler/src/frontier.rs crates/tiler-compiler/src/call_registry.rs` → empty), so the widening enlarges the set of encodable regions without moving any previously encodable region's bytes: appends-only, no domain step, no ledger entry, no recompute ticket.
