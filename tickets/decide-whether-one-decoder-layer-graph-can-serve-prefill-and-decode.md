## What was found, and where

**Measurement, 2026-08-05, at `crates/tiler-reference/tests/decoder_layer.rs`.** The assembled decoder layer (P2) carries **fifty-eight** occurrences at the C1 prefill row (`T = 10`) and **sixty-two** at the C1 decode row (`T = 1`). It is not one graph at the two rows, and the difference is not extents.

**Fact — the mechanism.** `BroadcastAxisMapping::result_shape` refuses a many-to-one relation onto a result axis of extent below two, under `broadcast.mapping.relation-does-not-widen`, and the refusal's own documentation names the replacement: "written as a replication it is a reindex's unit-axis insertion". At `T = 1` six of the layer's widenings pad the position axis onto extent one:

- the two `[1024]` normalization weights (`w_input_layernorm`, `w_post_attention_layernorm`) lose their broadcast entirely, because the mapping left after removing the position axis is the identity and states no widening either — `[1024] -> [1, 1024]` is a `Reindex::insert_unit_axis` and nothing else;
- the two `[128]` per-head weights and the two `[2, 1]` rotary signs keep a broadcast over the head axis and gain an insertion.

Four extra occurrences, and two broadcasts become reindexes. `a_rank_pad_onto_a_single_position_refuses` watches the refusal at all three widenings beside its admitted neighbour at ten positions; `a_single_new_position_changes_six_widenings` counts the delta.

**Fact — the cache half of the claim does hold.** At a fixed `T`, the layer built against `C = 0` and against `C = 8` has an identical occurrence signature: same families in the same order, same reindex forms, same contraction structures, same broadcast relations (`a_nonempty_cache_changes_no_occurrence`). So the sequence extension is exactly the binding change the autoregressive-state record says it is.

## What this bears on

Two proposals in merged records read as though one program serves both phases:

- [the autoregressive-state record](../docs/research/runtime/autoregressive-state-and-kv-cache.md) — "the decode step is not a second program design. It is L4's program with two inputs, two occurrences, and one changed extent binding", and candidate **P2**'s ground that P1 "packages the same twenty-two steps twice".
- [the complete-model record](../docs/research/program-planning/complete-model-ingestion-and-execution.md) — "the complete C1 run is nine forward passes of thirty executions, **270 executions over exactly three artifact identities**, and a build that produced a fourth would have specialized something it must not."

Neither is refuted as a *design*; what is refuted is that the current semantic vocabulary can express it. The attention-block work already recorded the weaker half — an explicit `BroadcastAxisMapping` carries its declared result extents into canonical identity, so a prefill row and a decode row differ in attribute bytes. This ticket is the stronger half: they differ in occurrence count and family sequence, so no amount of extent-symbol plumbing inside a mapping's *extents* field fixes it on its own. A mapping would additionally have to state a widening whose legality is not decidable at construction.

## Candidates, none eliminated here

1. **Accept two identities per layer**, one for prefill and one for decode, and correct the three-identity claim to four (P1, P2-prefill, P2-decode, P3). Cheapest; costs the record's stated invariant and a test that can assert it.
2. **Admit a symbolic-extent broadcast mapping**, so a `replicate` onto a symbolic extent is legal and its widening is checked where the extent is bound. Reaches the records' claim; is a semantic-vocabulary change with identity, validation and lowering consequences, and is Tom's.
3. **Spell every position-axis rank pad as a unit-axis insertion followed by a stretch**, uniformly at both rows. Refused as written today — `StretchUnit` onto a result extent of one is the same `relation-does-not-widen` refusal — so this candidate needs the same relaxation as 2 and is not independent of it.
4. **Chunked decode** (`T >= 2` always, with a one-token chunk padded), which makes the question disappear at the cost of computing positions the consumer discards. Not evaluated.

## Closes when

The disagreement between the measured occurrence counts and the two records' one-program claim is resolved by an explicit decision: either the records are corrected to state two identities per layer with the ground above, or a semantic change is accepted that makes the two rows one graph. Whichever it is, the affected sentences in both records move in the same change, and `decoder_layer.rs`'s two count assertions are updated or kept as the evidence.

## Outcome

**One candidate survives, and it is neither of the two the *Closes when* above anticipated.** The records are corrected — but not to "two identities per layer", because that number is true in no vocabulary that exists or is planned — and the semantic change is filed with its exact dependency rather than taken, because it is Tom's at its interface and unstartable at its base. `decoder_layer.rs` is untouched: its two count assertions **are** the evidence and neither needed updating.

### Two facts the candidate list did not have, both read from source

**Fact — the divergence is stronger than the ticket's mechanism section states, and the extra clause is what closes candidate 3.** `BroadcastAxisSource::is_many_to_one` returns true for `StretchUnit` as well as `Replicate` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/semantic/broadcast.rs:151`), so the `< 2` test at `:483` refuses a stretch onto an extent-one result axis under the identical rule; and `:492` additionally refuses a mapping of only one-to-one correspondences. Together with the source-count and ascending-consumption rules at `:462` and `:468`, `[1024] -> [1, 1024]` has no broadcast spelling **at all** — not a different one.

**Fact — the whole three-identity claim is a claim about the symbolic world, and today's vocabulary misses it by far more than four.** `compute_graph_identity` encodes every input shape and every operation-result shape into `SemanticGraphIdentity` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/semantic/identity.rs:87`, lines 103 and 125) and `Extent` is a plain `u64` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/shape.rs:60`), so under fixed extents each distinct `(T, C, S)` triple of the C1 row is its own identity: P2 at nine, P1 at two, P3 at two — **thirteen**. L6's own refusal 3 says the inline route refuses a symbolic region, so the claim is not evaluable today rather than false today, and the correction has to say which.

### The elimination

Each candidate tested against correctness, performance and long-term maintainability, with the derivation stated so a reader can refute the elimination rather than only the conclusion.

| Candidate | Survives? | Ground |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** — accept two identities per layer; correct three to four | **No** | The number is wrong in every vocabulary. Under today's fixed extents the C1 row is thirteen, not four; under a vocabulary supplying symbolic value shapes *and* symbolic mapping extents *and* a defined degenerate widening it is three, exactly as the records say. Four is the count under a vocabulary supplying the first two and not the third — which nothing proposes and nobody is building. Writing four into the records would pin a number true in no world that exists or is planned, and L8 would then gate a conformance run on it. Refutable by exhibiting the vocabulary in which four is the count. |
| **2** — symbolic-extent broadcast mapping, widening checked where the extent is bound | **No as stated; its repaired form is the only route to the claim** | The layer declares `T` over `[1, 32768]`, so the check has three readings and two are worse than the divergence. At construction against the proved lower bound, `T >= 2` is not proved and the layer is unbuildable at *both* rows. At binding, `T = 1` refuses at `LiveDevicePreflight` and the decode step never runs — a construction-time graph divergence converted into a run-time refusal at exactly the row that needed it. Only *defining* the degenerate binding yields one graph, which is a different change from the one the candidate names: a family's normative definition and admitted-relation set, not a relocated predicate. That is Tom's, and it is unstartable before the sourced-extent vocabulary reaches `tiler_ir::shape`. Filed rather than taken. |
| **3** — uniform insertion followed by a stretch | **No** | Not independent, and now measured so: `is_many_to_one` covers `StretchUnit`, so the stretch is refused under the same rule at `T = 1`, and `[1, 1024] -> [1, 1024]` is additionally `no-many-to-one-relation`. It needs exactly candidate 2's repaired form and adds an insertion at every row including prefill, where the current spelling needs none. Strictly dominated. |
| **4** — chunked decode, `T >= 2` always | **No, on all three axes** | *It does not reach the claim*, which is decisive on its own: the mapping still declares a literal `[2, 1024]` against `[10, 1024]`, so it is still two identities. *Correctness*: the padded position is appended to the retained `K` and `V` by the concatenation, and removing it needs a sub-tensor selection the corpus does not define, while a `[8, C+2, 128]` row-major payload does not truncate by re-declaring an extent — the head axis is outermost, so the sequence stride is a function of the extent. The correct spelling — bind `C - 1` and `T = 2`, recomputing the previous position — obliges the driver to carry a `[2, 1024]` residual through all 28 layers and doubles every decode step's position-dependent work. *Performance*: either the KV grows two positions per token (1.7773 GiB of logical payload becomes 3.55 at B1-d) or every step copies to truncate, against a step whose cache traffic is currently 0.27% of its weight traffic. Paying a correctness surface and a residency multiple for a property that is not the one in dispute is not a trade. |
| **5** *(derived)* — remove the widenings by widening a consuming family's signature, e.g. `rms-norm-f32` taking a weight of the reduced axis's extent | **No** | Two independent grounds. It does not close the gap: it removes four of the six widenings and leaves fifty-four against fifty-six, because the two `rope_sign` widenings feed a `Multiply` and are untouched. And it reintroduces implicit broadcasting into a family signature, which the broadcast family's own module documentation forecloses — "Rank padding, extent-one stretching, and every other many-to-one mapping require an occurrence of this key, in every signature and at every rank." |
| **6** *(derived)* — relax the literal degenerate refusal alone, admitting `Replicate` onto a literal extent of one | **No** | It equalizes the occurrence count today with no symbolic vocabulary, and buys the wrong half: the mapping still declares `[1, 1024]` against `[10, 1024]`, so identity still moves with the row. What it costs is the family's injectivity — at a literal extent of one a `Replicate` and a `Reindex::insert_unit_axis` become two spellings of one function, so two frontends emitting one program mint two identities and two cache entries. An injectivity loss for an occurrence-count cosmetic is a straight loss. |
| **7** — record the finding as a named, dated condition on the claim and file the vocabulary work with its exact dependency | **Yes** | No present candidate reaches the claim; the one that does is downstream, unstartable, and Tom's at its interface. Correcting the records to state the claim conditionally, with the condition named and falsifiable, is the only action that is both executable now and true in both vocabularies. |

**Inference — the two survivors the ticket expected are not two, because they are not alternatives.** Correcting the records is required whatever the semantic change turns out to be, and the semantic change is downstream of a correction that names what it must supply. One is not a substitute for the other, so there is no fork and no decision packet; what *is* Tom's is D-19 itself, and it is filed at `deferred` because its own base has not landed.

**Inference — the numerical dimension bounds the whole question and is why none of this is a correctness incident.** **Measurement:** both rows reference-evaluate at 0 differing elements on all three outputs against an independent recomputation that widens the weight identically at both rows (`decoder_layer.rs:1417`), so the padded-broadcast prefill graph and the reindex decode graph are the same function per element — over synthetic operands at a recorded seed, on one host, at two rows of one workload. The divergence can affect identity and occurrence count and nothing else, which is what makes it a vocabulary question rather than a defect.

### Records corrected, in this change

- [L6, complete model ingestion and execution](../docs/research/program-planning/complete-model-ingestion-and-execution.md) — the "270 executions over exactly three artifact identities" sentence marked conditional with a dated correction carrying the three properties, the derivation that a relocated check makes it worse, and the measurement boundary; the identity count in the worked C1 example pointed at it; **D-19 opened**. The typed refusal that a build producing more than one identity for the twenty-eight layer executions is refused at artifact assembly is **unaffected and says so**: `T` is fixed within one pass, so all twenty-eight layers share one graph.
- [L5, autoregressive state and KV cache](../docs/research/runtime/autoregressive-state-and-kv-cache.md) — "the decode step is not a second program design" corrected with the measurement that its cache half holds and its `T` half does not; P2's retained-item row narrowed to `C`; P1's elimination **narrowed rather than withdrawn**, with the distinction that P1's duplication is a design choice and this one is a family rule.
- [L8, model-level qualification](../docs/research/program-planning/model-level-qualification.md) — the C1 row, the shared conformance frame, the "exactly 3 times cold" runtime-preparation invariant, and the pinned regression list. The sharpest addition is that **A-prompt-1 already exercised the divergence**: a one-token prompt is a `T = 1` prefill at `C = 0`, so its graph is neither the C1 prefill graph nor a decode graph in L5's sense, and the row's expected outcome named the cache path rather than the graph. The pipeline-count invariant survives and its *attribution* does not — four pipelines now has two causes with opposite remedies, separable at the artifact because a specialization on `S` mints nine P2 identities at C1 and the divergence mints two.
- [L4, first attention program vertical](../docs/research/program-planning/first-attention-program-vertical.md) — **not edited, deliberately.** Its claim is that `S` is a separate symbol from `T` so that widening the context is a binding change rather than a graph change, and `a_nonempty_cache_changes_no_occurrence` measures exactly that. The finding confirms L4 rather than contradicting it, and L6's correction says so.

### Measurement boundary

Everything asserted here is the semantic layer, read from source at base `b81034e6`, plus the two rows `crates/tiler-reference/tests/decoder_layer.rs` already measures on one host over synthetic operands at a recorded seed. Nothing is established about a plan, a schedule, a kernel, a device, artifact identity **bytes** (the identity reasoning is over what `compute_graph_identity` encodes, not over a computed digest), or any layer-level numeric tolerance. The thirteen-identity figure for the C1 row is an inference from that encoding and from the row's nine `(T, C, S)` triples, not a count of built artifacts — none exists.
