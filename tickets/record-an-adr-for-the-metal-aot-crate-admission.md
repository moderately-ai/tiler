**Fact — no accepted ADR records `tiler-metal-aot`'s admission, and two accepted ADRs still read as though it does not exist.** `prototype-apple-aot-driver` created the crate, added it to `Cargo.toml`'s `members` and `[workspace.dependencies]`, and pinned it in `scripts/check_workspace.py`'s `EXPECTED_MEMBERS`, `PACKAGE_DESCRIPTIONS`, `PACKAGE_DIRS`, and `EXPECTED_DEPENDENCIES` (empty list). It held `implementation/metal-aot` and `implementation/workspace`, never `contracts/decisions`, so it could not have written one. `record-metal-aot-in-architecture-crate-profile` then recorded the crate, its empty dependency closure, and the development-only `tiler-metal` → `tiler-metal-aot` edge in `docs/architecture.md`'s accepted packaging profile, and stated there that the ADR record is still open. That is the whole gap this ticket closes.

**Fact — what the ADR record currently says.** ADR 0056 (`decision_status: superseded`) decided the packaging profile by exhaustive enumeration and stated "MSL emission and AOT invocation remain modules in `tiler-metal`" and "No frontend, proc-macro, Candle, generalized cache, or reusable Metal-runtime crate is created for the first proof." Its status line supersedes it only "for the reusable-crate count and reference-evaluator placement" (ADR 0065) and "for the compiler-to-artifact dependency edge" (ADR 0070), and says "the remaining packaging boundaries are retained" — so the AOT-invocation clause is a retained boundary that the workspace has departed from. ADR 0070's Decision block states "The dependency direction is:" and then lists five crates, omitting the sixth and the development edge; the edges it does list are all still correct, so it is incomplete rather than wrong. ADR 0065 is correct exactly as accepted: its "Add a fifth reusable target-independent crate" is an ordinal statement about `tiler-reference`, not a standing cardinality, and nothing about a sixth crate reverses or narrows it. It needs no superseding note.

**What the ADR should decide.** That `tiler-metal-aot` is admitted as the offline Apple Metal compiler driver; that its **empty dependency closure is a decided, mechanically checked property** rather than an incidental one, because the crate's value is that the exact `xcrun metal`/`xcrun metallib` invocation can be audited without the lowering stack behind it; and that the `tiler-metal` → `tiler-metal-aot` edge is **development-only**, for the two reasons `compile-golden-msl-through-the-aot-driver-in-the-gate` established — `tiler-metal` must not acquire Apple tool discovery in a consumer's build graph, and Cargo permits a cycle through a development dependency but rejects one through a normal dependency, so keeping the edge out of the normal graph preserves the eventual `tiler-metal-aot` → `tiler-metal` production direction. It must supersede ADR 0056's retained AOT-invocation clause explicitly rather than leave the workspace silently contradicting it, and it should restate ADR 0070's dependency block with six crates and both development edges.

**The Apple target-vocabulary split belongs here as a consequence clause, not as its own ADR.** `record-metal-aot-in-architecture-crate-profile` decided that the split does not warrant a standalone ADR: it changes no public surface, so none of ADR 0075's always-ask categories reaches it; the full reasoning already lives on `crates/tiler-metal/src/target.rs`, `crates/tiler-metal-aot/src/input.rs`, and `crates/tiler-metal/src/target_correspondence.rs`; and the one sentence that would have led a future reader to consolidate it has been removed from `docs/architecture.md`. But the split *follows from* the empty-closure property this ADR would establish — a shared crate or a dependency edge for three enums is rejected because it costs the driver that closure — so one or two Consequences sentences here, naming `target_correspondence.rs` as the enforcement mechanism, put it in the ADR record without creating a fourth standalone authority. Do not restate the reasoning in full; reference the types.

**Why three scopes.** `contracts/decisions` for the new ADR and for ADR 0056's `superseded_by`/status text. `contracts/navigation` because `scripts/docs.py render` regenerates the `ADR TOPICS` and `ADR CHRONOLOGY` blocks in `docs/decisions/README.md`, and `scripts/check_repository.py` runs `scripts/docs.py validate`, which calls `render(check=True)` and fails on a stale block — so a new ADR cannot land without that file. `contracts/foundation` to replace `docs/architecture.md`'s paragraph naming this ticket as the open gap with the accepted decision reference. Holding `contracts/navigation` is why this could not be done inside `record-metal-aot-in-architecture-crate-profile`: `scope-einsum-contraction-support` held it exclusively at the time.

**Do not accept the ADR.** Draft it `decision_status: proposed`; Tom accepts.

## Outcome

**ADR 0077, `Admit tiler-metal-aot as a dependency-free offline driver`**, drafted `decision_status: proposed` at `docs/decisions/0077-admit-tiler-metal-aot-as-a-dependency-free-driver.md`. `implementation_status` is `implemented`, which is the honest pairing for this record and the reason it exists: the crate, the empty closure, and the development-only edge are already in the workspace and mechanically pinned, and what was missing was the decision. Acceptance changes the record, not the workspace.

**Fact — how ADR 0056 is superseded, and what remains retained.** The supersession is recorded in three places and cuts exactly one clause. ADR 0077 carries `supersedes: ["ADR-0056"]` in frontmatter, which is the repository's only supersession relation — there is no `superseded_by` field; `scripts/docs.py` validates the decision-to-decision `supersedes` edge in both directions and every prior supersession (ADRs 0065, 0067, 0070) uses this form. ADR 0077's Decision item 5 states that it supersedes exactly one clause of ADR 0056 — that AOT invocation remains a module in `tiler-metal` — and then restates, as a four-item list, what is untouched: MSL emission remains a `tiler-metal` module (only the invocation half moved); multiple target-independent IRs remain `tiler-ir` modules and compiler passes remain `tiler-compiler` modules; the runner depends on the artifact contract and live Metal bindings, never the compiler; and no frontend, proc-macro, Candle, generalized cache, or reusable Metal-runtime crate is created for the first proof. That last item carries an explicit inference, because a careless reader could take this admission as precedent against it: `tiler-metal-aot` is a build-time compiler driver that never touches a live device, an `MTLDevice`, or a pipeline state, so it is not the reusable Metal-*runtime* crate the clause withholds. ADR 0056's own status line gains a clause naming ADR 0077 and saying it is not accepted, so that clause is retained text the workspace has already departed from — worded that way deliberately, since a proposed record may not be reported as having superseded anything.

**Decision — one edit to ADR 0056 is deliberately withheld, and ADR 0077 says so rather than leaving it to be noticed.** ADR 0056's Consequences already carry an in-body `**Retired:**` marker where ADRs 0070 and 0071 retired a clause, so the repository has a convention for annotating a superseded clause at the point of contact, and the Decision paragraph should gain the same marker beside "MSL emission and AOT invocation remain modules in `tiler-metal`". Writing it now would assert a supersession that has not happened. ADR 0077's "Implementation boundary" section records the pending edit and names it as the acceptor's, so it cannot be lost.

**Decision — ADR 0070 is refined, not superseded, and not amended in place.** `refines: ["ADR-0070"]`, and ADR 0077's item 3 restates the dependency block completely with six libraries and both development edges. Every edge ADR 0070 lists is still exactly what `scripts/check_workspace.py` pins, so superseding it would retire correct decisions in order to add omitted ones and would tell a reader that one of its edges had changed when none has; editing its Decision block in place would rewrite what an accepted record decided at the time it decided it. The repository already sets this precedent in the same lineage: ADR 0070 restated ADR 0065's block including `tiler-reference` without editing or superseding ADR 0065, and said in terms which single ADR 0056 clause it superseded. Two omissions are covered, not one — the sixth crate, and both development edges including `tiler-compiler` → development `tiler-reference`, which predates the driver entirely and which ADR 0065 already authorizes as test consumption.

**Fact — ADR 0065 is untouched, and the argument for that was corrected during drafting.** The first draft argued that "fifth reusable target-independent crate" could not count `tiler-metal-aot` because the driver is target-*dependent*. That does not survive arithmetic: ADR 0056's four are `tiler-ir`, `tiler-artifact`, `tiler-compiler`, and `tiler-metal`, and `tiler-metal` is itself target-dependent, so "fifth" is correct only when the count runs over every reusable crate — which makes "target-independent" a description of `tiler-reference`, not a filter on the counted set. ADR 0077 records the failed argument explicitly so it is not reached for later, and rests on the ordinal reading alone, which is sufficient: ADR 0065 states which crate it adds and what that crate owns, not how many the workspace may hold.

**Fact — the Apple target-vocabulary split is two Consequences bullets, referencing the types rather than restating them.** One bullet states that the vocabulary stays owned twice and that the decisive argument was dependency *closure* — collapsing the overlap into a shared type or a dependency edge spends the driver's empty closure on three enumerations — pointing at `crates/tiler-metal/src/target.rs` and `crates/tiler-metal-aot/src/input.rs` for the reasoning and rejected alternatives. The second names `crates/tiler-metal/src/target_correspondence.rs` as the enforcement: a total map whose matches are exhaustive over both vocabularies, which can only live in `tiler-metal` because the development edge is the sole edge in the workspace over which both vocabularies are visible at once. A third bullet records the consequence that falls out — `MslVersion` and `ApplePlatform` cannot become `#[non_exhaustive]` under ADR 0074 convention 5b — because that is a live obligation on a ticket still in the backlog.

**Fact — `docs/architecture.md`'s "no accepted ADR yet" sentence is kept and repointed.** The sentence remains true and remains, because ADR 0077 is proposed; what changed is what follows it. The paragraph previously named this ticket as the owner of the gap, which would have become a stale pointer the moment the ticket closed. It now links ADR 0077 as the proposed record, says the supersession takes effect on Tom's acceptance and that until then ADR 0056's retained text still places AOT invocation inside `tiler-metal`, and states that this section and that proposal are together the record. The ADR 0065 clarification is kept and extended to say it is superseded by neither record. The Component-ownership paragraph above it, which says the `tiler-metal-aot` row "carries no accepted ADR of its own" and routes the reader to the packaging profile, was left alone: it is still true and the routing now terminates at ADR 0077.
