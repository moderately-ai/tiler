**Fact — no accepted ADR records `tiler-metal-aot`'s admission, and two accepted ADRs still read as though it does not exist.** `prototype-apple-aot-driver` created the crate, added it to `Cargo.toml`'s `members` and `[workspace.dependencies]`, and pinned it in `scripts/check_workspace.py`'s `EXPECTED_MEMBERS`, `PACKAGE_DESCRIPTIONS`, `PACKAGE_DIRS`, and `EXPECTED_DEPENDENCIES` (empty list). It held `implementation/metal-aot` and `implementation/workspace`, never `contracts/decisions`, so it could not have written one. `record-metal-aot-in-architecture-crate-profile` then recorded the crate, its empty dependency closure, and the development-only `tiler-metal` → `tiler-metal-aot` edge in `docs/architecture.md`'s accepted packaging profile, and stated there that the ADR record is still open. That is the whole gap this ticket closes.

**Fact — what the ADR record currently says.** ADR 0056 (`decision_status: superseded`) decided the packaging profile by exhaustive enumeration and stated "MSL emission and AOT invocation remain modules in `tiler-metal`" and "No frontend, proc-macro, Candle, generalized cache, or reusable Metal-runtime crate is created for the first proof." Its status line supersedes it only "for the reusable-crate count and reference-evaluator placement" (ADR 0065) and "for the compiler-to-artifact dependency edge" (ADR 0070), and says "the remaining packaging boundaries are retained" — so the AOT-invocation clause is a retained boundary that the workspace has departed from. ADR 0070's Decision block states "The dependency direction is:" and then lists five crates, omitting the sixth and the development edge; the edges it does list are all still correct, so it is incomplete rather than wrong. ADR 0065 is correct exactly as accepted: its "Add a fifth reusable target-independent crate" is an ordinal statement about `tiler-reference`, not a standing cardinality, and nothing about a sixth crate reverses or narrows it. It needs no superseding note.

**What the ADR should decide.** That `tiler-metal-aot` is admitted as the offline Apple Metal compiler driver; that its **empty dependency closure is a decided, mechanically checked property** rather than an incidental one, because the crate's value is that the exact `xcrun metal`/`xcrun metallib` invocation can be audited without the lowering stack behind it; and that the `tiler-metal` → `tiler-metal-aot` edge is **development-only**, for the two reasons `compile-golden-msl-through-the-aot-driver-in-the-gate` established — `tiler-metal` must not acquire Apple tool discovery in a consumer's build graph, and Cargo permits a cycle through a development dependency but rejects one through a normal dependency, so keeping the edge out of the normal graph preserves the eventual `tiler-metal-aot` → `tiler-metal` production direction. It must supersede ADR 0056's retained AOT-invocation clause explicitly rather than leave the workspace silently contradicting it, and it should restate ADR 0070's dependency block with six crates and both development edges.

**The Apple target-vocabulary split belongs here as a consequence clause, not as its own ADR.** `record-metal-aot-in-architecture-crate-profile` decided that the split does not warrant a standalone ADR: it changes no public surface, so none of ADR 0075's always-ask categories reaches it; the full reasoning already lives on `crates/tiler-metal/src/target.rs`, `crates/tiler-metal-aot/src/input.rs`, and `crates/tiler-metal/src/target_correspondence.rs`; and the one sentence that would have led a future reader to consolidate it has been removed from `docs/architecture.md`. But the split *follows from* the empty-closure property this ADR would establish — a shared crate or a dependency edge for three enums is rejected because it costs the driver that closure — so one or two Consequences sentences here, naming `target_correspondence.rs` as the enforcement mechanism, put it in the ADR record without creating a fourth standalone authority. Do not restate the reasoning in full; reference the types.

**Why three scopes.** `contracts/decisions` for the new ADR and for ADR 0056's `superseded_by`/status text. `contracts/navigation` because `scripts/docs.py render` regenerates the `ADR TOPICS` and `ADR CHRONOLOGY` blocks in `docs/decisions/README.md`, and `scripts/check_repository.py` runs `scripts/docs.py validate`, which calls `render(check=True)` and fails on a stale block — so a new ADR cannot land without that file. `contracts/foundation` to replace `docs/architecture.md`'s paragraph naming this ticket as the open gap with the accepted decision reference. Holding `contracts/navigation` is why this could not be done inside `record-metal-aot-in-architecture-crate-profile`: `scope-einsum-contraction-support` held it exclusively at the time.

**Do not accept the ADR.** Draft it `decision_status: proposed`; Tom accepts.
