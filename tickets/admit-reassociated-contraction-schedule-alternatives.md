## User-visible outcome

*Rewritten 2026-08-19 under [`revise-contraction-split-admission-to-contiguous-only-delivery`](revise-contraction-split-admission-to-contiguous-only-delivery.md); the retired two-plan wording and the reason it became unsatisfiable are recorded in [Contract revision — 2026-08-19](#contract-revision--2026-08-19-at-exact-base-01e4ecec) below.*

A caller whose request permits reassociation on a `tiler::tensor-contraction-f32@1` occurrence gets a contracted-axis contiguous split schedule whose combine tree is a witnessed member of the operation's declared result set; a caller who withholds reassociation gets the strict left fold, bit for bit, exactly as before. A caller who permits *permutation* as well gets the same contiguous split and a typed **algebraic** refusal of the lane-strided one — because the operation's own descriptor declares fold permutation `unsupported`, no permission can grant it, and the refusal must say so rather than blaming the caller's numerical contract.

## The distinction, measured

**Measurement — from the [L3 realization record](../docs/research/scheduling/first-metal-contraction-realizations.md).** Two split kernels were implemented and measured. `ksplit_contiguous` partitions the contracted axis into contiguous intervals merged in ascending order and is attributed uniquely to `contiguous_split+ftz`; `ksplit_strided` partitions it into strided subsets and is attributed uniquely to `strided_split+ftz`. They return different bits: at the spike's `split_topology` case, `0xbb1d0683` against `0xbb1d0672`. [Reduction semantics and legality](../docs/research/numerics/reduction-semantics-and-legality.md) fixes why — reassociation without permutation may combine only contiguous contributor intervals in order, and a lane-strided partition reorders leaves — and this is the measurement that makes it a plan difference rather than a definitional one.

**Measurement — and one of them is the fastest thing measured at decode.** At the complete vocabulary projection (`M=1, N=151936, K=1024`) `ksplit_contiguous` ran in 4,247 microseconds on the M3 Pro bench host, ahead of `MPSMatrixMultiplication` at 4,418 and the strict `direct` kernel at 4,757. The cell is bandwidth-bound at about 146 GB/s and those candidates are all near the same bound, so the win is small — but it is the shape where the split is worth having, and prefill is the shape where it is not: the same kernel is roughly 5x to 7x *slower* than the tiled strict kernel at `M >= 128`.

## Required delivery

*Rewritten 2026-08-19; the retired two-alternative list is quoted in [Contract revision — 2026-08-19](#contract-revision--2026-08-19-at-exact-base-01e4ecec) below, which also carries the verifier contract and the three review-inherited obligations this list depends on.*

- **One** schedule alternative — the contiguous contracted-axis split — declaring the exact freedom it consumes, admitted only through the accepted two-fact join and never through a permission alone.
- A lane-strided proposal, wherever one is reachable, refused with the **algebraic** cause naming the withheld dimension, distinct from the numerical cause and never collapsed into it. A rejection that says "reassociation forbidden", or that blames the caller's ceiling, for a lane-strided plan is the wrong explanation.
- The split precondition `K` a positive multiple of the split width as a typed refusal.
- Partial state per [Reduction semantics and legality](../docs/research/numerics/reduction-semantics-and-legality.md): the seed attaches once at the root-facing boundary and never per lane, and a partial carries `has_value` unless nonemptiness or proven-neutral padding is established.
- **This implementation is not the strategy.** The spike's split kernels dedicate 32 lanes to one output and idle most of them at large `M`, which is a schedule written to isolate a reduction topology rather than to be fast. Their prefill numbers bound that implementation and are not evidence against split reductions; a split that also tiles the free indices is unmeasured.

## Non-goals

Distributivity, in either direction. Regrouping a contraction *chain* is a different dimension that no contract Tiler can express grants, and its rejection must name distributivity rather than reassociation.

Lane-strided contributor membership as a *delivered* plan. It stays a refusal subject here, parked behind the trigger recorded in [Contract revision — 2026-08-19](#contract-revision--2026-08-19-at-exact-base-01e4ecec).

### Lane-strided admission: parked, with its trigger stated here

The 2026-08-18 acceptance's downstream item (6) leaves lane-strided admission behind a trigger but records it in the accepting packets, not here. It is stated here now, because this is the ticket a worker reads.

**Trigger — both conditions, neither sufficient alone.**

1. **A future successor key generation declares fold commutativity.** Concretely: a generation after `tiler::tensor-contraction-f32@1` whose reduction record spells `permutation: permission-gated` instead of `unsupported`. This cannot be a fact edit on the current key — ADR 0112 fixed a meaning change as requiring a new key generation, on the measured artifact-hybrid control, and the decoder actively refuses the alternative spelling today (`crates/tiler-ir/src/semantic/contraction/descriptor.rs "Permutation and signed-zero elimination are operation-owned unsupported"`).
2. **Permutation permission is independently resolved for the occurrence**, through the same effective-profile join, not inferred from reassociation and not defaulted.

Until both fire, a lane-strided proposal is refused **algebraically** on condition 1, before and independently of any check of condition 2. Reversal evidence that would reopen this: an accepted key generation declaring fold permutation, or a demonstration that the descriptor's permutation maximum has stopped being the authority the algebraic decision fixed it as.

**Preserved evidence, referenced and not deleted.** The unmerged implementation attempt `648a372f8cbb306df43a4edfc4e14a6211cac7b1` over `07aca5cd8f67824019d8c183fd3a9584ce84b670` stays preserved on this ticket's branch and stays un-mergeable, for the reason the 2026-08-17 review stop gives. It is the only place a lane-strided physical construction exists, so it is the starting evidence if the trigger ever fires. The accepted membership vocabulary in [`decide-the-fixed-strided-contributor-membership-vocabulary`](decide-the-fixed-strided-contributor-membership-vocabulary.md) likewise stays accepted and referenced: `LaneStrided` remains a named, tag-reserved spelling of the refused population, which is what lets the refusal name a dimension instead of reporting a generic illegality.

### The verifier contract this delivery carries

From the accepted algebraic authority in [`decide-the-algebraic-capability-authority-for-contraction-splits`](decide-the-algebraic-capability-authority-for-contraction-splits.md), landed in the vocabulary ADR 0112 shipped. Steps run in this order; none may be skipped, reordered, or defaulted.

1. **Bind the exact occurrence** — successor `OpKey`, reached registry authority, unique effective realization.
2. **Decode the descriptor** from the reached registered definition through the sole decoder, `crates/tiler-ir/src/semantic/contraction/descriptor.rs "pub fn decode(definition: &OperationDefinition)"`. A second compiler or reference decoder is forbidden. Absence or malformation is a typed error, never a silent strict fallback.
3. **Resolve the ceiling** into the effective profile through the only join, `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/contraction_profile.rs "pub fn resolve("` — the sole constructor of `EffectiveContractionF32Profile` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/contraction_profile.rs "is the sole constructor of"`). These two are the **two-fact join**: descriptor decode is ADR 0014's operation-declared algebraic fact, ceiling resolution is the independently resolved numerical fact, and neither substitutes for the other. Contiguous admission requires effective reassociation.
4. **Refuse lane-strided on the descriptor's permutation maximum**, with the algebraic cause, before any permutation-permission check.
5. **Report the failing source distinctly.** The algebraic cause is `crates/tiler-compiler/src/frontier.rs "The operation's declared algebraic maximum withholds a freedom the"` — `StrategyDeclineCause::AlgebraicCapabilityUnsupported`, stable reason key `crates/tiler-compiler/src/frontier.rs "algebraic-capability-unsupported"`, canonical tag `0x06` appended under the existing per-tag injectivity rule (`crates/tiler-compiler/src/frontier.rs "is the algebraic case; it was unused before"`), so adding it moved no pinned identity. It routes to `ExplainStage::CapabilityResolution` and carries a `withheld-dimension` fact (`crates/tiler-compiler/src/pipeline/trace.rs "withheld-dimension"`), where the numerical cause routes to `ExplainStage::NumericalLegality` and carries `refused-dimension` — `crates/tiler-compiler/src/pipeline/trace.rs "the failing source is"`. The two are never collapsed into one verdict.
6. **Require refinement-proved realization** of the descriptor's reducer primitive for every partition fold and for the merge, then the shape/partition checks, **then recheck every fact on provider output before frontier admission**. Provider-side proposal filtering alone is bypassable; the recheck is not optional and is not discharged by the provider having declined correctly.
