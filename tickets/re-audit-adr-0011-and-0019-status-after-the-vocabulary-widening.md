`close-remaining-adr-status-drift` audited all 71 accepted ADRs against `crates/` and deliberately left ADR 0011 and ADR 0019 at `not-started`, recording the exact reason: "the decision's central mechanism does not exist and a bump would misreport a type-system reservation or an architectural seam as implemented support: ADR 0011 and ADR 0019 (single-variant permission and subnormal enums that can never resolve anything)".

**Fact — that reason no longer holds.** `widen-numerical-vocabulary-and-complete-identity` (`1f78223`, 2026-07-24) made both enums multi-variant. `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/numerics.rs` defines `SubnormalMode` as `Preserve | FlushToZero { zero_sign }` and `NumericalPermission` as `Forbidden | Permitted`. Both subnormal dimensions of `NumericalRealization` now resolve independently and can differ; both permissions can be granted or withheld. The enums resolve something.

**What this ticket does not assert.** That either ADR is therefore `partial`. The falsified premise is the stated *reason for exclusion*, not the conclusion, and the two records differ:

- ADR 0019's decided behaviour is close to the landed shape — independent input and result dimensions, both behaviours expressible, a backend that must emulate, consume an authorized relaxation, or reject. `crates/tiler-metal/src/emit.rs` and `crates/tiler-metal/src/record.rs` now realize the reject branch per declared dimension with three typed gap variants. Its Consequences also claim reference evaluation can distinguish input flushing from result flushing; `crates/tiler-reference/` matches neither `SubnormalMode` nor `NumericalPermission` anywhere, so that clause is unrealized.
- ADR 0011's decided behaviour is a *program ceiling* intersected with per-operation restrictions and operation capabilities, resolved before semantic optimization, with every rewrite declaring which effective permission it consumes. None of that machinery exists; what exists is a region-level `NumericalRealization` and `FusionNumericalProof::forbidden_transforms`. A bump on the strength of the widened enum alone would misreport exactly what the audit ticket warned about.

**What closes this.** Read both decisions and their implementations in full — not this ticket's summary — and either bump or restate the exclusion reason so the corpus records a live justification rather than a dead one. Apply the rule `close-remaining-adr-status-drift` established and `docs/document-metadata.md` states: `implementation_status` is the highest maturity the record's own decided behaviour has reached, a retained high-water mark rather than a live mirror. Do not change `decision_status`. `reconcile-adr-0019-zero-sign-placement-with-the-landed-flush` is a separate question about ADR 0019's body and should not be folded in here.

## Outcome

Both records move `not-started` to `partial`, and each gains an `Implementation boundary` section so the value is legible rather than asserted. `decision_status` is untouched on both. The dead exclusion reason in `close-remaining-adr-status-drift` is marked superseded for these two records and left standing for the other six.

**Retracted — this ticket's own claim about ADR 0011, which was the reason it declined to predict a bump.** It states that ADR 0011's decided machinery — a ceiling intersected with per-operation restrictions and operation capabilities, with every rewrite declaring which effective permission it consumes — does not exist, and that "what exists is a region-level `NumericalRealization` and `FusionNumericalProof::forbidden_transforms`". Reading `crates/tiler-compiler/` in full falsifies that. `crates/tiler-compiler/src/fusion_legality.rs` carries `FusionNumericalCapabilities`, a compiler-owned registry mapping an operation family `OpKey` to a declared `FusionOperationRole`, resolved per member operation by checked lookup and failing closed to `FusionLegality::Unknown` for an unregistered family. That is the Decision's "operation's declared capabilities" term, and it is the term the ticket says is absent. The same module discharges each numerical obligation against that role *and* the resolved contract, emitting an ordered list of `DerivedObligation`s each carrying a `FusionEvidenceClass`, with contraction, reduction reassociation, and operand permutation discharged separately so granting one never discharges another — which is "every semantic rewrite declares which effective permission it consumes" for the one rewrite the compiler has. It is on the compile path at `crates/tiler-compiler/src/pipeline.rs`, not behind `#[cfg(test)]`.

**Fact — two further ADR 0011 clauses are realized and neither depends on the widened enum.** `crates/tiler-metal/src/emit.rs`'s `realization_requirements` derives `MetalNumericalRequirement::{NoFloatingPointContraction, SafeMathMode}` from the resolved realization, and `crates/tiler-metal/src/golden_compilation.rs`'s `realization_honours` checks the driver selection actually delivers each, through a same-crate exhaustive match — "backend compiler flags are derived from the resolved program and cannot grant additional freedoms". And both `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/model.rs` and `crates/tiler-ir/src/kernel/model.rs` encode the profile key, canonical NaN bits, both subnormal dimensions, and both permissions into canonical identity.

**Fact — what ADR 0011 still lacks, so `partial` and not more.** There is no per-operation restriction to intersect the program contract with, no region or operation override, and no canonical per-operation permission representation for controls to resolve into. Two of the Decision's three combination terms exist; the middle one has no representation at all. A single program-wide contract behaves like a ceiling only because nothing can be tighter than it, which is a weaker claim than the Decision makes. The granularity is the region, not the operation, so "one program can safely optimize numerically different regions under different effective permissions" is unrealized.

**Fact — ADR 0019 is `partial` on four realized clauses and three unrealized ones.** Realized: the two dimensions exist as independent `SubnormalMode` fields that can differ and are independently encoded into identity; the reject branch of the backend obligation is per declared dimension with three typed gap variants and a fail-closed `require_declared_realization`; and a target that couples the two behaviours in one hardware mode still faces two separately compared contract fields. Unrealized: the emulate branch — `tiler-metal` emits no compensating operations, which is deliberate for a pure source lowering but leaves one of the three stated outs unbuilt; the reference-evaluation consequence, where the exact check `grep -rn 'SubnormalMode\|NumericalPermission\|NumericalRealization' crates/tiler-reference/src/` returns nothing, so the evaluator names no part of the vocabulary and cannot distinguish the two dimensions; and the gap record's granularity, since `record_subnormal_obligation` compares both dimensions against the same target fact and inserts into one set, so the record cannot say which dimension failed. Partially realized: "relaxed contracts may permit either or both kinds of flushing" — both is registrable via `governed_flush_to_zero`, and *either* alone is expressible in the type but not registrable, because `governed_profile` admits exactly preserve/preserve and flush/flush.

**Decision — an `Implementation boundary` section on each rather than a bare frontmatter bump.** `close-remaining-adr-status-drift` touched no ADR body, and that was right for a ten-record sweep. It is wrong here: `partial` on these two records covers a genuinely uneven spread — ADR 0011 has two of three combination terms and the wrong granularity, ADR 0019 has one of three backend branches and an unrealized Consequence — and a bare bump would tell a reader the records had advanced without saying how far. The sections follow ADR 0071's precedent, state clauses as realized or unrealized with the exact site, and add no decision.

**Not folded in.** ADR 0019's Decision-body amendment about zero-sign placement is `reconcile-adr-0019-zero-sign-placement-with-the-landed-flush`'s and landed separately in `591370b`. The six other exclusions in the audit ticket's bullet, and its `undetermined` ADR 0034 finding, were not re-examined and are explicitly left standing.

**Measurement.** `implementation_status` is not a catalog input, so `docs/decisions/README.md` regenerated unchanged. `uv run --locked python scripts/docs.py render` and `uv run --locked python scripts/check_repository.py` both pass; the gate reported "complete repository validation passed".
