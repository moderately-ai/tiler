### What landed

- **The transient-residency predicate is hard feasibility.** `crates/tiler-compiler/src/target/residency.rs`, a crate-private authority carrying no cost notion, no ordering, and no conversion to a number. `assess` answers `Fits`, `Exceeds`, or `BudgetUndeclared`, each carrying the exact byte requirement, the exact declared budget where one exists, and the plan's `n`. An unrepresentable requirement is refused at construction rather than saturated, because a saturated total compares as *exceeds* against every budget and would look like a reasoned refusal carrying a figure that is not the requirement.
- **`Unknown` is a first-class verdict, and it needed a new record rather than a new arm.** `ExplainEvent::Feasibility`'s `available` is not optional and its validation ties the outcome to `required > available`, so "requirement exact, budget absent" was **structurally unstateable** — encoding the absence as zero manufactures a refusal and as `u64::MAX` manufactures an admission. `ExplainEvent::TransientResidency` with a three-valued `ResidencyOutcome` states it instead, dispositioned `DeferredUnsupported`: explain and search state only, never an executable frontier.
- **The five findings are distinguishable**, checked by `the_five_findings_render_distinguishably_and_none_reads_as_not_fused`: a missing fusion role (`Check` / `Assessment::Unknown` / `unsupported-operation-capability`), a budget stop (`BudgetStop`), a target rejection (`Feasibility` / `Rejected`), an `Unknown` residency verdict (`TransientResidency` / `BudgetUndeclared`), and a dominance pruning (`CostAssessment` / `Dominated`). The population is sized from a named constant so deleting a case fails rather than shrinking the census, and every pair is compared with the offending pair named.
- **Identity: nothing stepped, derived rather than assumed.** The new event takes tag `16`, the next unused value; `9` was left alone because nothing records why it is absent and an unexplained gap may already mean something to a decoder. Tags `1..=15` keep their meanings and bytes, so no previously encodable record encodes differently, and neither `tiler.explain.compilation.v1` nor `tiler.explain.trace.v1` steps. `tiler.compiler.request-subject.v6` is untouched — no budget value moved. Confirmed empirically: the pinned `deterministic_trace_is_sealed_and_rendered_separately` request qualifier is unchanged and its test passes.
