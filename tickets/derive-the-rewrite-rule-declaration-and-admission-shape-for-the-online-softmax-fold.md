## User-visible outcome

The one *rule* object the two accepted numerical decisions name as the thing they are waiting for: a derivation of how an operation declares the functional equation it satisfies, how a rewrite rule declares the dimensions it consumes and carries its own parametric bound, and how the six admission obligations are discharged against a complete scheduled candidate — worked end to end on the online-softmax rescaling fold. **It admits no permission, proposes no ADR, and presumes neither accepted decision's outcome.** Under a continued decline it is what makes the refusal checkable rather than described; under an admission it is the object that would consume the permissions. Both readings are served by the same deliverable, which is why doing this work presumes nothing.

## Why this exists, and why it is the ownerless one

**Fact — ADR 0095's 2026-08-06 reaffirmation added a second reopening condition and spelled its prerequisites concretely.** The two permissions "are considered *together* when a rewrite rule with a derived bound instantiable at a schedulable fold shape is ready to consume them", and "ready to consume" is stated as three things: **a rule in the certified-bounds admission shape**, a retrievable `eps_exp` to instantiate its bound, and the bound derived at the fold shape a parallel schedule would select. [ADR 0101](../docs/decisions/0101-treat-elementary-function-identities-as-a-fourth-numerical-dimension.md) item 5 names the same reassessment as its own first trigger clause, so the two decisions move together or not at all.

**Fact — two of the three prerequisites have owners and one does not.** [`expose-the-numeric-elementary-accuracy-a-parametric-bound-can-instantiate`](expose-the-numeric-elementary-accuracy-a-parametric-bound-can-instantiate.md) is `todo`; [`derive-the-tree-fold-form-of-the-online-softmax-rescaling-bound`](derive-the-tree-fold-form-of-the-online-softmax-rescaling-bound.md) is live. The first prerequisite — the rule itself — has no owner anywhere in the graph, verified by [the flash-class capability record](../docs/research/program-planning/flash-class-capability-set.md)'s axis 1.

**Fact — the shape is derived and the declaration layer is not.** [The certified-bounds record](../docs/research/numerics/certified-bounds-as-rewrite-permissions.md) Part 3 fixes the admission rule completely: a per-rule closed-form bound parametric in shape and target facts, instantiated by exact-rational arithmetic, compared against a caller-stated tolerance, answering `Admit` / `Refuse` / `Undecided` with `Undecided` fail-closed, sitting as a feasibility answer at semantic exploration in the same position as `require_elementary_accuracy`, and never as a cost. Five obligations are stated there and ADR 0101 decision 7 adds a sixth. What has no shape at all is layer 1: `OperationAlgebraicCapabilities` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/semantic/operation.rs`, `pub struct OperationAlgebraicCapabilities`) is a struct with one `bool` field, `ordered_associativity`, and a functional-equation capability would be the vocabulary's first **parameterized** law.

**Fact — the three-way decision machinery already exists one layer down.** `ConformanceDecision` (`crates/tiler-reference/src/accuracy.rs`, `pub enum ConformanceDecision`) is `Conforms` / `Violates` / `Undecided { reason }` and `decide_predicate` (`pub fn decide_predicate`) compares an `ExactRational` candidate against a `CertifiedEnclosure`. The rule should reuse it rather than grow a parallel one, and stating why it can or cannot is part of this ticket.

## What this ticket must produce

- **The capability spelling for a declared functional equation**, with its real-domain side condition, derived rather than sketched: what a parameterized law costs the operation-identity encoding, whether it is appends-only at each encoding site by per-tag injectivity reasoning, and what refuses a malformed or unsupported equation. State the identity-domain consequences without executing any of them.
- **The rule object**, worked on R1 (shifted-max rescaling) and R2 (the online normalizer fold) of the flash-class record's five-rule table: which dimensions each declares, what its bound is parametric in, and where the bound's side conditions come from.
- **Each of the six admission obligations discharged or refused explicitly** for the worked rule, including ADR 0101 decision 7's sixth — that the rewritten program's elementary arguments are proved to lie inside every accuracy clause's declared domain. The elementary-identity record's Part 4 already gives that discharge for the online form (the running maximum is non-decreasing and includes `x_j`, so both argument families stay non-positive); restate it as an obligation the rule performs, not as an observation a reader makes.
- **The refusal, emitted rather than described.** ADR 0101 decision 6 requires a rejection to name every missing dimension, the function, and the identity; the elementary-identity record's Part 7 quotes the exact wording as a checkable block. Derive where that string is produced and what carries it into the explain vocabulary, whose `ExplainDisposition::RejectedNumerical` already exists (`crates/tiler-compiler/src/explain.rs`, variant on `ExplainDisposition`; disposition arm maps `RejectionClass::NumericalIllegal` → `RejectedNumerical`).
- **A statement of what remains missing after this ticket**, so the reopening condition's readiness is a checkable fact rather than a judgement call.

## Non-goals

Admitting either permission; proposing an ADR; editing `docs/numerical-semantics.md` or any `docs/decisions/` record; implementing anything under `crates/`; stepping any identity domain; deriving the tree-fold bound (its own ticket owns it); deriving R4's output-accumulator rewrite, whose schedule half is a separate deferral.

## Closes when

The three layers ADR 0101 decision 3 names are each spelled with their obligations and their refusals, the six admission obligations are each discharged or explicitly refused for the worked rule, the refusal wording has a derived production site, and the record states which of ADR 0095's three reopening prerequisites remain open — with the answer traceable to a source read rather than to this ticket's own assertion.

## Outcome — 2026-08-06

**Why first: the rule object is not a rule plus a dimension set, because the dimension set is a function of the scheduled fold tree — and that question arrived with the tree-fold bound after this ticket was filed.** The deliverable is [`docs/research/numerics/online-softmax-rule-object.md`](../docs/research/numerics/online-softmax-rule-object.md), `disposition: pending`. It admits no permission, proposes no ADR, edits no contract or crate, steps no identity domain, and presumes neither accepted decision's outcome. R4 is untouched per the non-goals and neither bound is re-derived.

**Inference — the headline shape.** A rule declares an identity, a capability requirement, a shape parameter, a bound, a side-condition set, a three-way answer, and a **dimension function** `consumed(T)` over the scheduled merge tree `T`: `{distributivity, elementary-function identity}` when `T` is the canonical left-deep chain, and those two plus reassociation otherwise. Three alternatives are eliminated in the record's Part 3 — declare the union (over-names, and an over-named refusal is the same false inference ADR 0080 item 5 prevents, run backwards), split into two rules (relocates the shape dependence and buys nothing), declare the sequential set only (ADR 0101 decision 6's stated failure mode). The declaration's obligations are totality over the declared shape domain, refusal on an undeclared shape, and no assumed monotonicity across shapes.

**Inference — two findings the ticket did not anticipate.** First, **`(D, h2)` prices the rewrite and does not decide its dimensions**: `h2 = V-1` says the tree is a chain, not that it is the *canonical left-deep* chain, and only the canonical one avoids reassociation — so the schedule must hand the rule the tree. A dated note widens [`carry-the-rescale-depth-a-parametric-bound-instantiates-from`](carry-the-rescale-depth-a-parametric-bound-instantiates-from.md) accordingly; it stays `deferred`. Second, **R1 is not a rule object over the registered vocabulary** — there is no pair of `exp` occurrences to fold (`crates/tiler-ir/src/semantic/softmax.rs:6`), it is a step inside R2's bound, and declaring it separately would double-charge the same evaluations. Working R1 to that conclusion is how it is worked.

**Inference — the six obligations: five refuse, one discharges.** (1) side conditions **refuse** on the no-subnormal clause, on the operation's own registered `SOFTMAX_F32_FACT_SUBNORMALS` measurement, and **that refusal is independent of both permissions**; (2) `eps_exp` **refuses**, but the gap is narrower than filed — the requirement-side number is reachable and conservative, and what refuses is a ULP-to-relative conversion valid only above the subnormal band, i.e. conditional on obligation 1; (3) evidence identity **refuses** on the missing schedule field; (4) exact-rational comparison **discharges as a pattern**, and `ConformanceDecision` is deliberately *not* the type to reuse — `tiler-compiler` does not depend on `tiler-reference` and the two `Undecided`s have disjoint generators (enclosure width there, undischarged obligation here); (5) value precondition is **live rather than vacuous** and **refuses**, and is obligation 1 seen from ADR 0021's provenance side; (6) ADR 0101 decision 7's **discharges**, at all three fold shapes, restated as a per-site witness the rule produces from the fold's monotonicity invariant rather than as a reader's observation.

**Fact — the refusal's production site, read end to end.** A rule's `Vec<RewriteAssessment>` → `record_rewrite_assessment` (`crates/tiler-compiler/src/normalize.rs`, `fn record_rewrite_assessment`) → `ExplainEvent::Check { stage: NumericalLegality, assessment: Disproved(ReasonCode), rejection: NumericalIllegal }` → `ExplainDisposition::RejectedNumerical` (`crates/tiler-compiler/src/explain.rs`, disposition match arm on `RejectionClass::NumericalIllegal`). The elementary-identity record's Part 7 block is the **rendering** of one declined `Numerical` assessment per missing dimension, not a string in the tree; the channel already admits several assessments per class, and the function and identity fit `FactValue::Identity`. The one thing that cannot be carried is the price — `FactValue` has no rational variant — and that fires only under an admission.

**Inference — the readiness statement, derived from two `status:` lines and one merged record.** ADR 0095's second reopening condition has **not** fired. Prerequisite (3), the bound at a schedulable fold shape, is satisfied — `derive-the-tree-fold-form-of-the-online-softmax-rescaling-bound` is `done`. Prerequisite (1), a rule in the certified-bounds admission shape, is satisfied by this record in the sense the condition states, which asks for the object rather than a registered rule. **Prerequisite (2) is the one open item and it is owned:** [`expose-the-numeric-elementary-accuracy-a-parametric-bound-can-instantiate`](expose-the-numeric-elementary-accuracy-a-parametric-bound-can-instantiate.md) is `todo`.

**Filed, and one thing deliberately not filed.** [`derive-the-value-precondition-the-online-softmax-bound-needs-for-its-subnormal-clause`](derive-the-value-precondition-the-online-softmax-bound-needs-for-its-subnormal-clause.md) (`deferred`, trigger check log), [`carry-an-exact-rational-quantity-in-the-explain-fact-vocabulary`](carry-an-exact-rational-quantity-in-the-explain-fact-vocabulary.md) (`deferred`, trigger check log), [`correct-the-flash-class-rule-table-rows-this-record-refutes`](correct-the-flash-class-rule-table-rows-this-record-refutes.md) (`todo`, because the table is a filed probe's declared input). Dated notes added to `carry-the-rescale-depth-…` and `expose-the-numeric-…`. **Not filed:** whether the definition-projection version steps for a rendering the vocabulary admits and no definition uses — it is an identity-layer convention that arises only inside the change that declares the capability, and answering it here would be executing half an identity step in prose.
