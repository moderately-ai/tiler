# Spike the serial-sum canonical receipt spine

## Goal

A retained test-only spike carrying one serial-sum member through the complete canonical receipt join, including typed unavailability and tamper refusal, without deleting or weakening current conformance or cross-process evidence.

## Work

1. Re-read the complete serial-sum compiler, conformance, artifact publication, proof-sidecar, runtime, retained-record, and producer/consumer prototype paths before choosing the member.
2. Re-audit the serial, split, and tree populations and the exact four-versus-twelve contributor grouping obligations.
3. Implement only the smallest private spike representation accepted by the receipt-design dependency; reference owner identities rather than copying their fields.
4. Carry semantic program, numerical/reference authority, retained alternatives, selected plan, schedule/KIR/program, proof sidecar, artifact, compilation provenance, runtime route, terminal completion, environment, and exact grouping-specific comparison.
5. Retain a device/toolchain unavailable outcome in the same schema without converting it to pass.
6. Perturb the receipt subject independently at oracle, selected-plan, program/artifact, environment/freshness, and terminal-completion joins; retain exact failure output.
7. Measure runtime, retained bytes, canonical size, and maintenance burden.
8. Dual-write beside current assertions. Do not remove the independent process/file producer-consumer pair.

## Non-goals

- Do not generalize to every operation or create a public conformance API.
- Do not replace the reference oracle or the retiring host KIR simulators.
- Do not retire duplicate tests in the spike.

## Stop conditions

Stop if the accepted receipt model lacks an owner for a required field, if the reference and implementation cannot be independently perturbed, or if a public boundary is required.

## Acceptance

- One exact member produces a complete verifiable join and one typed unavailable observation.
- Every identity/freshness/completion perturbation fails for the intended reason with retained output.
- Existing evidence still runs unchanged beside the spike.
- Costs and unsupported cases are measured and the next implementation boundary is explicit.
