`settle-contraction-chain-distributivity-permission` derived that regrouping a tensor-contraction chain consumes **distributivity** — exchanging a product of a sum for a sum of products — which is independent of reassociation and operand permutation and which no permission in `docs/numerical-semantics.md` grants. That derivation follows from the contract's own definitions and is settled. This ticket owns one atomic product choice.

**Whether to admit a distributivity permission at all.** Admitting it makes
contraction-order exploration reachable under a relaxed contract. Declining it
makes contraction association a planning choice within one semantic
contraction rather than a logical rewrite over a chain. Declining is not a gap:
no admitted contract currently grants this freedom.

If admitted,
`decide-whether-distributivity-directions-share-one-permission` owns the
dependent question of whether factoring and expansion share a permission.

Both questions are downstream of Q-SEM-015's semantic half. The Milestone 6 framing in `docs/roadmap.md` already reserves two contraction choices for Tom — one keyed family versus fixed-arity keys, and whether a semantic contraction node may consume more than two operands — and this is a third that belongs in the same decision record. Note that the derivation holds under either answer to the multi-operand question, so this does not depend on it.

**Trigger:** the accepted decision that admits a tensor-contraction family, or any earlier proposal to register a numerical contract that permits reassociation. Until one of those, `docs/numerical-semantics.md` names the dimension, admits no permission for it, and rejects the rewrite explicitly.

Closing this needs an ADR in `docs/decisions/`, which is why this ticket claims `contracts/decisions` alongside `contracts/numerics`. **`contracts/navigation` was added on 2026-08-01 at landing**, because both catalog views in `docs/decisions/README.md` and the Q-SEM-015 entry in `docs/open-questions.md` map to it and both had to move; `docs/roadmap.md` maps to the same scope and was deliberately left to [`cite-adr-0095-in-the-milestone-6-distributivity-framing`](cite-adr-0095-in-the-milestone-6-distributivity-framing.md) on a file-level conflict with a live branch.

## Both trigger clauses fired, and the decision followed (2026-08-01)

**Fact — the trigger above fired on both of its clauses, and the ticket stayed `deferred` regardless.** The first clause, "the accepted decision that admits a tensor-contraction family": [`admit-the-contraction-semantic-profile`](admit-the-contraction-semantic-profile.md) is `done` and `StandardSemantics` registers `tiler::strict-tensor-contraction-f32@1` under [ADR 0087](../docs/decisions/0087-model-contraction-as-one-keyed-family-with-an-index-structure.md), recorded at `docs/roadmap.md:421`. The second, "any earlier proposal to register a numerical contract that permits reassociation": [`admit-a-reassociating-contract-without-contraction`](admit-a-reassociating-contract-without-contraction.md) is `done`. Meanwhile [`decide-whether-distributivity-directions-share-one-permission`](decide-whether-distributivity-directions-share-one-permission.md) sat `deferred` behind it, so a fired trigger held a dependent parked as well. Nothing owned noticing this; [`sweep-the-deferred-tickets-whose-reconsideration-triggers-have-fired`](sweep-the-deferred-tickets-whose-reconsideration-triggers-have-fired.md) now does, and carries this as its worked example.

**Decision — Tom declined, 2026-08-01, relayed by the coordinator.** No distributivity permission is admitted. `docs/numerical-semantics.md` continues to name the dimension, admit no permission for it, and reject contraction-chain regrouping explicitly — which, as the body above says, is a settled legality position rather than a gap: contraction association remains a planning choice within one semantic contraction rather than a logical rewrite over a chain.

**Reopening trigger:** the first workload whose natural spelling is a directly regroupable contraction chain — one where the regrouping that consumes distributivity is what the workload asks for, rather than one an optimizer might speculatively want. A workload that merely *contains* a contraction chain does not fire it; the chain must be one whose profitable form requires exchanging a product of a sum for a sum of products.

**Why this is `todo` rather than parked or `done`.** The decision is taken, so `awaiting-decision` would misreport this as still needing Tom — the misreport the workflow configuration's own note exists to prevent — and `deferred` is now simply false. The remaining work is dispatchable and specific: write the decline into an accepted ADR under `docs/decisions/`, correct the reservation sites that describe this as an open choice (`docs/open-questions.md:301` and the Milestone 6 framing in `docs/roadmap.md`), and close [`decide-whether-distributivity-directions-share-one-permission`](decide-whether-distributivity-directions-share-one-permission.md), whose question does not arise under a decline. It is not `done` until that record lands, because a decision recorded only in a ticket is not a decision recorded.

## Outcome — the decline is recorded as ADR 0095 (2026-08-01)

**[ADR 0095](../docs/decisions/0095-decline-a-distributivity-permission.md) — "Decline a distributivity permission and keep contraction-chain regrouping rejected", `decision_status: accepted`.** A decline is a decision and is recorded as `accepted`, not as a rejected proposal: what is accepted is that no permission is admitted. The record had no drafted body upstream of it — it was authored at the ADR from this ticket's dated decision section, [ADR 0080](../docs/decisions/0080-treat-distributivity-as-a-third-numerical-dimension.md) item 4's reservation, and [`settle-contraction-chain-distributivity-permission`](settle-contraction-chain-distributivity-permission.md)'s landed derivation — so no byte-identity claim applies to it and none is made. It `depends_on` ADRs 0011, 0014, 0015, 0080, and 0087, `applies_to` [Numerical semantics](../docs/numerical-semantics.md) alone, and supersedes nothing: ADR 0080 reserved this choice rather than making it, so answering the reservation contradicts no accepted decision. [The optimizer model](../docs/compiler/optimizer.md) and [fusion and scheduling](../docs/compiler/fusion-and-scheduling.md) are deliberately absent from `applies_to` because the decline falsifies no sentence in either — both already state the rewrite fails closed and cite ADR 0080 — and neither was touched.

**The full decision, in three clauses.** The dimension stays named and unpermissioned; the contraction-chain regroup stays explicitly rejected under every expressible contract, as a settled legality position rather than an unexplored one; and contraction ordering remains a planning question *within* one semantic contraction under [ADR 0087](../docs/decisions/0087-model-contraction-as-one-keyed-family-with-an-index-structure.md)'s keyed family, never a logical rewrite over a chain.

**One ground in the dispatch brief was overstated and is corrected in the record rather than repeated.** The brief gave the grounds as "no regroupable chain exists in the pinned workload — attention and MLP both interpose nonlinearities". The MLP half holds: [the operation and shape surface](../docs/research/shapes/transformer-operation-and-shape-surface.md) records SwiGLU as `down(silu(gate(x)) * up(x))`, so a SiLU and an elementwise multiply sit between its contractions and no regroup is statable. **The attention half does not hold as stated, checked by reading the operation table rather than inferred from the shape.** [The first attention program vertical](../docs/research/program-planning/first-attention-program-vertical.md) separates contractions 15 and 19 by a scale, a mask add, and a softmax — a nonlinearity, as claimed — but separates 19 and 21 by operation 20, which is a *structural* `Reindex` permute-and-merge. Composing 19 through 21 and factoring gives `sum_s probs · (sum_d v · W_o)` in place of `(sum_s probs · v) · W_o`, and that regroup does consume distributivity. The ground was therefore narrowed to what survives: a regroupable pair exists, the workload's natural spelling is the direct order, and the regroup is one an optimizer might speculatively want — precisely the case the reopening trigger is written to exclude, and the direction cost runs besides, since the factored form contracts `V` against a `W_o` slice per grouped head. The record states that negative case explicitly so the trigger has a worked example to be applied against rather than argued from.

**The reopening trigger is carried verbatim from the section above** into ADR 0095's own "Reopening trigger" section, together with the distinguishing test.

**Contract and navigation sweeps.** [Numerical semantics](../docs/numerical-semantics.md) carried the reservation in three places and all three moved: the canonical-policy paragraph's "adding a field here would convert a reserved question into an admitted permission" now says such a field would contradict a decision; the distributivity subsection's ADR 0080 citation now names ADR 0095 as the record that closed the reservation by declining; and the closing sentence, which read "It is reserved for the accepted decision that admits a tensor-contraction family", now states the decline, its grounds, its reopening trigger, and the dependent question's parked status. [Q-SEM-015](../docs/open-questions.md) listed three reserved choices with the distributivity admission as the third; two are now settled and the multi-operand question is the only one left. Both catalog views in [the decision index](../docs/decisions/README.md) carry the record — theme row in *Numerical operations* between ADR 0076 and ADR 0022 by title sort, chronology row after ADR 0094.

**`docs/roadmap.md` was deliberately not touched and its remainder is filed, not absorbed.** Two Milestone 6 passages still describe this as an open product choice — the contraction-order paragraph's closing sentence at `:282` and the "a third choice belongs in that same record but is framed elsewhere" sentence at `:304`. Both are `contracts/navigation`, which this branch holds, but the file is under concurrent edit by [`realize-the-strict-contraction-on-metal`](realize-the-strict-contraction-on-metal.md), whose worktree carries uncommitted changes to it. Editing it would have been a file-level collision on a live branch, so the correction is [`cite-adr-0095-in-the-milestone-6-distributivity-framing`](cite-adr-0095-in-the-milestone-6-distributivity-framing.md) rather than a silent omission.

**[`decide-whether-distributivity-directions-share-one-permission`](decide-whether-distributivity-directions-share-one-permission.md) stays `deferred` rather than closing**, which diverges from the plan in the section above and is stated rather than absorbed. Its opening line, "activate only if Tiler admits a distributivity permission", is now precise rather than conditional, and its activation condition is exactly ADR 0095's reopening trigger — it cannot fire on its own. `deferred` is parked, excluded from `ready`, and never satisfies a dependent, so it costs nothing to keep and preserves the conditional framing that [`sweep-the-deferred-tickets-whose-reconsideration-triggers-have-fired`](sweep-the-deferred-tickets-whose-reconsideration-triggers-have-fired.md) carries this pair as its worked example of. The reasoning is recorded on that ticket too, so a reader arriving from either end sees the same answer.
