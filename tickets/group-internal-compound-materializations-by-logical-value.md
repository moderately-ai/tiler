## User-visible outcome

A compound value produced inside a physical program remains one logical value across every internal materialization, stage access, allocation, artifact record, and lifetime decision. Its components can be scheduled and stored separately, but scale, code, zero-point, codebook, mask, or future roles can never be detached, regrouped, or confused with a same-shaped component of another logical value.

## Why this is separate

`prototype-quantized-value-vertical` implements complete role-addressed compound interface inputs and outputs and fails closed on internal compound materializations. The existing program IR has no producer-derived logical identity for internal values, so accepting internal roles there would preserve names while losing which components belong together. The selected quantized language-model profile is **not** an internal producer: its encoded weights are compound interface inputs and its executed program contains neither `Quantize` nor `Assemble`. The first named future production consumer is the dependent [`admit-strict-affine-quantize-physical-candidate`](admit-strict-affine-quantize-physical-candidate.md), which does not exist yet. Synthesizing grouping in the artifact layer would be a second semantic authority and is prohibited.

## Implementation keys

- After [`decide-the-producer-derived-logical-group-public-boundary`](decide-the-producer-derived-logical-group-public-boundary.md) is accepted, implement its exact program-owned logical-group association minted from a verified semantic producer/result relation, not a caller-supplied integer, resolved type, scheme, or role list. Do not assume the accepted spelling is `LogicalValueId`.
- Represent one internal logical materialization as its complete `ResolvedValueType`, logical shape, canonical ordered component set, and producer-derived identity. Component allocation, storage encoding, view, and lifetime remain physical facts attached below that group.
- Derive the required roles, component resolved types, and component shapes from the encoded value contract and parameter maps. Reject missing, duplicate, extra, swapped, wrong-type, and wrong-shape components before stage verification.
- Bind stage accesses and definitions to `(logical value, component role)` rather than a bare materialized buffer. A component from one logical value cannot discharge another's role even when every physical fact matches.
- Ensure program and artifact identity distinguish every result-affecting logical group, complete resolved type, producer relation, ordered role, component encoding, and access through the canonical grammar accepted by the decision dependency. Do not redundantly restate a field when an already-folded authoritative identity commits it. The artifact codec validates closure and never reconstructs grouping from slot order.
- Keep `BindingSpec` declaration-only. Artifact and runtime projections derive every logical/component target from the verified program.
- Preserve independent components through allocation/lifetime analysis while retaining the whole logical value through moves, views, outputs, and error reporting. Any alias or reuse across components needs an explicit ownership proof.
- Generalize over ordered component declarations; do not hard-code affine roles. Complex planar/interleaved storage, codebooks, hierarchical scales, masks/outliers, and future compound extensions must fit without modifying a universal three-field struct.
- Continue rejecting unsupported parameter maps and internal compound operations by exact scheme/type/capability until their real producers land.

## Closes when

The accepted generic producer-derived grouping boundary is implemented from the exact verified result relation through program construction, stage access/definition, allocation/lifetime analysis, artifact construction, codec round-trip, and decoded bindings. A checked internal compound result retains its complete resolved type, logical shape, ordered components, and producer relation while component storage remains independently allocated and live. Missing, duplicate, extra, swapped, wrong-type, wrong-shape, and cross-result components reject; identity changes for every result-affecting group, role, map, or encoding change; no caller-declared semantic or ABI fact and no slot-position inference is introduced. Every new check is perturbed by changing its subject and its failure is quoted; targeted formatting, package checks, tests, doctests, Clippy, rustdoc, `tkt lint`, `make citations`, `git diff --check`, the exact-base `tkt guard`, and one `make full` pass.

The real strict-affine `Quantize` producer, downstream consumer, reference agreement, and end-to-end executable proof remain the closure of [`admit-strict-affine-quantize-physical-candidate`](admit-strict-affine-quantize-physical-candidate.md). They are deliberately not duplicated here, because that ticket depends on this generic infrastructure and reversing the edge would create a cycle. This ticket's tests exercise the accepted proof-derived boundary without fabricating a caller-authored artifact identity; they do not claim a physical `Quantize` candidate exists.
