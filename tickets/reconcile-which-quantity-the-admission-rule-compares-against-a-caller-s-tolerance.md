## User-visible outcome

The certified-bounds record states one comparison semantics for the admission rule — what a caller's tolerance is a tolerance *on* — instead of two that disagree between its Part 2 Step 4 heading and its Part 3.

## The tension, verified on the tree at 24d7dab

**Fact.** [The certified-bounds record](../docs/research/numerics/certified-bounds-as-rewrite-permissions.md):143 heads Step 4 with "the price, which is the quantity a caller's tolerance is compared against" — the tolerance reads as a budget on the rewrite's *extra* cost over the baseline, so `P` is the compared quantity. **Fact.** The same record's Part 3 (:233) states "the admission rule is the same three-way exact-rational comparison applied to a rewrite's *bound* and a caller's tolerance" — the tolerance reads as an absolute relative-error budget, so `B_online` is the compared quantity. **Fact.** [The tree-fold record](../docs/research/numerics/tree-fold-online-softmax-bound.md) Part 5 and [`separate-the-rescaling-price-from-the-observed-fold-divergence`](separate-the-rescaling-price-from-the-observed-fold-divergence.md)'s outcome both endorse the Part 3 reading, calling `P` presentational — but neither owns the Step 4 heading, and the separation ticket's non-goals excluded relitigating the rule's shape, which is why this is a ticket rather than a wording fix inside it.

**Why it matters.** The two readings admit different rewrites at the same stated tolerance: Step 4's worked instantiation ("a caller stating `2^-13` admits, `2^-16` refuses" against `P = 6.09e-5`) only makes sense under the price reading, while Part 3's reuse of `decide_predicate` over "a rewrite's bound" only makes sense under the absolute reading. A vocabulary offered to callers must say which number their tolerance is.

## What this must produce

The derivation of which semantics the admission rule offers — price-relative, absolute, or both as distinct named tolerances — argued from what a caller can actually state and what the rule can soundly compare, with the losing reading's worked instantiation corrected wherever it appears (Step 4's heading and its worked instantiation are the known sites; sweep the record). If the answer resolves to a vocabulary surface, draft and park it for Tom under ADR 0075; never self-accept.

## Closes when

The record carries one comparison semantics stated where the derivation produces it, every worked instantiation in the record uses it, and the tree-fold record's Part 5 cross-reference still reads true against the landed wording.

## Outcome — 2026-08-06

**The answer is the price, and the losing reading was Part 3's — which is the opposite of what the weight of prose suggested.** Three sites said "bound" (this record's Part 3, [the tree-fold record's](../docs/research/numerics/tree-fold-online-softmax-bound.md) Part 5, and [`separate-the-rescaling-price-from-the-observed-fold-divergence`](separate-the-rescaling-price-from-the-observed-fold-divergence.md)'s premise) and one said price (Step 4's heading). None of the four carried a derivation; the tree-fold record and the separation ticket both inherited the reading from Part 3, and the separation ticket's own non-goals excluded deriving it. Count of sites is not evidence.

**Derived from the rule's jurisdiction rather than from the algebra**, landed at [the certified-bounds record's](../docs/research/numerics/certified-bounds-as-rewrite-permissions.md) Part 3 under *What quantity the rule compares, and why the other candidate belongs to a different authority*. Three grounds, in the order they eliminate:

1. **Unenforceable in its own terms.** The rule is a per-candidate feasibility answer that always retains the baseline. Under `B_1 <= tol`, a caller whose own unrewritten fold already exceeds `tol` has every rewrite refused and still ships a program violating the number they stated, because refusing the baseline is not among the things this rule can do. A number enforced against rewrites and unenforced against the program as written is not an accuracy guarantee.
2. **Instantiability, after its steelman.** `B_1` carries `exp(A*u)` in the logit spread and `P` does not. The steelman is real: obligation 1's no-subnormal clause already wants a spread bound, so a candidate that discharges the obligations does hand the rule an `A_max`. What does not survive is the consequence — under `B_1 <= tol` two candidates identical in shape, target, and rewrite get different verdicts at one stated tolerance whenever their proved spread bounds differ, so the verdict moves with a precondition proof about the *inputs*. `P`'s cancellation is an identity in `A`, so its guarantee holds uniformly over every admitted input. That uniformity is the soundness argument.
3. **The absolute number's type exists and is not this one.** It is [the region-accuracy contract's](../docs/research/numerics/region-accuracy-contract.md) `RegionAccuracyGoal` under a non-empty `DelegatedPermissions`, which that record leaves empty by design. Not a second reading of one rule — a second authority.

**And the ticket's own framing of the caller's problem is inverted, which is the crux.** The worry that a "how much worse than what I wrote" budget is relative to a baseline the caller may not know the bound of does not hold: because `1 + B_1 = (1 + B_2)(1 + P)` is an identity, the guarantee is stated, checked, and delivered without either party computing `B_2`. It is the *absolute* reading that needs knowledge the caller lacks — whether their own program already meets the number they stated.

**Strongest counterpoint, recorded beside the survivor rather than after it.** A price budget grants no accuracy guarantee at all: the caller is told only that the rewrite is at most a factor `1 + tol` worse *in bound* than a fold whose bound they were never told, and no experiment produces `P` — the observable quantity is the divergence, which `B_1 + B_2` bounds and `P` does not. A price is a *policy* number, never a *measurement* number. The answer is that the counterpoint asks the wrong authority, and what the price reading owes in exchange is two obligations now recorded: the vocabulary must not spell a price so it reads as an error budget, and a per-rewrite threshold is not a program-level budget.
