## Outcome

One accepted partitioned concatenate index region projects into the first canonical verifier-classified `RegionProgram::PartitionedCopy` region. It carries ordered operand members, distinct input bindings, one output, and exact source/destination subdomains while retaining zero-extent members and repeated operand occurrences.

## Strict construction

Re-derive the accepted index law rather than copying unchecked offsets. Prefix offsets use checked arithmetic. Member rectangles must be pairwise disjoint and jointly exhaustive; source and destination maps must refine their bounds; rank, dtype, axis, and result shape must agree exactly. Zero-extent members stay in identity and execute no access. Deduplicate boundary input bindings, never ordered members.

Keep output ownership and source-partition coverage as distinct proof obligations. Do not weaken `ContributorPartition`, global launch-tail rules, generic single-write rules, or the accepted index ownership law. No pointwise identity spelling, fabricated scalar operation, default numerical record, or multi-kernel fallback is admitted.

## Closes when

Arity 2 and 8, every axis position, unequal/all-zero members, operand reorder, and `concat(x, x)` are covered; overlap, gap, overflow, wrong member, and wrong prefix perturbations fail by distinct typed rules; and old scheduled-region bytes remain identical or the owning domain moves coherently.

## Exact-base dispatch audit — 2026-08-17 at `783e9b5b743afafdf4957396dbcfdb2f4c34565c`

The implementation is **not dispatch-ready**. The accepted dependency fixes one semantic topology — one concatenate occurrence becomes one scheduled copy region and ultimately one kernel — but it does not fix the exact public Rust representation, builder transition, diagnostic vocabulary, proof/member association, supported population, or schedule/request identity tags and framing.

Current source makes those choices consequential rather than mechanical: `IndexRegion` and `ScheduledRegionBuilder` require one `ScalarProgram` and one `NumericalRealization`; `ScheduledRegionDiagnostic` has no partitioned-copy-specific variants; schedule identity is `tiler.schedule.v6\0`; the compiler request subject is `tiler.compiler.request-subject.v6\0`; and no `RegionProgram` or `PartitionedCopyProgram` type exists in `crates/`. The accepted downstream numerical-state record deliberately used the sum spelling conceptually and said exact names follow the source audit.

[`decide-the-partitioned-copy-scheduled-region-public-surface`](decide-the-partitioned-copy-scheduled-region-public-surface.md) now owns the missing Tom decision and complete Pareto audit. This ticket remains blocked until that exact surface is accepted. Do not infer a generic copy population from the name, let callers assert proof authority, assign tags opportunistically, or implement only the narrow fixture while exposing a broader public construct.

## Implementation re-audit — 2026-08-18 at `02c44eef78239a73f11664ebcf76b1d90df82edc`

The accepted packet was written against a v6-era base; the elementary-numerical-dimensions merge `841c373e` landed between packet and implementation, so every load-bearing packet claim was re-read from source at this exact base before any edit. Per-claim verdicts, each against the file the claim names:

1. **Verified with drift — schedule domain.** `encode_identity` in `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/model.rs` opens `tiler.schedule.v7\0` (anchor `Why this is a v7 step`), not the packet's `v6`. The appended-tag byte-preservation argument was re-derived at `v7` and recorded on `TAG_REGION_PARTITIONED_COPY`: every field before the program position is framed and self-delimiting, every `v7`-encodable region writes `0x22`–`0x2A` there, so `0x2B` is a byte no earlier region can carry; within-arm recoverability follows from the fixed-width framed member run; equal meanings encode equally because derived quantities are never written and `partitioned-copy-source-order` pins one read order per meaning. The domain does not step.
2. **Verified with drift — ten numerical fields.** `NumericalRealization` and `ResourceRequirements` carry ten numerical dimensions (`reciprocal_transform: NumericalPermission` and `approximate_intrinsics: ApproximationEnvelope` beyond the packet's eight); `push_numerical` in both the schedule and kernel encoders destructures exhaustively (anchor `Destructured exhaustively rather than field-accessed`). The `FloatingPoint` arm of the accepted sum therefore carries ten fields, and both encoders' arm projections were proved byte-for-byte by the unmodified pins.
3. **Verified with drift — downstream domains.** The kernel identity domain is `tiler.kernel.v9\0` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/kernel/model.rs`, anchor `KERNEL_DOMAIN`), the kernel-program domain `tiler.kernel-program.v12`, the artifact program domain `tiler.artifact-program.v19` with `MANIFEST_SCHEMA = (19, 0)` (`crates/tiler-artifact/src/program/codec/encode.rs`) — not the packet's `v12`-as-kernel / schema `16.0`. Rederived at the current grammar: the `ResourceRequirements` sum forces **no** schema or domain step anywhere, because the wire and identity grammars carry exactly the ten flat floating-point rows every existing subject wrote, the `FloatingPoint` arm reproduces them byte-for-byte, and the `BitPreservingCopy` arm is a typed refusal (`unlowered-region-program` at the kernel encoder, `bit-preserving-copy-resources` wrapped in `ModelObligation` at the artifact encoders) unreachable while canonical lowering refuses the copy region.
4. **Verified — program-position tag.** Scalar-program tags run `0x22`–`0x2A` ending at `TAG_SCALAR_SQUARED_SUM_EPILOGUE`; no ticket or acceptance reserves `0x2B` or `0x2C`; `TAG_REGION_PARTITIONED_COPY = 0x2B` as packeted.
5. **False as packeted — access tag `0x0A`; reconciled to `0x0D`.** See the tag reconciliation below.
6. **Verified — request subject.** `canonical_explain_subject_bytes` opens `tiler.compiler.request-subject.v6\0`; the five arithmetic arm sub-tags are self-framing; the appended `partitioned-copy-f32.v1` framed sub-tag is reserved by the acceptance with its payload deferred to [`plan-concatenate-through-one-partitioned-copy-entry`](plan-concatenate-through-one-partitioned-copy-entry.md), so no `request.rs` code lands here — the recognizer and arm are the plan ticket's, and the domain does not step.
7. **Verified — binding tag `0x03`.** `ExecutionBinding::FixedVectorMap` holds schedule binding tag `0x03`; it is the one binding the shared gates admit over `ReductionTopology::None` besides the linear one, which makes the copy `Topology` rule's binding clause independently watchable (test below).
8. **Census, two packet omissions found and repaired here.** Beyond the review-completed migration table: `crates/tiler-compiler/src/component_cost.rs` reads `region.numerical.profile_key` for the memory-traffic bound (a copy region now answers `CostValue::Unknown` through the `Option` chain — the fail-closed cost answer), and the opaque-call numerical comparison in `crates/tiler-compiler/src/frontier.rs` (anchor `NumericalContractMismatch`) compares eight declared dimensions against the contract (a copy-armed declaration now mismatches by construction). Both were migrated arm-aware.
9. **False as packeted — the byte-level control.** The packet requires an arity-2 copy identity and the pinned strict-F32 identity to "first diverge at the program-position byte". No fixture can realize that against the pin: a copy read's access-map byte (the appended copy-source tag) is encoded **before** the program position, so the first divergence against the pinned bytes sits at the first read's map byte — index 53, `0x0d` versus `0x01`, proved by `against_the_pinned_strict_identity_the_copy_first_diverges_at_the_access_map_byte`. The load-bearing half of the packet's claim — cross-arm discrimination at the program position — is proved on equal prefixes instead: two regions identical in every byte before the program position first diverge exactly there, `0x2b` versus `0x24` (`the_copy_and_numerical_arms_first_diverge_at_the_program_position_byte`). This contradicts the packet's spelling, not its argument.

## Tag reconciliation — 2026-08-18

Three same-day accepted decisions assign logical-access identity tags, and the partitioned-copy packet's `0x0A` collides with an earlier reservation:

- [`decide-the-source-bound-live-row-major-access-surface`](decide-the-source-bound-live-row-major-access-surface.md) (accepted 2026-08-18, reviewed `c4522496`) takes **`0x0A`** for `LiveRowMajorSource` and **`0x0B`** for the fieldless `LiveRowMajor` consumer marker, retiring `0x09`. Not yet implemented at this base.
- [`decide-the-data-dependent-index-representation-public-surface`](decide-the-data-dependent-index-representation-public-surface.md) (accepted 2026-08-18, reviewed `a25f4268`) takes **`0x0C`** for `GatherSource`, and itself records that `0x0A`/`0x0B` "remain reserved by the earlier live-row-major decision packet … a gap is preferable to colliding reviewed identities".
- The partitioned-copy packet, drafted without visibility into those reservations, named `0x0A`. Taking it would put two reviewed identity assignments on one byte.

Resolution, derived from the encoder at this base (`0x01`–`0x09` written, `TAG_LIVE_ROW_MAJOR = 0x09` live) plus the two acceptance records: live-row-major keeps `0x0A`/`0x0B`, gather keeps `0x0C`, and **`TAG_PARTITIONED_COPY_SOURCE = 0x0D`**, the next value no accepted record claims. The injectivity argument is unchanged — `0x0A`–`0x0C` are reserved-and-unwritten at this base, so no encodable region carries any of them and a reader that reaches `0x0D` is reading an access the earlier vocabulary could not express. Recorded in full on the constant in `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/model.rs`. The program-position tag has no competing reservation and stays `0x2B` as accepted.
