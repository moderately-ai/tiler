## User-visible outcome

A cover region holding a tensor contraction beside another operation derives its fusion legality from a declared role instead of failing closed to `Unknown`, so the support matrix's R5 criterion is met for the family rather than skipped.

## Why this ticket exists

**Fact — the direct path reached R6 without R5's own criterion, and said so.** `realize-the-contraction-through-the-appendable-direct-path` carried a whole-program contraction from `compile()` to an emitted Metal entry point on 2026-08-01. It registered no `FusionOperationRole`: `FusionNumericalCapabilities::governed` in `crates/tiler-compiler/src/fusion_legality.rs` maps `constant-f32`, `multiply-f32`, `add-f32`, `silu-f32`, `strict-serial-sum-f32`, `rms-norm-f32`, `reindex-f32`, and `broadcast-f32`, and no contraction key.

**Fact — the omission is invisible today, and that is the reason it is filed rather than fixed there.** `crates/tiler-compiler/src/pipeline/planning.rs` skips `derive_fusion_legality` for any cover region with fewer than two members, and the recognized contraction shape is exactly one operation, so no reachable program consults a contraction's role. Registering one during that ticket would have been a declaration with no consumer and no evidence — the mirror image of the K-multiple refusal that ticket explicitly refused to ship.

**Inference — what makes it load-bearing is a second operation in the region, and two tickets bring one.** A pointwise epilogue fused into a contraction, or a prologue fused in, is a region the recognizer does not yet build; when it does, `classify` returning `None` is what stops the plan. `admit-reassociated-contraction-schedule-alternatives` and the epilogue work Milestone 6 frames are the consumers.

## Required delivery

- One declared role for `tiler::strict-tensor-contraction-f32@1`, derived rather than borrowed. `OrderedReduction` and `PrologueCarryingOrderedReduction` both describe a fold over *one* contributor domain; a contraction folds a product of two operands read at different coordinates, so state whether that is a third role or a widening of the second, and say which obligations move with the choice.
- The legality obligations the role carries, stated per obligation rather than inherited: what ADR 0015 contraction permission means for a region that fuses a multiply into this fold, what reassociation and permutation mean for it, and which of these the existing `ArithmeticContraction` obligation already decides.
- A fixture whose cover region actually holds two members including the contraction, so the role has a reachable consumer and the `Unknown` it replaces was observed first.

## Non-goals

The `tiled` realization; contraction-order exploration; GEMM recognition; layout-conversion costing; the multi-operand structure reserved behind ADR 0087's fifth rule.

## Closes when

A cover region containing a contraction and at least one other operation derives `FusionLegality::Legal` or a typed rejection instead of `Unknown`, the `Unknown` it replaces was watched firing first, and the support matrix's contraction row records R5 as met rather than skipped.
## Outcome

**Fact — the family no longer fails closed, and the role is a widening rather than a seventh variant.** `FusionNumericalCapabilities::governed` registers `tiler::strict-tensor-contraction-f32@1` under the existing `FusionOperationRole::PrologueCarryingOrderedReduction`, so a cover region holding a contraction beside another operation derives `FusionLegality::Legal` with all nine obligations discharged where it previously returned `Unknown` with obligation `OperationCapabilitiesResolved` and reason `"unsupported-operation-capability"`. The registration comment states the derivation in place rather than citing a record, and the role's own doc gained the paragraph that makes the widening a claim a reader can refute.

**Inference — the elimination, stated so it can be refuted rather than only agreed with.** The role is module-private and consulted in exactly six places: `is_arithmetic`, `is_reduction`, `is_value_source`, `is_coordinate_relation`, the closed match in `is_exact_governed_same_family_pointwise`, and `region_structure`'s counts. A contraction and an RMS normalization produce the same answer from all six — neither is arithmetic, a value source, or a coordinate relation; both are reductions; both fall to the closed proof's disqualifying arm; both count under `reductions` — so a seventh variant would derive no obligation differently and would be a distinction with no consumer. The ticket's framing question resolves against the split: the contraction's two-operand prologue is a property of the *access relation* (its operand maps drop different free iteration coordinates), and this authority discharges no access obligation — every obligation here is about a rounding, a fold order, a conversion boundary, or an exceptional value, and a per-contributor product of two loaded values commits the same separate rounding a per-contributor square does. The contributor domain is still one domain folded in one declared order, which is this role's actual contract. The three roles it is *not* each fail for a stated reason: `OrderedReduction` claims the whole operation is the fold, which the `K` separately rounded products refute; `ExtremumShiftedOrderedReduction` claims a second, permission-free pass, which this family's own `reassociation-permitted: false` withholds; `ElementwiseArithmetic` would make `is_reduction` false, discharge all four reduction obligations vacuously, and derive `Legal` under a reassociating contract that grants exactly the regrouping the family forbids — a silently wrong accept rather than a weaker answer.

**Fact — no identity moved, and none was owed.** No new `FusionOperationRole` variant and no fifth `FusionRegionStructure` count: the contraction counts under `reductions` beside the other two prologue-and-extremum roles, so the four counts still sum to `members` and no previously encodable region's content bytes move. A region holding a contraction had no encodable proof before this change, because it returned `Unknown` before `region_structure` ran. `GOVERNED_PROVIDER_REVISION` stays at 1 on the same reasoning the reindex, broadcast, softmax, and concatenate landings used: the revision is output-affecting, and adding a family changes no *existing* proof's bytes.

**Measurement — the pinned explain digest did not move, observed rather than inherited.** `cargo nextest run -p tiler-compiler -E 'test(deterministic_trace_is_sealed_and_rendered_separately)'` passes unchanged and `explain.rs:4149` still pins `"tiler-explain-v7 request=6dd42be71c6745fe"`. **Inference:** this matches the concatenate landing's reading — `ExplainWriter::new` folds the fusion provider identity, not the role table.

The absolute request digest moves for unrelated reasons; read the current pin from `crates/tiler-compiler/src/explain.rs` in `deterministic_trace_is_sealed_and_rendered_separately` rather than from this ticket. The substantive claim that *this* landing did not move the pin, and the mechanism Inference (provider identity folded, not the role table; `GOVERNED_PROVIDER_REVISION` unmoved by this role registration), still hold.

**Fact — the ADR 0015 obligation arm was decided, in the direction opposite to the concatenate's.** `is_exact_governed_same_family_pointwise` is closed over exact keys so each family is decided rather than inheriting a neighbour's answer, and its own comment anticipated "a future capability could classify another contraction-capable family". This family *is* that case: its per-contributor step `accumulator + a * b` is the multiply-plus-add adjacency the closed proof exists to rule out, so its key is admitted to no passing arm and it disqualifies the proof for its own reason rather than the fold's. The decision is corroborated by a sibling authority written independently: several admitted families declare the Contraction *dimension* (multiply/add via `ARITHMETIC`, rms-norm via `NORMALIZATION`, contraction via `TENSOR_CONTRACTION`); what is distinctive is that this family's *per-contributor step* is itself the multiply-plus-add adjacency, so the closed same-family-pointwise proof must disqualify it rather than admit it. The consequence is stated rather than hidden — under `RELAXED_F32`, the one governed contract permitting ADR 0015 contraction, a contraction-bearing region is `Unknown` with reason `"unrealized-contraction"`, because a fused body would be free to emit the fused multiply-add this family's facts forbid and nothing in the bounded profile proves it will not. It is an unknown and not a rejection because a realization carrying `-ffp-contract=off` through to the emitted text satisfies the obligation, so no violation is proved. Under every contract that forbids contraction the obligation discharges by normative guarantee and the region is legal.

**Measurement — both perturbations were watched failing, on this branch's tree with one edit reverted at a time.** Withdrawing the registration (`roles.insert` removed) failed all four new tests; the two region tests reported `Unknown(FusionUnknown { obligation: OperationCapabilitiesResolved, reason: "unsupported-operation-capability", ... })` where `Legal` is asserted, which is the `Unknown` this ticket replaces, observed before it was removed. Admitting the contraction key to a passing arm — the counterfactual in which the arm decision had gone the other way — failed exactly one test, at the arm assertion: `left: Discharged`, `right: Unknown { reason: "unrealized-contraction" }`. Under the governed contract that second perturbation is invisible, which is why it is stated against `StrictF32NumericalContract::governed_relaxed()` and the test asserts that contract does not forbid contraction before relying on it.

**Fact — two reachable consumers, not one.** `gated_projection_program` is a two-member region — the projection `td,od->to` followed by an elementwise gate — which is the ticket's stated fixture and derives `Legal` with nine discharged obligations, the two order obligations carrying `NormativeGuarantee` rather than the vacuous structural class a non-reducing region would produce. `attention_scores_program` is the three-member `Contraction -> Softmax -> Contraction` shape that [flash-class capability set](../docs/research/program-planning/flash-class-capability-set.md)'s axis 2 names as the case that makes the omission fire; it derives `Legal` end to end, and its perturbation withdraws the contraction's and the softmax's roles one at a time so the positive result belongs to this ticket's entry rather than the softmax's.

**Fact — the matrix rung moved, and two stale doc claims were corrected in place.** The contraction row reads `R6 for a whole-program contraction occurrence, with R5 met for the F32 family`, the R5 paragraph carries the elimination and the arm decision, and the trigger column repoints from this ticket to the request-boundary wall that still keeps a fused contraction region from reaching a *plan*. Milestone 6's framing paragraph said "nothing would fuse" over a three-role registry and inferred a contraction would resolve to `Unknown`; it is corrected in tense with its original inference preserved. `FusionRegionStructure::reductions` said "both reduction roles", which the softmax landing had already made false; it now names all three.
