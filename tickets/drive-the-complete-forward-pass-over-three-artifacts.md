## User-visible outcome

Token IDs go in and logits come out: one forward pass runs as 30 executions of 3 compiled artifacts, on one device, in order, against retained tensors the consumer binds and rebinds as ordinary program inputs and outputs.

## Required behaviour

From [the L6 record](../docs/research/program-planning/complete-model-ingestion-and-execution.md).

- **The partition.** One embedding execution, 28 layer executions, one head execution. **Thirty executions per pass is unconditional.** The 28 layer executions of one pass with fixed `T` are the **same artifact identity** bound to 28 different weight sets; a build producing a fourth identity within that pass has specialized on a binding. **Exactly three artifact identities across prefill and decode together is conditional on L6 D-19** ([`define-the-widening-relation-over-a-symbolic-broadcast-extent`](define-the-widening-relation-over-a-symbolic-broadcast-extent.md)) and the three properties named in the 2026-08-05 Whole-model composition correction under [`decide-whether-one-decoder-layer-graph-can-serve-prefill-and-decode`](decide-whether-one-decoder-layer-graph-can-serve-prefill-and-decode.md) (sourced shapes through inference, symbolic broadcast attributes, and the degenerate widening definition). Until that condition holds, report the observed identity count with attribution (the `T = 1` graph divergence versus an `S` specialization) rather than treating a count other than three as a gate failure — matching [L8's regression-policy conditional pin](../docs/research/program-planning/model-level-qualification.md).
- **The layer count is a driver loop bound.** No artifact carries it, and a checkpoint with a different `num_hidden_layers` reuses the layer artifact unchanged.
- **Model-level preflight comes before the first routing commit.** The decision to run compiled or to decline is taken once, over the bound facts of all 30 executions, at the tensor-level preflight [Candle integration](../docs/integration/candle.md) already places there. It is not a held `Preflight`: `DecodedProgram::preflight` takes `&mut self` and the returned borrow carries into the commit, so 28 simultaneous preflights of the layer program are not expressible, and the check that is expressible is over the values and extents about to be bound.
- **After the first commit no execution of that pass may fall back.** A pass in which one execution fell back while another had committed would compose two numerical contracts and report one — the misattribution the Candle contract's numerical-scope section describes — so it refuses, naming both ordinals.
- **One ordered stream, one completion observation per token.** Under ADR 0047's initial profile the 28 layer dispatches share one ordered stream, and the host observes terminal success once, for the logits. *Corrected 2026-08-04 under [`supersede-the-runtime-owned-kv-state-design`](supersede-the-runtime-owned-kv-state-design.md):* the driver — not Tiler — advances its cursor and swaps all 56 retained tensors on that same observation, together or not at all, so no reader of its state observes a partially advanced model.
- **A post-commit failure names the execution ordinal and the token in flight, makes that execution's outputs unobservable, and never selects an alternative.** *Corrected 2026-08-04:* this bullet read "poisons the model state". Tiler holds no model state to poison; the driver owes the refusal to continue from its pre-failure tensors, because the failed step's token was never produced. This ticket's ordinal obligation is **call-site pairing**: the driver already knows which of its thirty invocations failed and pairs that ordinal (and token in flight) with Tiler's typed reason. The five-class consumer diagnostic surface that composes ordinal, phase, and token into the full report is owned by [`name-the-execution-ordinal-in-model-level-failures`](name-the-execution-ordinal-in-model-level-failures.md); both tickets must not claim exclusive delivery of "reports its ordinal".

**Correction — 2026-08-10.** Required behaviour bullet 1 and Closes when previously treated "exactly 3 artifact identities" as an unconditional gate for one prefill and one decode step of C1. That omitted L6's 2026-08-05 Whole-model composition correction (under today's fixed-extent vocabulary each distinct `(T, C, S)` of C1 is its own graph identity — thirteen, not three — and three identities require D-19 plus properties (a)(b)(c)) and L8's regression-policy pin that identity and pipeline counts are pinned **conditionally** on L6's D-19. Thirty executions per pass stays unconditional. Scopes move from `implementation/runtime` and `implementation/frontend` to `implementation/candle`, matching L6's placement of the first complete-model driver beside the Candle prototype and the peer ordinal ticket's 2026-08-09 scope correction.

## Closes when

One prefill pass and one decode step of the C1 row each run as 30 executions; the artifact-identity count across those passes is **exactly 3 when L6 D-19 and properties (a)(b)(c) hold**, and until then is reported with attribution rather than gated as three; a deliberately mismatched weight refuses at bind with its interface key before any route; a deliberately refused variant at execution 17 declines the whole pass before the first commit; and a forced post-commit failure is paired at the call site with its ordinal and produces no observable outputs, after which the driver refuses to continue from its pre-failure tensors rather than producing a plausible next token. Full five-class ordinal/phase/token report composition remains [`name-the-execution-ordinal-in-model-level-failures`](name-the-execution-ordinal-in-model-level-failures.md).
