## What L1 handed over, and what this record adds

**Fact.** L1's traced surface names the per-layer computation at the pinned `transformers` v4.51.0 reference, the six weight shape classes, the nine contractions per layer plus the vocabulary projection, the per-head q/k RMSNorm, the half-split RoPE form, the 8→16 GQA repetition, the SwiGLU MLP, the softmax over the key axis, the tied-embedding gather, and the two varying extents `T` and `S`. Every statement in this record about what the reference computes is that trace's, not a fresh reading of the reference; nothing here asserts a new fact about `modeling_qwen3.py`.

**Inference — the trace is not the derivation, and the gap is the whole of this rung.** A list of family names does not say which names are one semantic operation and which are a shape of graph; it does not say which requirements Tiler can already express; and it does not distinguish a family that needs a new key from one that needs only a key that other work already owes. This record answers those three questions and turns the residue into capability tickets.

## The causal mask is a feasibility crossover, not a convenience

**Inference.** Two routes exist and they fail at different lengths.

Supplying the additive mask as an F32 program input of shape `[T, S]` needs no family that is not already required: it is one `Broadcast` over the head axes and one `Add`. It costs `T × S × 4` bytes of materialized input, which is 720 bytes at C1 and **268,435,456 bytes at B1-d** (`T = S = 8192` at prefill). L1 records that a materialized-score prefill already reaches 4.00 GiB at that row; a quarter-gibibyte mask on top of it is not the binding term, but it is a transfer and a residency cost that exists only because the mask cannot be derived.

Deriving the mask from iteration coordinates needs a comparison over index expressions and a selection between two values. Neither exists. The index-domain predicate vocabulary [ADR 0084](../../decisions/0084-reference-canonical-index-expressions-from-domain-predicates.md) fixes is the two atoms `NonNegative` and `LessThanExtent`, and the ADR states that it "contains no arbitrary Boolean composition, provider-defined atom, physical guard, runtime check, or proof result" — [IR](../../ir.md) renders the same rule as "no Boolean escape hatch". The support matrix places a tensor `Select` at R1 with no key, and its own trigger records that closure "needs an admitted predicate value type, which does not exist because the registry admits no boolean dtype". `crates/tiler-ir/src/kernel/model.rs` carries `CompareOp` in the structured-kernel vocabulary but no selection construct over F32 values.

**Inference — the two routes are not a preference.** The input route is correct and expressible at every row this workload declares, so nothing is blocked today. It is a materialization that grows as `T × S` against a program whose other terms grow as `T` or `S`, which means the crossover is real and reachable, not hypothetical. That makes the predicate-and-select family a tracked capability with a stated activation trigger rather than an omission. It is not filed here: L3′ owns masking by name, and this crossover is the evidence handed to it below.

## Typed refusals

Each unsupported case below must reject with a typed, explainable diagnostic rather than approximate. They are stated as refusals because the ticket requires it and because every one of them is a place where a silent approximation would return a plausible tensor.

- An extent symbol with no proved upper bound refuses rather than compiling a generic program. `ExtentInterval::states_no_upper_bound` already exists for exactly this, and its doc comment records that the remedy "is to state a constraint rather than to retry".
- A context length beyond the checkpoint's declared 32,768 positions refuses. The tokenizer's `model_max_length: 131072` is not the position budget, and L1 records the disagreement rather than resolving it silently.
- A contraction occurrence with no resolvable index-access lowering capability refuses at resolution, which is the current behaviour and must stay the current behaviour: `resolve_lowering` is on the ordinary `compile()` path and a program whose occurrences no installed capability covers fails closed.
- A malformed contraction index structure refuses at construction, by each of the five named structural rules separately, so the diagnostic says which rule and not merely that the structure is invalid.
- A token ID outside `0..151936` refuses or is validated at a named enforcement boundary; it never reads out of bounds and never clamps.
- A reduction whose contributor order, reassociation, or permutation permission is not established refuses rather than selecting a faster tree. [Reduction semantics and legality](../numerics/reduction-semantics-and-legality.md) fixes that permission is necessary and never sufficient, and that the reducer's own algebraic capability is the second half.
- A target that does not honour the effective numerical realization refuses. ADR 0076 forbids any authority from narrowing, weakening, or substituting the caller's stated contract to make a plan feasible, and the qualified Apple9/F32 row's subnormal flush is a declared realization rather than a tolerance.
