## How to read this record: four maturity claims, kept apart per capability

AGENTS.md holds a type-system reservation, an architectural seam, implemented support, and a tested guarantee to be four different claims. Every axis below states which one each capability has reached, because the difference is what separates reachable work from work that looks reachable:

- **Seam** — a place in the architecture where the capability would attach, with the dependency direction understood, and nothing spelled.
- **Reservation** — a name, variant, field, or refusal exists so the capability's absence is stated rather than silent.
- **Implemented** — the construct compiles and the compile path reaches it.
- **Tested** — a case that must fail has been watched failing.

## Axis 1 — Rewrite and permission vocabulary

**The capability.** An operation declares, once and in its own identity, the local algebraic identity it satisfies; a rule consuming that identity declares which numerical dimensions it spends and carries the derived bound that prices them; the contract independently resolves whether the caller granted those dimensions. The global algorithm then emerges from search over fusion, materialization, schedule, and declared rewrites — the compiler never invents algebra.

**Fact — the dimension vocabulary is complete for this rewrite, and that is new since the ticket was filed.** `CANONICAL_DIMENSIONS` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/numerics.rs:149`) declares eleven dimensions; [numerical semantics](../../numerical-semantics.md#distributivity-is-outside-the-order-contract) names distributivity as a twelfth and [elementary-function identity](../../numerical-semantics.md#elementary-function-identity-is-a-fourth-dimension) as a thirteenth, both normative and both unpermissioned. ADR 0101 was accepted on 2026-08-06 and its acceptance sweep moved the contract sentence. **So there is no longer an unnamed freedom in this rewrite.** Both freedoms it consumes are named, both refusals are specified, and the registered fact that previously mis-authorized the rewrite has been corrected: `SOFTMAX_F32_FACT_ONLINE_SINGLE_PASS_FORM` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/semantic/softmax.rs:602`) now registers `not-a-reassociation-of-the-sum-but-a-horner-nesting-consuming-distributivity-…-and-the-subordinate-exponentials-elementary-function-identity-…`.

**Fact — the declaration layer does not exist, and its shape is undesigned.** `OperationAlgebraicCapabilities` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/semantic/operation.rs:922`) is a struct with exactly one field, `ordered_associativity: bool`. ADR 0101 decision 3 places the per-function content in an "operation-owned identity-encoded capability" declaring a functional *equation* with its real-domain side condition — which would be the vocabulary's first **parameterized** law where the one existing law is a flag.

**Fact — the admission-rule layer is derived but unbuilt.** [The certified-bounds record's](../numerics/certified-bounds-as-rewrite-permissions.md) Part 3 fixes the shape completely: a per-rule closed-form bound, parametric in shape and target facts, instantiated by exact-rational arithmetic, compared against a caller-stated tolerance, answering `Admit`/`Refuse`/`Undecided` with `Undecided` fail-closed, sitting as a feasibility answer at semantic exploration in the same position as `require_elementary_accuracy`, and **never** as a cost. Five validation obligations are stated there; ADR 0101 decision 7 adds a sixth. **Fact — the three-way machinery the rule would reuse is implemented and is not a reservation:** `ConformanceDecision` (`crates/tiler-reference/src/accuracy.rs:735`) is `Conforms`/`Violates`/`Undecided { reason }` and `decide_predicate` (`:772`) compares an `ExactRational` candidate against a `CertifiedEnclosure`.

**Fact — how the dimensions compose with ADR 0095 is settled and is a conjunction.** [The elementary-identity record's](../numerics/elementary-identity-rewrite-dimension.md) Part 6 gives the four-outcome table, and only the both-admitted corner makes the fold reachable. ADR 0095's reaffirmation replaced its "no caller exists" ground with the narrowed form — *no caller can spend a distributivity permission admitted alone* — and added the joint reopening condition: the two permissions are considered together **when a rewrite rule with a derived bound instantiable at a schedulable fold shape is ready to consume them**, spelled as three concrete prerequisites.

**Fact — the evidence classes that prove a declared identity sound are already distinguished, at two layers.** `FusionEvidenceClass` (`crates/tiler-compiler/src/fusion_legality.rs:84`) carries `NormativeGuarantee`, `SoundProof`, `ExhaustiveFinite`, `Empirical`, and `Unknown` as five never-collapsed variants, with the last three documented as *reserved* so a future finite-domain or measured discharge declares itself honestly. The certified-bounds derivation is `sound-proof` within its stated model and is checked — not established — by a 22-case bounded measurement. **Inference — what refuses an unsound declaration is `Undecided`, and it is the same object in both layers:** any obligation that cannot be discharged yields a non-admission, and the baseline alternative is retained regardless, so a refusal costs a candidate and never costs correctness.

**What is missing, and it is exactly one of ADR 0095's three named prerequisites.** Prerequisite (2) is owned by [`expose-the-numeric-elementary-accuracy-a-parametric-bound-can-instantiate`](../../../tickets/expose-the-numeric-elementary-accuracy-a-parametric-bound-can-instantiate.md) (`todo`). Prerequisite (3) is owned by [`derive-the-tree-fold-form-of-the-online-softmax-rescaling-bound`](../../../tickets/derive-the-tree-fold-form-of-the-online-softmax-rescaling-bound.md) (`in-progress`). **Prerequisite (1) — a rule in the certified-bounds admission shape — has no owner anywhere in the graph.** Deriving it is not an admission and presumes no decision: it produces the object whose *existence* the standing reopening condition names, and under a continued decline it is the thing that makes the refusal checkable rather than described.

*Corrected 2026-08-06:* **both statuses moved and prerequisite (1) is owned and delivered.** `grep -m1 '^status:' tickets/derive-the-tree-fold-form-of-the-online-softmax-rescaling-bound.md` and the same command against `tickets/derive-the-rewrite-rule-declaration-and-admission-shape-for-the-online-softmax-fold.md` both return `status: done`; the first landed [the tree-fold record](../numerics/tree-fold-online-softmax-bound.md) and the second [the rule-object record](../numerics/online-softmax-rule-object.md), which derives the declaration, the dimension function, the bound it carries, the shape parameter, the six admission obligations with a verdict each, and the refusal's production site. ## Axis 2 — Fusion and materialization search

**The capability.** The cover enumeration must contain a candidate in which the quadratic score matrix is never a whole value, that candidate must be provably legal to implement as one kernel, and the caller's program must reach the enumeration at all.

**Fact — the no-`S` candidate is already enumerated, and it is already preferred.** `crates/tiler-compiler/src/cover.rs` enumerates materialization per edge — "for each (produced value, consuming region) pair the search enumerates both answers: read the value across the boundary, or recompute its producer inside the consumer" (module header) — and retains both the fully-materialized and the fused covers unconditionally, neither losable to a budget. `derive_cover_cost` (`:2166`) charges `materialized_elements` only for cross-region edges and `recomputed_elements` only for a member's *second and later* occurrences in canonical order. **Inference — in this chain neither charge lands on the fused cover.** `S` is consumed only by the softmax and `P` only by the second contraction, so absorbing both producers repeats no member; the fused cover is `(region_count 1, materialization_count 0, materialized_elements 0, recomputed_elements 0)` and strictly dominates every partition under `CoverCost::dominates` (`:624`). `CoverEnumeration::non_dominated` (`:802`) is documented as "a pure view. It prunes nothing" — so the preference is visible in explain without removing the alternative, which is the invariant the optimizer contract requires.

**Fact — what actually bounds the axis is one missing fusion role and one request-boundary wall.** `FusionNumericalCapabilities::governed` (`crates/tiler-compiler/src/fusion_legality.rs:270`) registers ten families — constant, multiply, add, silu, strict-serial-sum, rms-norm, softmax, reindex, broadcast, concatenate — and no contraction; `classify` returns `Option`, and a family with no role yields no legality at all. **Fact — the omission is currently unobservable**, because `crates/tiler-compiler/src/pipeline/planning.rs:115` skips `derive_fusion_legality` for any cover region with fewer than two members. **Inference — a flash-shaped region has three members, so it is precisely the case that makes the omission fire.** The epilogue wall sits earlier still, at the request boundary. **Corrected 2026-08-06, hours after this record landed — the missing role is delivered and this axis's "seam" rating is behind the board.** [`admit-a-fusion-role-for-the-tensor-contraction`](../../../tickets/admit-a-fusion-role-for-the-tensor-contraction.md) registered the contraction under the existing `PrologueCarryingOrderedReduction` role (a widening, not a third role — the six consultation sites answer identically for a contraction and a normalization), decided the closed same-family-pointwise arm *against* admission because the per-contributor `accumulator + a * b` is the multiply-add adjacency that proof exists to rule out, and derived `Legal` end to end for the three-member `Contraction → Softmax → Contraction` region this record names as the firing case. The epilogue wall now bounds the axis alone, and it too moved: its ticket is `blocked` on [`admit-a-materialized-intermediate-read-in-the-scheduled-region-vocabulary`](../../../tickets/admit-a-materialized-intermediate-read-in-the-scheduled-region-vocabulary.md), the scheduled-region access-contract widening its own discovery stop filed.

**Fact — the softmax's role is registered and its shape is the one this axis needs.** `FusionOperationRole::ExtremumShiftedOrderedReduction` (`:202`) exists specifically because the softmax carries *two* folds with different order obligations, and its doc records why collapsing it into `PrologueCarryingOrderedReduction` would be "wrong in one direction … and wrong in the other". That distinction is what keeps a permission covering the sum from silently licensing the maximum — the exact confusion a flash rewrite would otherwise exploit.

**What is missing.** Nothing that lacks an owner. The three walls are [`admit-elementwise-epilogues-over-a-materialized-intermediate`](../../../tickets/admit-elementwise-epilogues-over-a-materialized-intermediate.md) (`todo`, p2 — its own 2026-08-05 correction records that discharging it flips one of the compiler-facade gate's two named open bounds), [`admit-a-fusion-role-for-the-tensor-contraction`](../../../tickets/admit-a-fusion-role-for-the-tensor-contraction.md) (`todo`, p2, whose required delivery already asks whether a contraction is a third role or a widening of the second), and [`plan-the-materialized-attention-decomposition`](../../../tickets/plan-the-materialized-attention-decomposition.md) (`todo`, p1) with its recomputing sibling.

**Maturity.** Per-edge materialization enumeration: **implemented and tested** — `cover.rs`'s own tests watch a materializing cover dominate a partial recomputation and watch a materializing and an absorbing cover come out incomparable. Contraction fusion role: **seam** — the registry is a checked lookup that grows one family at a time, so the attachment point is exact and nothing occupies it. Three-member flash region: **not reachable**, blocked first at the request boundary.

**Outcome — a contract-relevant correction, carried by no new ticket.** The ticket's premise that the epilogue wall and the fusion-role gap "bound it first" is confirmed; the premise that the cover enumeration might not *contain* the no-`S` candidate is refuted at source. The correction is recorded here and reported to the coordinator for the two owning tickets' dispatch briefs; it files nothing, because both walls already have owners and neither ticket's stated outcome changes.

## Axis 3 — Schedule composition

**The capability.** A schedule that streams key/value blocks through one workgroup, staging a blocked operand tile, carrying `(m, d)` and an output accumulator across rounds, over a context axis whose extent grows.

## Axis 4 — Cost authority and calibration

**The capability.** Something that can prefer one implementation of the flash-shaped cover over another, on evidence, without ever converting a hard feasibility refusal into a large number.

**Fact — four cost models exist, and the ticket's "no cost model exists anywhere" premise is stale in a way its own 2026-08-06 correction only half repairs.** That correction names `PhysicalCostEstimate` alone. Read at this base there are four, each with its own governed key and each deliberately incomparable with the others:

| Model | Key | Dimensions | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CoverCost` (`cover.rs:581`) | `tiler.cost.partition-structural.v1` (`:85`) | region count, materialization count, materialized elements, recomputed elements | ranks *partitionings* before any implementation exists |
| `PhysicalCostEstimate` (`frontier.rs:345`) | `tiler.cost.structural.v1` (`:100`) | dispatch count, launched threads, temporary bytes | prunes strictly dominated proposals from one region's local frontier |
| `PlanStructuralCost` (`selection.rs:132`) | `tiler.cost.structural.v1` | the three above plus materialization count | the sole pruning input over complete plans |
| `AnalyticalPlanCost` (`component_cost.rs:313`) | `tiler.cost.analytical.v1` (`:76`) | nine governed components with units | **reported and never pruned on** |

**Fact — the separation is enforced in three places and is not a convention.** `component_cost.rs:9-24` records them: the frontier rejects a proposal whose estimate is not `tiler.cost.structural.v1`, `aggregate_cost` refuses a plan whose regions mix keys, and every `dominates` returns `false` across differing keys — "plans carrying different keys never dominate each other, so the non-dominated set would silently become the whole set and Pareto pruning would go dark with nothing reporting that it had."

**Inference — so the gap is neither vocabulary nor a missing axis; it is that every one of the four is a partial order.** Two implementations of one cover that trade dispatch count against temporary bytes are *incomparable by construction*, and incomparability is the designed outcome rather than a defect. Selecting between them needs either a total order (which would require calibration, since a total order over incommensurable units is a claim about a device) or a caller-stated preference. **Fact — the axis a flash decision would win on is present but unmodelled:** `CostComponent::ResourcePressure` and `CostComponent::CompileTime` share one match arm evaluating to `CostValue::Unknown` for every plan (`component_cost.rs:619`), and `Unknown` is pinned as neither zero nor known by that module's own test.

**Fact — the smallest credible cost model is the one already chosen, and it is calibrated rather than replaced.** [The bootstrap cost model](../cost-model/bootstrap-cost-model.md) is `disposition: adopted` and [the cost-model contract](../../compiler/cost-model.md) is `contract_status: accepted` with `implementation_status: spike-only`. Seven of the nine analytical components are computed from values a plan already carries; `MemoryTraffic` is the one `Bounded` component, with a low bound of owning writes only and a high bound of every access, because the plan does not model cache reuse.

**Inference — the ticket asks where simulation sits, and the elimination resolves against it.** A memory-traffic simulator's only product would be narrowing that `Bounded` interval. Against that: the interval is already *sound*, so a simulator buys precision rather than correctness; it would be an uncalibrated model of an Apple cache hierarchy no primary specification documents, so its own error would be unbounded and unfalsifiable; and `calibrate-device-cost-models` already owns fitting parameters against held-out device measurement on the two host rows this project owns, which is the same precision obtained from evidence instead of from a model. **A simulator is eliminated, not deferred**, and what would refute the elimination is a stated one: a candidate pair whose choice turns on the traffic interval's width *and* that no measurement on either host row can separate.

**Inference — the ticket also asks where a quantitative target row lives, and that eliminates too.** `CapabilityAxis` (`crates/tiler-compiler/src/target/feasibility.rs:211`) has seven variants — grid-axis threads, workgroup threads, buffer bindings, device address space, local memory bytes, u64 index arithmetic, device address width — and every one is a *hard bound*. Silence about an axis is `Unknown`, which never reaches an executable frontier. **A bandwidth or clock number is a cost input, so placing it on a target profile would make silence about it render a profile unexecutable for a quantity no feasibility predicate reads** — exactly the wrong failure direction. It belongs in the cost model's calibration record, which `calibrate-device-cost-models` already owns.

**Fact — what `calibrate-and-activate-parallel-reduction-selection` measured, updated 2026-08-07.** Its 2026-08-02 outcome was the explicit-negative branch, because the superseded four-thread grid-axis row forced the comparable domain to the single shape `rows=1, contributors=4` and "a fit through one point is not a model". Selection is unchanged, because consulting that model needs a target profile to declare its one decision-bearing parameter, which is the public boundary and identity move `activate-measured-reduction-selection-from-a-target-cost-row` carries. **That parameter is a cost input, so the elimination in the paragraph above applies to it too** — it is the same "silence would render a profile unexecutable" trap, and the successor ticket has to answer it rather than inherit a hard-bound axis. **Inference — a flash-shaped decision needs strictly more than that work delivers.** Its subject is one region's reduction topology under a grid-axis bound; a flash decision is a whole-program comparison between two implementations of one cover across several regions' worth of work, and it turns on occupancy — the component that is `Unknown` for every plan.

**Fact — that is a trigger evaluation, not an opinion.** [`calibrate-device-cost-models`](../../../tickets/calibrate-device-cost-models.md) is `deferred` behind three triggers, of which trigger 2 is "a plan choice turns on an `Unknown` component". Its 2026-08-04 log entry records triggers 1 and 2 unfired because both `Unknown` components are constant across candidates. **Inference — a flash-versus-naive choice is the first identified candidate pair for which `ResourcePressure` would not be constant**, and it therefore names trigger 2's firing condition concretely for the first time. It does not fire it: no such pair is enumerable, because axis 2's refusals stand in front of it.

**Maturity.** Four cost models: **implemented and tested**, each with its own key and its own dominance test. Calibration: **nothing** — `EstimateProvenance::Measured` is a variant with no producer anywhere in `crates/`. Total-order selection: **seam**, owned by the deferred calibration ticket. Occupancy model: **seam**, owned by [`model-resource-pressure-from-a-register-and-occupancy-model`](../../../tickets/model-resource-pressure-from-a-register-and-occupancy-model.md) (`deferred`).

## Axis 6 — Conformance oracle

**The capability.** A rewritten, deliberately bit-different implementation must be checkable against something that is not the naive spelling's bits.

**Fact — the whole-program oracle is bit-exact by construction and refuses to be anything else.** `ReferenceNumericalConformance::from_realization` (`crates/tiler-reference/src/conformance.rs:166`) destructures a `NumericalRealization` and returns an error for **each** of `contraction`, `reassociation`, `permutation`, and `signed_zero` resolved `Permitted`, and for either exceptional-value assumption resolved `AssumeAbsent`. Its module header states the ground exactly: the evaluator computes a separately rounded multiply and add and a strict left fold, which is *one* legal realization of a permissive contract, and "an oracle that returned a single value would assert a bitwise equality the contract does not promise. … Refusing names each gap instead of hiding it."

**Inference — the refusal is right and the gap it names is unowned.** Refusing is strictly better than silently comparing against one arbitrary member of the contract's result set. But it means the reference layer can qualify a candidate **only** under the strict contract, and the naive decoder-layer baseline's zero-differing-elements evidence is exactly that case. For any candidate compiled under a permissive contract the oracle has no verdict at all — not `Undecided`, but a refusal to be constructed.

**Fact — this already bites, before any flash work.** `NumericalContract::FLUSH_AND_REASSOCIATE_F32` (`crates/tiler-compiler/src/session.rs:1490`) is a registered contract; `crates/tiler-compiler/src/request/contract.rs "FLUSH_AND_REASSOCIATE_F32.resolve()"` resolves it, and `calibrate-and-activate-parallel-reduction-selection`'s 2026-08-02 sweep measured under it. **Inference — so a reassociating candidate is compilable today and unqualifiable today**, and the gap is not a consequence of admitting anything.

**Fact — the machinery an enclosure-based oracle would use exists one layer down and is the right shape.** `CertifiedEnclosure`, `decide_predicate` (`crates/tiler-reference/src/accuracy.rs:772`), and the fail-closed three-way `ConformanceDecision` (`:735`) already answer "does this exact-rational candidate lie inside this enclosure" for an accuracy predicate over one evaluation. **Inference — what is missing is the lift from one evaluation to a program:** a whole-program enclosure is not the composition of pointwise ones, exactly as ADR 0101 decision 7 says of the accuracy contract, and the certified-bounds record's third obligation says a bound derived for a sequential fold does not admit a tree fold. So the oracle for a permitted-divergence candidate is a *derivation* question about which object bounds a program, not an engineering question about wiring two existing types together.

**Inference — the alternative, per-contract golden regeneration, is eliminated and the ground is not cost.** A golden regenerated under a permissive contract pins one member of a result set the contract does not promise, so the next legal schedule change fails a test that was never testing the contract. That is the same failure the refusal above exists to prevent, moved from the oracle into the fixtures where it is harder to see.

**Maturity.** Bit-exact whole-program oracle: **implemented and tested**, and correctly refusing outside its domain. Enclosure machinery for one evaluation: **implemented**. Permitted-divergence program oracle: **nothing** — not a seam, and the refusal at `conformance.rs:166` is the only evidence in the tree that it is absent by design rather than by oversight.

**Outcome — a bounded research question, filed.** [`derive-the-oracle-for-a-permitted-divergence-candidate`](../../../tickets/derive-the-oracle-for-a-permitted-divergence-candidate.md), `todo`, filed by this record. It is `todo` rather than `deferred` because its trigger has already fired: a reassociating contract is registered and reachable now.

## Axis 7 — Information the system lacks
