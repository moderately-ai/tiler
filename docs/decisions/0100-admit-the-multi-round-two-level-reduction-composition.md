# 0100: Admit the multi-round two-level reduction composition over a lexicographic round-subgroup-lane block index

**Status: accepted by Tom on 2026-08-05 at the third live decision review in the coordination session, witnessed first-hand by the coordinator that executed this sweep.** The acceptance supersedes [ADR 0096](0096-compose-the-subgroup-and-workgroup-reduction-levels.md) **decision 8 only** — the one-round exclusion, whose stated ground that record had already corrected as stale — and nothing else in that record; the supersession is recorded in prose on both records rather than in frontmatter, because a whole-record `supersedes` edge would overstate it. None of the five public-boundary items the derivation enumerates is accepted by this; each arrives individually under ADR 0075. It was drafted by the ticket [ADR 0096](0096-compose-the-subgroup-and-workgroup-reduction-levels.md)'s open questions hand the multi-round derivation to, and under [the decisions index](README.md)'s own preamble a proposed record remained a non-decision until this acceptance. [The multi-round two-level reduction composition](../research/scheduling/multi-round-two-level-reduction-composition.md) is the derivation; this record states the decisions and cites it rather than re-deriving them.

**What acceptance would do to ADR 0096, stated here because it is the one thing a reader must not infer.** ADR 0096 decision 8 reads "One staging round, and a second is refused rather than approximated", and its ground — that the cooperative profile models no per-round lifetime — was already dead at that record's own base, which its implementation boundary says. **Accepting this record supersedes decision 8 and nothing else in ADR 0096**; decisions 1 through 7 are consumed here unchanged, and decision 1 additionally receives a correction that is a correction of *scope* rather than a supersession (see decision 3). This record is deliberately not marked as superseding ADR 0096 in frontmatter, because whole-record supersession is not what is proposed and a frontmatter claim of it would be false.

**Measurement boundary, repeated here rather than left one link away, because it is total.** *Nothing in this design was executed, emitted, compiled, dispatched, or timed.* Every claim behind it is inspected source at `1d918b67` or a primary vendor specification relayed through [the two-level subgroup-then-workgroup reduction](../research/scheduling/two-level-subgroup-workgroup-reduction.md) rather than read primarily. The barrier counts below are derived counts under a stated convention, and no cost model in this repository prices a barrier against an addition. Two of the facts the derivation rests on are *tested guarantees* in the tree — the peeled round body and the extrema tile's cross-round combine — and a test is not a measurement.

## Context

ADR 0096 composes a subgroup combine and a workgroup staging level in one topology over a two-component coordinate, and its decision 8 excludes a second staging round. That exclusion's stated ground stopped being true when `admit-loop-carried-cooperative-staging` landed `CooperativeTile::rounds`, `AntiDependencyEdge`, `SynchronizationPlacement::RoundBoundary`, and `ConvergenceEvidence::EveryParticipantExecutesEveryRound`. ADR 0096's own open questions record that the deferral's trigger has fired, that nothing had been re-derived against the capability, and that round vocabulary decides what the verifier admits while the numerical question is a leaf order across rounds and an identity obligation for a ragged final round.

Two further landings changed what a multi-round derivation may assume. [ADR 0097](0097-admit-a-two-dimensional-cooperative-staging-relation.md) is implemented, and its decision 4 keeps the round ordinal out of the staging relation deliberately. The extrema fold reached both parallel topologies: `cooperative_family` admits `ScalarProgram::StrictSerialMaximum` with `EmptyDomainContract::NoIdentity` and `consumes_reassociation: false`, and the non-emptiness argument that discharges its identity-less-ness is stated for *exactly covered* splits.

## Decision

1. **The composition's contributor-block index is the triple `(round, subgroup index, lane index)` read lexicographically — `index = r·T + g·W + l` — stated in the schedule as one statement rather than two.** The implemented cooperative split already fixes the outermost component: `ReductionTopology::CooperativeWorkgroup::partition` states that participant `p` of round `r` owns the contiguous range at index `r * partitions + p`, and ADR 0096 decision 5 fixes the inner pair. The composed leaf order of three ascending levels over one contiguous partition is the declared contributor sequence ascending, so **the multi-round composition consumes reassociation alone**, exactly as the one-round composition does. A participant-major round nesting — participant `p` owning index `p·R + r` — is refused by name: it is a strided permutation at identical instruction count, and no coverage arithmetic distinguishes the two.

2. **Padding occupies the tail of the padded sequence, so only the final round may be ragged, and a round with no real contributor is refused rather than admitted.** Per-round padding is refused on `ContributorPartition`'s own ground — it is the second extent and second trip count `covers` declines to approximate. The canonicality rule is `T·k·(R−1) < C ≤ T·k·R`: below it a round exists whose every position is an identity, which discharges an anti-dependency and realizes two barriers for a program that computes nothing, and which makes two round counts state one semantics with two identities.

3. **The composition covers the *padded* sequence, its padding count is derived, and this is a rule of its own rather than the cooperative topology's.** `partition.partitions == T == threads_per_workgroup` holds as ADR 0096 decision 1 claims; the exact product `partition.total_contributors() * rounds == contributors` does not, because the composition's inner width is imposed and the region's contributor count is the real domain. `padding = T·k·R − C` is a total function of values the verifier already holds and is therefore derived, never declared — the argument ADR 0096 decision 3 makes for the staging writer set and ADR 0097 decision 2 makes for the participant range. The two topologies must not share one coverage rule identifier, because an explanation's reader has to know which sequence was covered.

4. **One identity constant, consumed at the inner level alone, and the count does not grow with the round count.** A contributor-free lane holds the stated two-sided `lane_identity_bits` and only on the final round; the outer fold over `G` slots escapes because all `G` slots are written on every round including the ragged one, so a fully padded subgroup stages an identity the inner level produced rather than one the outer level injects; and the cross-round accumulator escapes because `emit_loop_carried_cooperative` peels round zero and seeds the accumulator with its own staged total. The same two-sidedness proof discharges all three sites, because the outer fold and the round accumulator are the same binary operation as the inner combine.

5. **The peeled round zero is load-bearing for the composition's numerics and is recorded as such rather than left an emission detail.** An accumulator seeded with the region's `empty_identity_bits` and a loop over `0..R` would commit `+0.0` for a row whose true strict sum is `-0.0`, with every lane identity correct — the ADR 0096 identity trap moved from the lane to the accumulator, on a value the contract says is observable. A future emission that generalizes the peel away reintroduces a second identity obligation no schedule field states, and that is a case a test must be able to fail on.

6. **A multi-round composition declares two synchronization points, and a single-point one is refused by name rather than raced.** A two-phase tile whose phases repeat derives one `AntiDependencyEdge`, and the produce/consume phase boundary does not discharge it — `discharges_anti`'s disjunction is false at `preceding = 0`, `following = 1` against `consumed_in = 1`, `rewritten_in = 0`. A `RoundBoundary` point is therefore required, both points must name `EveryParticipantExecutesEveryRound`, and omitting the round boundary is `UndischargedAntiDependency`. This is precisely the write-after-read hazard the Metal specification's printed multi-round reduction carries with nothing ordering it; the composition refuses it by construction.

7. **The identity-less family needs a stated padding identity, `0xff80_0000` is provably it, and that is separate from its empty-domain refusal rather than a weakening of it.** The extrema family's non-emptiness argument is stated for exact coverage and does not reach a padded split. `maximum(-inf, x) = x` on every binary32 `x` under the NaN-propagating family with `-0.0 < +0.0`, with the NaN case observably neutral because the fold canonicalizes after every combine — which is the neutrality ADR 0022 requires proved before a schedule injects a padding value, and the separation of empty result, algebraic identity, and replicable padding the numerics contract already draws. A composition over that family with no stated padding identity is refused rather than flagged with a runtime `has_value`, padded by replicating a real contributor under idempotence, or restricted to exactly divisible contributor counts.

## Consequences

- A reduction longer than one threadgroup's single-round capacity gains a schedule that stays inside one dispatch, at `2R − 1` barriers and workgroup memory proportional to the number of subgroups. Whether that beats a larger per-participant serial prefix or a multi-dispatch split is a cost question no profile in this repository can answer.
- The schedule vocabulary gains its first obligation stated over three coordinates rather than two, and the round component is the only one of the three whose ordering no vendor specification can perturb.
- Two new named rules join the cooperative rule vocabulary — a round-canonicality refusal and a padded-coverage rule — and neither may be folded into the exact-product rule the cooperative topology keeps.
- The padding identity becomes a per-region stated value proved against the region's own combiner rather than a constant of the sum family, which is what lets one composition serve the softmax's two passes.
- Nothing here admits a round-dependent staged span, a per-access active-participant subset, a narrowing outer shuffle tree, a second staged allocation, a subgroup memory scope, a multi-launch reduction, or any Metal, CUDA, or WebGPU backend claim. It does not make the composition emittable: no emission lowers a two-component participant coordinate, and none exists to extend.

## Alternatives considered

- **Reading the round vocabulary's admission as the answer.** Rejected, and it is what the producing ticket exists to prevent: `rounds` decides the staged storage's per-round bijection and says nothing about the leaf order across rounds. A tile with a perfect per-round bijection under a participant-major block index consumes a permutation and passes every check the tile's own admission performs.
- **Leaving the round nesting to a lowering convention.** Rejected for ADR 0096 decision 5's reason one level out: a convention a schedule does not carry is one a verifier cannot check, and the two nestings differ in the permission consumed at identical instruction count.
- **Per-round padding, so that each round covers its own share of the real sequence.** Rejected: `ContributorPartition` is a single product and its doc already declines the second extent and second trip count a ragged per-round split needs.
- **A declared padding count on the topology.** Rejected: it is a total function of the partition, the round count, and the access map, so a field would be a second place to state one fact and a place for a producer to be wrong.
- **A cross-round accumulator seeded with a stated constant, instead of a peeled round zero.** Rejected on correctness: the region's empty identity is `+0.0` and `+0.0 + (-0.0)` is `+0.0`, so the seed would need a second proved two-sided identity that the peel makes unnecessary. The peel is also what the tree already emits.
- **A carried `has_value` flag for the identity-less family.** Rejected: the inner level is a register-transfer tree, so the flag needs a second shuffle tree over booleans and a select at every step, and it puts a runtime fact into a vocabulary whose staging argument is decided statically by enumeration.
- **Replicating a real contributor into padding positions under `maximum`'s idempotence.** Rejected: sound for one family and unsound for every sum, so it can never be the vocabulary's rule; it turns coverage into a multiset; and the freedom it spends is a duplication dimension ADR 0011's structure does not have and ADR 0095 declined to add a sibling of.
- **Refusing the composition over the identity-less family unless the contributor count divides exactly.** Rejected on reach: the row maximum exists for the attention softmax, whose relayed prefill row length is 8,192 and whose neighbours are not all factorable into a multiple of an imposed width, so the refusal would decline the strategy rather than the input.

## Traceability

## Implementation boundary

**This record decides nothing until Tom accepts it, and it implements nothing in either case.** `implementation_status` is `not-started`, and the tree state below is read at `1d918b67`.

**Current-status correction — 2026-08-09.** Tom has accepted the record, so it now decides the model above. The second half remains current: it still implements nothing, `implementation_status` remains `not-started`, and the source inventory below is the acceptance-era implementation boundary rather than a claim that acceptance is pending.

No crate gains an item under this record. `ReductionTopology` has exactly five variants and the two-level variant ADR 0096 argues for is unclaimed rather than reserved, so every field this record's decisions describe sits on a topology that does not exist. `LocalCoordinateSource` has `LocalLinearInvocation` and `LocalWorkgroupPosition`, and neither names a subgroup coordinate — its doc says in as many words that a subgroup-derived source "must be a variant of its own when it lands" — so the block index decision 1 states has no spelling. The exact check that no subgroup construct exists is `grep -rn 'SubgroupWidth\|lane_identity\|SubgroupThenWorkgroup' crates/`, which returns nothing.

**What *is* implemented is everything the composition would reuse, and naming it is the point of this section.** `CooperativeTile::rounds`, `AntiDependencyEdge`, `SynchronizationPlacement::RoundBoundary`, `ConvergenceEvidence::EveryParticipantExecutesEveryRound`, and the round-multiplied coverage check in `verify_cooperative_semantics` all compile and are exercised: `a_loop_carried_rewrite_with_no_round_boundary_is_refused`, `a_round_boundary_without_a_following_round_is_redundant`, `a_split_that_ignores_the_round_count_is_refused`, and `two_writers_reaching_one_slot_in_one_round_are_still_refused` in `crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/builder.rs`, and `a_loop_carried_tile_lowers_to_a_peeled_round_body` and `a_loop_carried_extrema_tile_carries_its_maximum_across_rounds` in `crates/tiler-ir/src/kernel/tests.rs`. **Decision 5's peel is therefore a tested guarantee at one level and a reservation at two**, and the difference is exactly the maturity claim this section exists to keep apart.

**One landed doc comment contradicts decision 7 and is not edited by this record.** `ScalarProgram::StrictSerialMaximum` states that "no binary32 value `i` satisfies `Maximum(i, x) == x` for every `x`, because any candidate is itself a possible contributor". Its conclusion — no empty-domain identity field — is supported by ADR 0023 and the numerics contract; its stated algebraic ground is refuted by the `-inf` case walk, and a reader who carries it forward concludes the extrema family can never be padded. [`correct-the-extrema-familys-identity-ground-and-name-its-padding-identity`](../../tickets/correct-the-extrema-familys-identity-ground-and-name-its-padding-identity.md) owns the correction, because `crates/` was outside the producing ticket's scopes.

## Open questions

The research record carries four deferrals, each with its evidence and trigger, and this record adopts them unchanged rather than restating them: **no cost evidence separates a multi-round composition from a single-round one with a larger per-participant fold**, closing with a measurement on a device and triggered by the first target profile declaring a subgroup width; **the composition is derived for a contributor stream whose blocking is free**, closing with a composition over two staged allocations and triggered by a fused blocked contraction feeding a row reduction; **the peel's generality is not proved for a two-level emission**, closing with a test that watches a seeded cross-round accumulator fail and triggered by the first emission that lowers the composition; and **the round-dependent staged span stays deferred**, with [`admit-a-round-dependent-cooperative-staging-span`](../../tickets/admit-a-round-dependent-cooperative-staging-span.md)'s own triggers unfired — the composition's spans are round-invariant for the same reason the tiled contraction's are.

**And ADR 0096's six deferrals are inherited unchanged.** The outer shuffle tree's deferral is *strengthened* rather than fired: at several rounds its second identity would be needed on every round rather than on the ragged one. The rest — the one-subgroup-per-slot restriction, the coordinate's spelling, the absent target-declared subgroup width, and `MemoryScope::Subgroup` — are untouched by the round dimension.
