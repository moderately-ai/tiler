# 0095: Decline a distributivity permission and keep contraction-chain regrouping rejected

**Status:** accepted. Tom declined the admission at the live review on 2026-08-01, relayed by the coordinator, in the same session that accepted [ADR 0094](0094-bind-a-subgroup-combine-to-a-register-transfer-tree.md). The decision is recorded on [`decide-whether-to-admit-a-distributivity-permission`](../../tickets/decide-whether-to-admit-a-distributivity-permission.md) under "Both trigger clauses fired, and the decision followed", which is where a reader can check the relay in one line. **A decline is a decision and is recorded as `accepted`, not as a rejected proposal** — what is accepted is that no distributivity permission is admitted, and [ADR 0080](0080-treat-distributivity-as-a-third-numerical-dimension.md) item 4, which reserved this choice rather than making it, is answered rather than superseded.

## Context

[ADR 0080](0080-treat-distributivity-as-a-third-numerical-dimension.md) settled the derivation and deliberately withheld the product choice. Regrouping a tensor-contraction chain consumes **distributivity** — exchanging a product of a sum for a sum of products — which is a numerical dimension independent of reassociation and of operand permutation, because the two forms produce contributor sequences sharing no value, indexed by different axes, of which no common sequence is a grouping. [`settle-contraction-chain-distributivity-permission`](../../tickets/settle-contraction-chain-distributivity-permission.md) landed that derivation from the numerical contract's own definitions, and [Numerical semantics](../numerical-semantics.md#distributivity-is-outside-the-order-contract) names the dimension, admits no permission for it, and rejects the rewrite explicitly. ADR 0080 item 4 reserved one question and one only: **whether to admit a permission for the dimension at all.** This record answers it.

**Both clauses of the reservation's trigger have since fired.** ADR 0080 reserved the choice "to the accepted decision that admits a tensor-contraction family", and the ticket restated the trigger as that decision "or any earlier proposal to register a numerical contract that permits reassociation". [ADR 0087](0087-model-contraction-as-one-keyed-family-with-an-index-structure.md) is accepted and `StandardSemantics` registers `tiler::strict-tensor-contraction-f32@1`; `StrictF32NumericalContract::governed_profile` now registers a permit-reassociation contract beside the strict one. The question is therefore reachable rather than hypothetical, and answering it is what this record does.

## Decision

**No distributivity permission is admitted.** Three clauses, and the third is the one a reader is most likely to skip:

1. **The dimension stays named and unpermissioned.** [Numerical semantics](../numerical-semantics.md#distributivity-is-outside-the-order-contract) continues to define distributivity, to state that no permission grants it, and to require that a rejection name the missing dimension rather than report a forbidden reassociation. Nothing in that section is weakened, and no field, variant, or capability naming distributivity enters any crate under this record.

2. **The contraction-chain regroup stays explicitly rejected under every contract Tiler can express, as a settled legality position rather than an unexplored one.** This is not a gap awaiting engineering. It is the same answer a compiler that accepted contractions under a contract permitting both reassociation and permutation would give, which is what makes it durable rather than incidental.

3. **Contraction ordering remains a planning question *within* one semantic contraction, never a logical rewrite over a chain.** Under ADR 0087's keyed family a contraction carries its index structure, and how a schedule orders the fold of that one contraction's contributor sequence is governed by the order-contract dimensions the numerical contract already has. What a decline forecloses is a rewrite that replaces one contraction chain with another whose products are different values — an optimization over the *graph*, not a choice within a node.

## Why decline, stated so the elimination can be refuted rather than only the conclusion

**No caller exists.** A permission's whole content is that a caller may authorize a freedom; admitting one nobody can spend adds a dimension to every contract, a field to the canonical policy, an arm to every exhaustive match over permissions, and an obligation on every future target profile to answer for it — while changing no program's meaning and enabling no rewrite anyone has asked for. **The vocabulary carries no caller-less permissions**, and this is the principle the decline rests on rather than a cost estimate.

**The pinned workload contains no chain the regroup is *asked for*, and the check was made against the operation sequence rather than against the shape.** [The transformer operation and shape surface](../research/shapes/transformer-operation-and-shape-surface.md) records nine contractions per layer, and [the first attention program vertical](../research/program-planning/first-attention-program-vertical.md) lists the block operation by operation. The SwiGLU MLP is `down(silu(gate(x)) * up(x))` — a SiLU and an elementwise multiply sit between its contractions, so no regroup is even statable there. **The attention block is the sharper case and the ground has to be stated narrowly to survive it.** Its first contraction pair, operations 15 and 19, is separated by a scale, a mask add, and a softmax, so a nonlinearity interposes exactly as the MLP's does. Its second pair is not: operation 19 produces `ctx`, operation 20 is a *structural* `Reindex` permute-and-merge, and operation 21 contracts the merged axis against `W_o`. Composing them and factoring gives `sum_s probs · (sum_d v · W_o)` in place of `(sum_s probs · v) · W_o`, and that regroup does consume distributivity. **So the honest ground is not "no regroupable pair exists" — one does — but that the workload's natural spelling is the direct order and the regroup is one an optimizer might speculatively want**, which is precisely the distinction the reopening trigger below is written on. It is also the direction cost runs: the factored form contracts `V` against a `W_o` slice per grouped head, which is work the direct order never performs.

**Admitting later is additive and costs nothing now.** A permission is a widening of a dimension set that no accepted decision claims to be exhaustive, so nothing about this decline has to be reversed to admit one — the derivation, the definition, and the rejection wording are exactly the material an admission would build on, and all three already exist. Declining is therefore not the cheaper option wearing a trade-off's clothes: it forecloses nothing, and the option it declines can be taken in full at the moment it acquires a caller.

**A permission admitted without evidence would have to guess its own shape.** ADR 0014 split reassociation from operand permutation on evidence that some combiners have one capability and not the other. No analogous asymmetry has been established between the factoring and expanding directions of the distributive identity, so admitting a permission today would force a one-or-two-permissions choice with nothing behind it. That dependent question is [`decide-whether-distributivity-directions-share-one-permission`](../../tickets/decide-whether-distributivity-directions-share-one-permission.md), and it stays parked: it does not arise under a decline and becomes live only if this record is reopened.

## Reopening trigger

**The first workload whose natural spelling is a directly regroupable contraction chain — one where the regrouping that consumes distributivity is what the workload asks for, rather than one an optimizer might speculatively want.** The distinguishing test is stated so it can be applied rather than argued: a workload that merely *contains* a contraction chain does not fire it; the chain must be one whose profitable form requires exchanging a product of a sum for a sum of products. The attention block's operations 19 through 21 are the worked negative case — a regroupable pair whose regroup nobody is asking for — and a candidate that cannot be distinguished from it does not fire the trigger either.

**Second reopening condition, added by the 2026-08-06 reaffirmation below: the two permissions are considered *together* when a rewrite rule with a derived bound instantiable at a schedulable fold shape is ready to consume them.** The online-softmax rescaling fold consumes both this dimension and [ADR 0101](0101-treat-elementary-function-identities-as-a-fourth-numerical-dimension.md)'s elementary-function identity, so the decision that reaches it is a joint one — [`decide-whether-to-admit-an-elementary-identity-permission`](../../tickets/decide-whether-to-admit-an-elementary-identity-permission.md) already names this reassessment resolving in the admitting direction as its own first trigger, and this condition is its converse. "Ready to consume" is concrete: a rule in the certified-bounds admission shape, a retrievable `eps_exp` to instantiate its bound, and the bound derived at the fold shape a parallel schedule would select rather than only the sequential form. The point at which those exist is the point at which the "admitting later is additive" ground stops being an argument for waiting.

## Reaffirmation — 2026-08-06

Reaffirmed by Tom on 2026-08-06 at the live decision review in the coordination session, witnessed first-hand by the coordinator, against [the reassessment packet](../../tickets/reassess-the-distributivity-decline-against-the-online-softmax-rescaling-caller.md) prepared for exactly this question. The record's grounds met a derived counterexample and the decline stands on strictly better evidence than it was made on:

- **The "no caller exists" ground met a caller and is replaced by its narrowed form.** [The certified-bounds record](../research/numerics/certified-bounds-as-rewrite-permissions.md)'s Part 2 derives that the online-softmax rescaling fold consumes this dimension — a caller for the *dimension*, and not a contraction chain, so the trigger above never fired. What replaces the original ground: **no caller can spend a distributivity permission admitted alone**, because the same and only identified rewrite independently consumes ADR 0101's elementary-function identity, for which no permission is admitted. The original sentence — a permission nobody can spend changes no program's meaning and enables no rewrite — remains true of a distributivity-only admission, now for that reason.
- **The price is on record so no reader re-derives it.** The fold's worst-case relative cost over the two-pass form is first-order `(V-1)(u + eps_exp)` — about `2^-14` at `V = 512` in binary32 with a correctly rounded exponential — input-independent because the logit spread cancels, and checked against 22 adversarial cases at [`spikes/numerics/online_softmax_bound/`](../../spikes/numerics/online_softmax_bound/README.md) with no violation. When the second reopening condition's prerequisites exist, admission is well-priced; the reaffirmation is about readiness, not about the price.
- **The contraction-chain trigger above is unchanged**, because the ground it rests on — the pinned workload's natural spelling asks for no chain regroup — was untouched by any of the new evidence.

The reaffirmation changes what this record rests on, not which way it decides; no contract sentence moves, no identity moves, and `implementation_status` stays `not-started` for the same reason as before.

## Consequences

- Contraction-order exploration over a chain remains illegal rather than unimplemented, and [the optimizer's third logical-exploration rule](../compiler/optimizer.md#logical-exploration) and [fusion and scheduling](../compiler/fusion-and-scheduling.md)'s contraction-planning boundary are unchanged in every word. Both already state the position and cite ADR 0080; this record settles the reservation they gesture at, and falsifies no sentence in either.
- The rejection wording obligation survives unchanged: a rewrite consuming distributivity rejects naming the missing dimension, never a forbidden reassociation. A decline makes that wording *more* load-bearing, not less, because it is now the only thing that tells a caller the freedom is withheld by decision rather than absent by oversight.
- `implementation_status` is `not-started` and that is the honest value under a decline as much as under an admission: the only thing the tree contains is the absence the rejection is required to name, and no crate changes.
- Q-SEM-015's third reserved choice is closed. The two that remain reserved for Tom — the keyed-family question, answered by ADR 0087, and whether a semantic contraction node may consume more than two operands — are untouched, and this record's conclusion holds under either answer to the second, because a flat multi-operand node's contributors are triple products that no binary association forms and factoring one operand out is distributivity again.

## Alternatives considered

**Admit a distributivity permission now, so contraction-order exploration becomes reachable.** Rejected on the caller argument above: it widens every contract and every exhaustive permission match to authorize a rewrite no registered contract could use, no admitted capability declares, and no pinned workload asks for. It would also force the untested one-or-two-permissions choice, since a permission has to be spelled before it can be admitted.

**Admit it in the factoring direction only, as the conservative half.** Rejected because "conservative" is doing no work: nothing establishes that factoring and expansion differ in capability, so a one-directional admission would be an arbitrary cut presented as caution. If a caller ever appears, the evidence it brings is what should decide the cut.

**Leave the choice reserved and close nothing.** The status quo, and it was already wrong before this record: both clauses of the reservation's trigger had fired, and [`decide-whether-to-admit-a-distributivity-permission`](../../tickets/decide-whether-to-admit-a-distributivity-permission.md) and its dependent both sat parked behind a trigger that had gone off, which nothing owned noticing. A reservation whose trigger has fired is not a reservation; it is an unanswered question wearing one's label.

**Fold the answer into ADR 0080 as an amendment.** ADR 0080's own last alternative anticipated this and left the choice to Tom. Rejected here on custody: 0080's authority is that it contains no product choice, and this record contains nothing but one. Keeping them separate lets a reader see at a glance which claims are derived and which were decided, and lets a future admission supersede this record alone without disturbing the derivation.

**Normative owner.** [Numerical semantics](../numerical-semantics.md#distributivity-is-outside-the-order-contract) owns the dimension's definition, the worked counterexample, and the reservation sentence this record converts into a decision. [The optimizer model](../compiler/optimizer.md) and [fusion and scheduling](../compiler/fusion-and-scheduling.md) state the rule and the legality boundary and are unchanged by this record, which is why neither appears in `applies_to`.

**Evidence.** [Reduction semantics and legality](../research/numerics/reduction-semantics-and-legality.md) is the order contract the derivation sits outside of. [The transformer operation and shape surface](../research/shapes/transformer-operation-and-shape-surface.md) supplies the per-layer contraction count and the SwiGLU composition, and already records at its open-questions list that contraction-order exploration over the SwiGLU or attention chains is illegal as a settled position. [The first attention program vertical](../research/program-planning/first-attention-program-vertical.md) supplies the operation-by-operation sequence the worked negative case above is read from.

**Decided authorities this record preserves rather than amends.** [ADR 0080](0080-treat-distributivity-as-a-third-numerical-dimension.md) defines the dimension and reserved exactly this choice at its item 4; this record answers the reservation and supersedes nothing. [ADR 0011](0011-per-operation-numerical-permissions.md) requires every rewrite to declare the permission it consumes and holds that one permission never implies another. [ADR 0014](0014-reassociation-vs-permutation.md) separates the two order-contract dimensions and supplies the capability-asymmetry standard the directional question fails. [ADR 0015](0015-fma-vs-contraction.md) settles the structurally identical question for fused multiply-add — a permission over an existing pattern does not authorize manufacturing a new one. [ADR 0087](0087-model-contraction-as-one-keyed-family-with-an-index-structure.md) is what makes clause 3 statable: a contraction carries its index structure, so ordering within one is a planning question that needs no new permission.
