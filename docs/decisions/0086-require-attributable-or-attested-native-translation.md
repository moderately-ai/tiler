# 0086: Require attributable or attested native translation

**Status:** accepted. Tom decided this on 2026-07-31; the decision is preserved in the deciding ticket's own comment thread at [`tickets/authorize-macos-environment-identity-for-native-metal-translation.comments/1785509001789554000-d677bfb9.md`](../../tickets/authorize-macos-environment-identity-for-native-metal-translation.comments/1785509001789554000-d677bfb9.md), added by commit `562829c`. It refines the applicability and provenance terms of [ADR 0043](0043-use-typed-phased-target-feasibility.md) for the one case ADR 0043 named as legal without saying what authority it carries: required device translation of an AOT metallib.

## Context

**Fact — Apple's metallib is not final machine code, and the local research record says so from primary documentation.** The [Apple Metal artifact compatibility](../research/apple-targets/artifact-compatibility.md) memo records under "Primary Apple contracts / Source-backed" that "Apple describes a metallib as Metal IR, a GPU-independent intermediate representation that the runtime may still compile into a GPU-specific binary during pipeline creation", citing Apple's [Metal libraries](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metal/metal-libraries) documentation and the WWDC 2022 session "Target and optimize GPU binaries with Metal 3". Its first inferred contract states the consequence directly: "A metallib is source-level AOT, not proof of zero first-use compilation." The [Metal AOT backend](../backends/metal.md) contract carries the same sentence among its platform concerns, and its opening paragraph already states that "Runtime pipeline creation remains necessary, but runtime source compilation does not."

**Fact — ADR 0043 admits that translation and leaves its responsible component unnamed.** ADR 0043 decides that "Artifact representation, compatibility, and runtime-translation policy are explicit. This does not authorize runtime source compilation: the initial product still forbids it, while a backend may declare required device translation of an AOT target-IR artifact such as a metallib." It separately requires "typed capability facts with explicit availability phase, validity scope, authority, and provenance". Those two clauses have never been read against each other. ADR 0043 permits a backend to declare that a device translation is required; it does not say which component performs that translation, what authority stands behind the declaration, or what provenance the resulting fact carries.

**Fact — ADR 0043 already fixes what happens when that provenance is missing.** Its feasibility rule is that "Any disproved hard predicate rejects; otherwise a predicate with no admissible proof/query path is unknown", and its disposal of that class is unconditional: "An `Unknown` candidate may remain in search/explain state but cannot enter an executable frontier or manifest." Its ordered availability phases place the relevant question at `LiveDevicePreflight`, and it separately holds that "Live-device and prepared-kernel facts scope runtime caches and routing provenance, not portable semantic identity."

**Measurement — the native AOT route cannot attribute the component that performs the translation.** On 2026-07-31 the preserved [AOT runtime-compiler observer](../../spikes/apple-targets/aot-runtime-compiler-observer/README.md) offline-compiled one MSL 4.0 metallib for `air64-apple-macos26.0`, then loaded its bytes, resolved one function, and prepared one compute pipeline with no source input and no `newLibraryWithSource:options:error:` selector in the compiled probe. Its [retained summary](../../spikes/apple-targets/aot-runtime-compiler-observer/results/2026-07-31-macos27-m4max/summary.tsv) records `observation.compiler_images_at_process_start=2`, `observation.compiler_images_after_pipeline=2`, `observation.new_compiler_images_during_route=0`, and `observation.unavailable_image_scans_after_pipeline=2`: the two `GPUCompiler.framework` library images were already resident before the route existed, the population did not move through device, library, function, or pipeline preparation, and both direct scans of their dyld-cache paths failed with `unavailable:NSCocoaErrorDomain:260`, so no build string was recovered and none is claimed absent from the mapped bytes. Three clean processes were byte-identical. The classifier's verdict is `conclusion.exact_runtime_compiler_attribution=unavailable` over `conclusion.evidence_class=loaded-image-membership-and-image-byte-scan`. A negative control preloading a synthetic `UnrelatedGPUCompiler.dylib` carrying a readable `metalfe-99999.1` string produced membership and a build string without producing attribution, and `MTLCompiler.framework` was present on disk while absent from the loaded-image population. The [Apple GPU numerical behaviour](../research/apple-targets/numerical-behaviour.md) record owns this measurement under "Native AOT runtime-compiler attribution".

**Measurement — what the same route does establish.** The retained [clean run](../../spikes/apple-targets/aot-runtime-compiler-observer/results/2026-07-31-macos27-m4max/clean-1.tsv) records exactly these public execution-environment fields: `environment.os_version=27.0`, `environment.os_build=26A5388g`, `environment.architecture=arm64`, `environment.device=Apple M4 Max`, and `environment.device_apple9_support=supported`. That row completely describes the environment which delivered the observation and contains no field naming the translating component.

**Fact — every cheaper identity was eliminated against its own source rather than by preference.** `metalfe-32023.921` is the build the macOS host loads for `newLibraryWithSource:options:`, measured by serializing an `MTLBinaryArchive` from a runtime-compiled library; findings 8 and 12 of the numerical record own it and it qualifies that comparison path alone. Tiler's AOT route supplies no source, so that build is not evidence about what answered native pipeline preparation. The offline `metalfe-32023.883` compiler and its AIR-LLD linker are artifact provenance for the bytes Tiler produced and say nothing about what consumes them on a device. Framework presence on disk is refuted as execution evidence by `MTLCompiler.framework` in the run above, and was already the subject of a recorded correction in finding 8. Loaded-image membership and membership deltas are refuted by the negative control. Registry ID is not a stable hardware identity: the retained records report 4294968621 and 4294968452 for the same named Apple M4 Max, and the iOS Simulator reports the host's own registry ID under a different device name.

**Inference — the surviving question was one of authority, not of measurement.** The bounded F32 numerical facts for this profile were replayed through both compilation paths and survived byte for byte, so the arithmetic evidence is not what is missing. What is missing is a fact of ADR 0043's own required shape: a capability fact for required device translation carrying an authority and a provenance. The exact measured execution-environment row supplies a **validity scope** and supplies neither of the other two. Reading it as though it did would collapse three separately typed fields into one.

## Decision

Native device translation of a metallib during Metal pipeline creation is a typed capability fact whose authority and provenance are `Unknown` on every macOS row currently observable, and the first authoritative native Metal profile stays unavailable until one of two named authorities exists.

1. **A positive host-applicability receipt requires an attributable identity for the private translating component, or exact host attestation binding the execution environment.** Tom's decision states it as recorded: "require either attributable private native-translation identity or exact host attestation, and keep the first authoritative native profile unavailable until one exists." Neither exists on current APIs, so the predicate is `Unknown` and the receipt is refused.

2. **The refusal is ADR 0043's existing disposal of `Unknown`, applied rather than amended.** A predicate with no admissible proof or query path is unknown, and an `Unknown` candidate cannot enter an executable frontier or manifest. No new feasibility outcome, no infinite cost, and no arbitrary penalty is introduced. The refusal is typed and names the exact unsatisfied predicate, so explain output distinguishes it from a disproved hard predicate and from a cost disadvantage.

3. **The exact measured public execution-environment row remains a necessary validity scope and is explicitly not sufficient authority.** OS family, version, and build; architecture; the exact reported device name; and supported GPU family continue to bound every claim derived from the retained measurement, and a changed row still invalidates it. They do not become the authority for the translation fact. The reason is the one Tom recorded: "an opaque translator can change while the observed OS/build/device row remains identical", so a predicate keyed on those fields would admit a host whose translating component had moved underneath it, and would report success rather than the divergence.

4. **No substitute may be relabelled as the translation authority.** The source-JIT build `metalfe-32023.921`, the offline compiler and linker identity, the OS build, the Xcode build, a framework present on disk, loaded-image membership, a membership delta before and after any stage, a readable build string inside a loaded image, a hard-coded framework path, and the device registry ID are each excluded by name. Each was measured, and each fails to associate a component with one native preparation.

5. **Offline compiler and linker provenance stays artifact-owned and is untouched.** The artifact continues to name the exact compiler, linker, SDK, target, flags, and language that produced its bytes. This decision adds no field to artifact identity, because the missing fact is resolved by the process that runs the kernel and cannot be fixed at expansion time.

6. **The first authoritative native Metal profile is blocked on a newly named observable or authority, not on an implementation task.** Nothing here directs a search for private symbols, and no unsafe production observer is justified by it: the spike already demonstrated that the strongest available foreign observation returns membership rather than attribution, so an ADR 0079 site would buy a wider blast radius and the same `Unknown`.

7. **Reconsideration triggers, any one of which reopens this record.** Apple publishes a stable API naming the component responsible for a given pipeline preparation; a separately accepted observer attributes that component on the native route; or Tiler adopts a host-attestation mechanism that binds the exact execution environment rather than reporting its public fields. A trigger produces a superseding decision; it does not license amending this one in place.

## Consequences

- `validate-macos-metal-profile-host-applicability` produces a typed refusal naming the unsatisfied translation predicate and cannot produce a positive eligibility receipt on current APIs. Its policy and observer halves remain worth building, because the refusal must be checked, explainable, and reached before `RoutingCommit`.
- `construct-and-bind-the-first-authoritative-metal-compile-profile` is blocked in one of its parts and not as a whole. Its quantitative, synchronization, independently measured F32/BF16 dispatchability, and independently measured F32/BF16 numerical rows are unaffected for their exact measurement boundaries; what they lack is the applicability authority that would let a host *offer* the profile. **Corrected 2026-08-03:** this bullet first read "remains blocked", then named the superseded F32-only endpoint after that ticket closed under a resolution Tom recorded. `tiler_build::BoundMetalCompileDeclaration` now binds the profile keyed `tiler.metal.macos-apple9.msl4-0.f32-bf16.v1`, and `accept_or_publish_metal_plan` verifies a compilation against it before emission; what this decision blocks — and still blocks — is the runtime offer, so the production path observes the host and returns this record's typed refusal instead. The profile ticket and authority ledger state the same split.
- The bounded F32 facts of the unified MSL 4 Apple9 replay stay valid evidence for their exact inputs and are not promoted into an applicability authority. Agreement between two compilation paths on one row is not the fact this decision needs.
- ADR 0043 gains no new outcome class, phase, or field. What it gains is a worked case: a declared required device translation whose authority is unavailable is an `Unknown` capability fact, and the validity scope of the measurement that observed it is not a substitute for that authority.
- A future host-attestation route, if adopted, is a security and platform mechanism with its own threat model. This decision names it as an admissible authority and does not specify, endorse, or assume any particular mechanism.

## Alternatives considered

**Authorize the exact execution-environment row as the validity scope for pipeline translation (public-row equivalence).** This was the option the deciding ticket recommended before the decision, and it is rejected. Tom's recorded reason is that "an opaque translator can change while the observed OS/build/device row remains identical", so the predicate would certify an equivalence its own evidence cannot check. The independent structural objection is the one in item 3 above: ADR 0043 types validity scope, authority, and provenance as three fields precisely so that the environment which delivered an observation is not mistaken for the thing that made it true. The option was cheaper exactly where it was wrong — it removes the requirement to name the component, which is the part that would have made the receipt mean anything.

**Treat the unknown predicate as proven, or hide it behind a large cost.** Eliminated by ADR 0043's separation of hard feasibility from estimated cost and by its rule that unknown is neither true nor false. Treating it as true is unsound, and treating it as a cost would let the plan run while reporting a preference.

**Name the component from loaded-image evidence.** Eliminated by the spike's own negative control, which put a plausibly named image carrying a readable `metalfe-*` build string into the process and still classified attribution as unavailable. Membership, deltas, and embedded strings are three evidence classes, and none of them associates a component with one preparation.

**Widen the observer with unsafe foreign access until something attributes.** Eliminated because the observation that is missing is an association, not a byte. No amount of additional reading of images the process already loaded produces an API-established link between a component and a specific pipeline preparation, and admitting an unsafe site under ADR 0079 for a probe that returns the same `Unknown` fails that ADR's first condition.

## Implementation boundary

Added 2026-08-01 by [`re-audit-adr-implementation-status-after-the-runtime-and-metal-landings`](../../tickets/re-audit-adr-implementation-status-after-the-runtime-and-metal-landings.md), which moved `implementation_status` from `not-started` to `partial`. This section states which clauses that value rests on and which it does not, read at `2aa0824`. It is a status record and adds no decision.

**Realized — the positive receipt is unreachable by construction, not by a test.** `NativeTranslationAuthority` at `crates/tiler-metal/src/applicability.rs:661` holds one private field of the empty enum `NoAdmissibleAuthority` at `:679`, so the type is uninhabited: not merely unconstructed, but impossible to construct anywhere including inside the crate. `MetalHostEligibility` at `:695` holds one, which makes a positive receipt unreachable; `native_translation_authority()` at `:1061` returns `None` because the comment at `:1056` states it can return nothing else. The proof is compile-enforced rather than asserted: `structural_unreachability::every_outcome_is_a_refusal` at `:1082` is an exhaustive `match` over `Result<MetalHostEligibility, _>` with no `Ok` arm, so inhabiting the authority stops that function compiling. Item 1's "neither exists on current APIs, so the predicate is `Unknown` and the receipt is refused" is therefore the code's shape rather than its current behaviour.

**Realized — the refusal is typed, names the unsatisfied predicate, and is told apart from a disproof.** `MetalHostPredicate::NativeTranslationAuthority` at `:397` is the named predicate; `MetalHostApplicabilityRefusal::UnknownNativeTranslationAuthority` at `:800` carries the versioned policy id and no observed value, because there is none; `rule()` at `:830` returns `metal.host-applicability.unknown-translation-authority` for it and `metal.host-applicability.outside-measured-row` for every environment mismatch, which is item 2's requirement that explain output distinguish `Unknown` disposal from a disproved hard predicate. No new feasibility outcome, infinite cost, or penalty was introduced.

**Realized — the measured row is evaluated as a necessary validity scope and refused as sufficient authority.** `evaluate_metal_host_applicability` at `:981` compares OS family, version, build, architecture, device name, and GPU family against `MetalHostApplicabilityPolicy::FIRST_MACOS_APPLE9` at `:570` first, then reaches the authority last, so a host matching the retained row in every public field still receives the ADR 0086 refusal — the doc-test at `:907` asserts exactly that, and asserts a different OS build is reported against `MetalHostPredicate::OsBuild` instead. The policy has no public constructor, so no caller can mint a row nobody measured.

**Realized — no substitute can be relabelled as the authority.** Item 4's excluded candidates are unnameable at this boundary rather than merely unused: the function's two parameters are a closed policy and a `MetalHostObservation` whose fields are strings and this module's own enums, and `crate::applicability_tests` pins both argument positions against a `TargetProfileRef` and against artifact bytes. The device registry ID is not an input, and `tiler-metal`'s dependency set makes a `Compilation` or an offline compiler identity unnameable in the crate.

**Realized — offline provenance is untouched.** Item 5 adds no artifact field, and none was added: this module reads no artifact and the compile-profile declaration continues to own compiler, linker, SDK, target, flags, and language.

**Unrealized — the refusal is not yet reached on a production routing path inside `crates/`.** The Consequences require the check to be "reached before `RoutingCommit`", and the only callers of `evaluate_metal_host_applicability` outside this crate's own tests are `prototypes/serial-sum-run/src/proof.rs:955` and `prototypes/candle-metal-adapter/src/proof.rs:1016`. The policy half is production code; the observer half that binds it into a `tiler-runtime` route is a prototype, and its bounded proof is one host.

**Unrealized — nothing consumes a receipt, because none can exist.** `MetalHostEligibility::policy` and `observation` are declared for the consumer that would read them, and item 6 is explicit that what remains is a newly named observable or authority rather than an implementation task. A superseding decision under one of item 7's triggers is what would make this clause reachable.

## Traceability

This record refines [ADR 0043](0043-use-typed-phased-target-feasibility.md)'s runtime-translation-policy clause and the authority and provenance fields of its typed capability facts; it supersedes nothing, because ADR 0043's disposal of `Unknown` is what it applies. The [Metal AOT backend](../backends/metal.md) contract owns the normative sentence for the Apple case. The [Apple GPU numerical behaviour](../research/apple-targets/numerical-behaviour.md) and [Apple Metal artifact compatibility](../research/apple-targets/artifact-compatibility.md) records own the measurements and the primary-source synthesis, and the [AOT runtime-compiler observer](../../spikes/apple-targets/aot-runtime-compiler-observer/README.md) is the reproducible harness behind the negative result.
