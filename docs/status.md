# Project status

Tiler is an alpha-stage, bounded architectural prototype. It has an executable compiler-to-Metal value proof and reviewed experimental public boundaries, not a production tensor compiler, a general Metal runtime, or a stabilized API.

## Delivered bounded vertical

- **Fact — semantic and reference:** `tiler-ir` exposes a typed, multi-result semantic graph with checked construction, governed operation and type authority, canonical identity, shaped-value evidence, and a downstream reference-evaluation path. The implemented standard profile remains deliberately narrow; the [operation-family matrix](roadmap.md#operation-family-support-matrix) and [dtype maturity ledger](dtype-support.md) own its exact breadth.
- **Fact — compiler:** the ordinary `tiler-compiler` path reaches checked normalization, capability resolution, semantic-to-index refinement, fusion legality and covers, target feasibility, scheduling, physical-plan selection, structured KIR, verified kernel-program construction, and artifact construction for the bounded governed profile. The `tiler_compiler::session` facade remains a reviewed experimental draft as a whole: its public `CompileRequest`, `InstalledCapabilities`, `compile`, `compile_governed`, compilation/result readers, and typed failure surface may still be reshaped during alpha. It already lets an out-of-crate caller install index-access lowering capabilities and state an ordered numerical-contract preference. Separately, Tom accepted the experimental `tiler_compiler::target::{TargetProfileBuilder, TargetProfile, TargetRequest}` boundary on 2026-07-30; it admits structurally attributed external guarantees and measurements, sparse per-axis quantitative facts whose omissions remain unknown, dimension-specific scalar numerical declarations, and exact full-`ResolvedValueType` dispatch facts. It does not let a caller assert compiler-governed proof, exact emulation, or ABI layout. These are usable alpha boundaries, not stable or workload-general APIs; shape-environment choice and planning budgets remain internal.
- **Fact — one authoritative macOS Metal compile profile:** `tiler_build::BoundMetalCompileDeclaration` binds the checked compiler profile, the exact `MetalTargetFacts`, the selected emission and numerical realizations, the total `MetalTarget` projection, and the structured sources, from exactly the rows of the [compile-profile authority ledger](research/target-profiles/first-macos-metal-compile-profile-authority-ledger.md) and no others. Its key is `tiler.metal.macos-apple9.msl4-0.f32-bf16.v1` at MSL 4.0 for macOS 26.0. Quantitative and synchronization rows are external normative guarantees; F32 and BF16 dispatchability plus their measured numerical rows carry one source pairing the offline compiler, linker, Xcode, and SDK builds with the exact execution environment. Compiler buffer capacity and the independent F32/BF16 subnormal projections are the three validated overlaps. F32 and BF16 resolve `Dispatchable`; both carry complete sign-preserving-flush input/result tables, while the remaining contract dimensions are still F32-only. `accept_or_publish_metal_plan` refuses a plan compiled under any other profile before emission. Device address width, a workgroup fact, F16, F64, and every iOS family remain absent and therefore unknown; no public BF16 numerical contract or BF16 backend vertical is implied.
- **Fact — the host offer is refused, by decision:** a `tiler-metal` host adapter evaluates applicability from a host observation alone and, per [ADR 0086](decisions/0086-require-attributable-or-attested-native-translation.md), refuses with a typed unknown-native-translation-authority even on a host matching the measured row in every public field. Nothing derives a profile offer from a compilation or from the artifact under validation. The retained prototype route is an explicitly labelled diagnostic carrying producer-declared equality rather than host-earned eligibility, which is what keeps the runtime machinery exercised on hardware without making the gated claim. An eligible-host offer waits on one of that ADR's reconsideration triggers, not on an implementation task.
- **Fact — identity and schema:** the current source-derived artifact ledger is resolved value type v3, scheduled region v5, structured kernel v6, verified kernel program v9, independently serialized artifact stage key v3, artifact program v14, and neutral manifest schema 12.0. The scheduled-region and kernel-program rows had gone stale against the source constants and are corrected here: the region moved at the two-dimensional cooperative staging step, and the program moved through canonical stage coverage, published output order, and — most recently — binding each covered occurrence to the reached-only refinement evidence proving it, which stepped the artifact stage key with it while leaving the artifact domain and manifest schema where they were. The numerical-contract key domain is `tiler.contract.f32.v2`: a contract key is the canonical injective encoding of the caller's resolved dimension vector rather than one of four hand-written preset names, so every identity that folds a contract key moved while no encoding above it stepped. The target feasibility profile's checked descriptor encoding is v10, its complete declaration is v11, and the governed feasibility vocabulary is `tiler.feasibility.phased-capability-and-numerical-honourability.v5` revision 1. The envelope format and canonical encoding remain 1.0; the program, ABI-expression, and guard-and-routing component schemas remain 1.0, while the target-requirement component schema is 3.0. These are separate subjects rather than one global version; [Artifact ABI](artifact-abi.md) owns the complete ledger and evolution rationale.
- **Fact — one typed synchronization point and one atomic target authority:** a cooperative workgroup tile declares synchronization points at its own phase boundaries, and the schedule verifier proves each one's operation kind, placement, participant set, arrival and publication scopes, fenced domains, ordering, and convergence *evidence class* before any requirement is derived — refusing a caller's bare assertion of convergence by name, and refusing a point that discharges no edge, two points over one edge, or any edge no point discharges. The structured-kernel verifier admits a barrier only when it names a declared point, sits where its tile's round structure makes it convergent — outside every predicate, and no deeper than the one loop level a multi-round tile authorizes — projects onto that point's subject through one total mapping, and lexically separates each staged write from the staged read consuming it; the canonical lowering emits that body, so one bounded cooperative reduction reaches a verified kernel and an artifact. The loop rule is *at most* one enclosing loop rather than exactly one, because a loop-carried body peels round zero and therefore realizes its phase boundary once at the top level and `rounds - 1` times inside the round loop; what refuses a stray top-level barrier is the realization count `verify_synchronization` checks against the placement — `rounds` for a phase boundary and `rounds - 1` for a round boundary — rather than the nesting rule. Feasibility composes one atomic realization subject against a target's declaration by whole-value equality: an unmatched subject is `Unknown`, a declared negative is a typed rejection, and neighbouring facts that each realize one dimension of the subject compose into no permission. A schedule with no synchronization derives no requirement, consults no fact, and emits no explain row. **No Metal support was promoted:** the macOS profile declares no realization, so a cooperative region is `Unknown` against it, and `realize-parallel-reduction-strategies-on-metal` owns backend qualification.
- **Fact — loop-carried cooperative staging is representable and lowered:** a `CooperativeTile` states how many times its phase sequence executes, so a tile may fill one fixed allocation, hand it off behind a point, and rewrite the same slots on a later round. The one-writer-per-slot rule is unchanged and now reads per round; the rewrite creates a second derived evidence class, an `AntiDependencyEdge` requiring round `r + 1`'s write to follow round `r`'s read, which is derived from the declared structure exactly as a `VisibilityEdge` is and needs exactly one discharging point — a new `SynchronizationPlacement::RoundBoundary`, or a phase boundary that already falls between the two. Convergence for a repeating tile rests on its own evidence class, because the round count is a declared literal every participant runs identically. **A body realizes one, since 2026-08-01.** The canonical lowering emits a multi-round tile as a peeled round zero followed by a `1..rounds` loop carrying the accumulator, because a fold seeds at its first contributor and the registered family declares no seed. The KIR vocabulary question this was expected to force — whether a predicated region may yield values — turned out to need no change: a predicated region still produces no values, but staged accesses are deliberately not the boundary effects predicate dominance governs, so the staged fold sits outside every predicate and the accumulator crosses the back edge unguarded. [`lower-a-loop-carried-cooperative-body`](../tickets/lower-a-loop-carried-cooperative-body.md) landed it. The shape does pay for that: every participant folds the staged set and only one commits, and removing the redundancy would need a predicated region that yields values, which this vocabulary still does not have. **Fact — 2026-08-01: that body is not what blocks the `tiled` contraction, and never was what blocks the log-depth tree.** Both were blocked by the *staged access relation*: a `StagedSpan` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/cooperative.rs`) addressed `stride * l + offset` over the linear participant coordinate, and a 16×16 operand tile's two staged reads need `16 * (l / 16)` and `16 * (l % 16)` — each refuted by two points, and refuted over every participant relabelling at once because an affine map on a 256-element domain is constant or injective while both profiles take sixteen values with multiplicity sixteen. The tree needs the same missing dependence stated over the round ordinal instead, plus a per-access active-participant subset. [`admit-a-two-dimensional-cooperative-staging-relation`](../tickets/admit-a-two-dimensional-cooperative-staging-relation.md) owned the relation and the `tiler.schedule` domain step every candidate widening of it forced; [`admit-a-cooperative-tile-over-shared-operands`](../tickets/admit-a-cooperative-tile-over-shared-operands.md) owns the second thing `tiled` needs, a tile whose participants each commit their own output — which the current tile's `commit.count != 1` refusal is not a narrowing of but the inverse of. **Fact — 2026-08-05: the relation landed, and the three sentences above are corrected in tense rather than deleted, because the refutation is the derivation the widening was accepted on.** [ADR 0097](decisions/0097-admit-a-two-dimensional-cooperative-staging-relation.md) is accepted and `implementation_status: implemented`. A `CooperativeTile`'s participants now occupy a `ParticipantSpace` of per-dimension extents, slowest-varying first, whose product the schedule verifier compares against the launched workgroup width, and a `StagedSpan` carries one stride per participant dimension, addressing `count` contiguous slots at `offset + sum_d strides[d] * l_d` — so the transposed write `16 * (l % 16) + (l / 16)` is statable, `the_measured_tile_kernels_four_staged_accesses_are_all_statable` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/schedule/builder.rs`) enumerates the measured kernel's four accesses, and a span whose rank disagrees with the tile's is refused by name under `CooperativeTileRule::SpanRank`. [`implement-the-two-dimensional-staging-relation-and-step-the-schedule-domain-to-v5`](../tickets/implement-the-two-dimensional-staging-relation-and-step-the-schedule-domain-to-v5.md) landed it at `a395852a` on 2026-08-02 and executed the `tiler.schedule.v4` to `v5` step whole, which is the region row this page already records above; both it and the relation's producing ticket are `done`, so neither owns the relation or the step any longer. **What did not land is a body, and the two are different maturity claims.** `cooperative_plan` (`crates/tiler-ir/src/kernel/lower.rs`) destructures exactly one stride from each span and refuses anything wider with `KernelDiagnostic::CooperativeLoweringShape`, because the emitted body reads a linear local index and has no form for a per-dimension position — so a two-dimensional tile verifies as a schedule and reaches no kernel. `tiled` therefore waits on the second tile relation above and on [`realize-the-tiled-contraction-schedule-and-its-metal-emission`](../tickets/realize-the-tiled-contraction-schedule-and-its-metal-emission.md), both `deferred`; the log-depth tree still waits on the round-dependent span and the per-access active-participant subset, which ADR 0097 refuses by decision and [`admit-a-round-dependent-cooperative-staging-span`](../tickets/admit-a-round-dependent-cooperative-staging-span.md) holds at `deferred`.
- **Fact — Metal artifact-to-proof path:** `tiler-metal` deterministically lowers the bounded structured KIR to MSL, `tiler-metal-aot` invokes the selected Apple offline toolchain and records provenance, and `tiler-build` assembles the carried Metal payload into the neutral artifact, including compiler-minted target requirements bound to exact prepared entries. The device-free `tiler-runtime` keeps its refusal-only preflight for routes with unanswered deferred predicates and also exposes a staged `RoutePreparation` whose exact-entry answers must satisfy the carried directional requirements before the same one-way commit. The retained `serial-sum-run` prototype binds resources, dispatches, waits for exact command-buffer success, and validates readback; the measured corpus below predates and does not itself establish hardware execution of the exact-entry deferred route.
- **Measurement — offline AOT:** the retained [Metal AOT proof](../tickets/prototype-metal-aot-slice.md) produced the selected fused and materialized programs on one Apple M4 Max with macOS 27.0 build 26A5388g, Xcode 26.6 build 17F113, Metal/AIR-LLD 32023.883, and macOS SDK 26.5 build 25F70; two independent links produced byte-identical 3,843-byte metallibs. This is deterministic construction evidence for that measured row, not runtime compatibility evidence.
- **Measurement — device execution:** the retained [runtime proof](../tickets/prototype-metal-runtime-proof.md) bit-compared 30 cases on one Apple M4 Max under the named flush-to-zero `f32` contract (`NumericalContract::FLUSH_SUBNORMALS_TO_ZERO_F32`), covering three reduction classes, both selected-fused and materialized roles, and five cases per class/role. The selected route used one dispatch and no shared allocation; the materialized route used two dispatches and one shared allocation. This establishes that exact host, toolchain, program shape, numerical realization, and corpus only; it is neither a portable Apple-family guarantee nor a production runtime qualification.
- **Fact — artifact and cache infrastructure:** `tiler-artifact` exposes the reviewed public neutral codec/capability boundary, including carried compilation-subject and object bytes, and `tiler-cache` implements the immutable self-validating cross-process expansion cache. The complete inline orchestration that composes those pieces remains open.

## Authorized prototype

Tom authorized the bounded serial `f32` `Sum` Metal value proof in [ADR 0055](decisions/0055-use-a-serial-sum-for-the-first-metal-value-proof.md). The proof passes through the documented semantic, reference, optimizer, schedule, structured-kernel, artifact, cacheable-build, and guarded-runtime boundaries; a handwritten standalone kernel would not satisfy it. ADR 0067 selects the exact `nightly-2026-07-19` toolchain and dependent-array `StaticShape<RANK, EXTENTS>` family, with a retained [shape conformance harness](../spikes/shapes/nightly-dependent-static-shapes/README.md).

The delivered path recognizes two one-input/one-output bounded F32 shapes: a four-operation pointwise add or multiply over one input plus constants, and a four- or five-operation scale-bias-strict-serial-sum program with a deterministic fused candidate and a deliberately materialized comparison candidate. Its generic authorities are real and reusable, but their current admitted workload is not representative of broad tensor compilation. Quantized U4 construction has later-layer structural fixtures but still fails closed before executable support; the dtype ledger records that non-monotone evidence without promoting it to a vertical guarantee.

## Evidence boundary

- Executable spikes and prototypes validate only their recorded representations, protocols, numerical cases, hosts, and toolchains.
- Apple compatibility remains bounded to measured toolchains and hosts; old-OS and device-family runtime coverage is not universal.
- Sound region-accuracy analysis exists for a narrow trusted-analyzer profile; independent certificate checking remains deferred.
- Multi-device, external-storage, and richer transfer work is deliberately deferred, not silently ready.
